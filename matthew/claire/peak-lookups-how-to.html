Peak desk look-ups

For Claire. You fill in the gaps on the Peak Pickleball desk: a company's website, main phone and general email, and where each one came from. The sellers do the calling and emailing. Start with your list "First 20 Peak look-ups".

1. Signing in

  • The desk is at traverseplatforms.com/peak-desk-28d38d/. Matthew gives you your own six-digit code. Don't share it.
  • Your account shows the companies and the expo organisations, and a card for each. Switch between Sponsors and Expo leads at the top.
  • Open a card with "Open the card" on your list, or search by name under Companies.

2. Where you may look

  1. The company's own website: its Contact, About or Locations page, and the bottom of every page.
  2. Its Google Business listing: search the company and town in Google; the panel shows its website and phone.
  3. The chamber of commerce member directory (in Colorado Springs, the Colorado Springs Chamber & EDC).

Nowhere else. General and role contacts are what we want: info@, events@, sponsorships@, marketing@, the front desk or main line. A person counts only when the company's own website names them for sponsorships, events or marketing, with the work email or phone it shows.

Never
  • No people-search sites, ever: Whitepages, Spokeo, BeenVerified, TruePeopleSearch, FastPeopleSearch and the like. That is Matthew's standing rule.
  • No personal cell phones, home addresses or personal email accounts.
  • Never guess an email address. Copy it exactly as the source shows it, or leave it out. "firstname@company.com" because it looks likely is a guess.
  • You never email, text or call anyone. Phones and emails show on your cards as plain text for that reason.

3. Filling in a card

  1. Website (sponsors): type it in the "Their website" box and tap Save and look up. The desk reads the site for free and lists the phones, emails and pages it found, with where on the site. Wrong website? Tap Change website. Expo cards have no website box: put the website in your note.
  2. Look them up again any time with the button under "Looked up". There is no paid look-up on your account, and none is needed.
  3. Phone or email: tap + Add a contact. Leave Name empty for a general contact. Role: "General inbox", "Main line" or "Events office". Type the email or phone exactly as the source shows it. Save contact.
  4. If it says "Already on the desk?", the detail is there already: tap "Add the new details to ..." only if it is the same contact, otherwise leave it and say so in your note.
  5. Mistake? Tap Edit (the pencil) on the contact and correct it.
  6. Mark looked up: in that box, write what you found and where, then tap Mark looked up. Do this on every card you open, even when you found nothing.

Example note: Website from their Google listing. Main phone (719) ... from the Contact page of their site. General email info@... from the site footer. No sponsorship contact on the site.

4. When sources disagree, or you're unsure

  • Two sources with different phones or emails: add neither. Write both in the note, with where each came from.
  • A national company (Kaiser, Molson Coors): look for the Colorado Springs or Colorado office first, and say in the note which office it is.
  • Can't find it in the three places: write that in the note and move on.

5. What stays with the sellers

Stages, owners, follow-ups, tours, proposals, prices, emails, the Inbox and removing anything are the sellers' and Matthew's. Your account can't do them. If you see something they should know, put it in a note, or tell Matthew.
