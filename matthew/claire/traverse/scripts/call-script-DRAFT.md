> We make a field-sales app built here in Colorado for [roofing / lawn and landscaping] companies: door maps, CRM, phones and quotes in one place. I'm calling to see if a 15-minute demo of your area would be worth it.

If they emailed back or got Matthew's email: "Matthew Sharpe emailed you about it earlier this month. I'm following up."

## Two or three questions

1. "How do your crews pick which doors to knock right now?"
2. "What do you use to keep track of leads and quotes?"
3. "Who else would want to see the demo?"

Listen, write the answers in the note on the desk, and don't argue with any of them.

## Booking the demo

> The demo is 15 minutes, and it shows your own area. You can pick a time at traverseplatforms.com/demo, or tell me a day and time that suits you and Matthew will confirm it.

Then on the desk: log the call as **Talked**, and use **Flag for Matthew now** with the day and time they asked for and the best number to reach them.

## If they ask

- **Price or discounts:** "Matthew goes through pricing in the demo." Never quote a price or offer a discount.
- **Where the data comes from:** "Matthew covers that in the demo."
- **Send me something:** "I'll email you the link." (Use the approved follow-up email only.)
- **Not interested:** "No problem. Thanks for your time." Stop there. On the desk, tap **Not interested**.
- **Take me off your list:** "Will do." On the desk, tap **Not interested** and **They said no more emails**.
