Reaching companies for Traverse

For Claire. You reach roofing and lawn companies on the Traverse prospect list by Nextdoor message, Facebook message, phone call, and email from your own Traverse address. You are always Claire with Traverse, never Matthew. Your four lists are in "First Traverse outreach lists".

1. Signing in

  • The prospect desk is at traverseplatforms.com/prospects-47bfe6/. Matthew gives you your own six-digit code. Don't share it.
  • Type a company's name in the search box and open it. Its phone, website, and Nextdoor and Facebook links are under How to reach them.

2. Only approved scripts

  • Use the scripts Matthew has approved, word for word. Fill in the company name only.
  • The scripts in your folder are marked DRAFT until Matthew approves them. Don't use a draft.
  • Anything new, or a question you can't answer from the script, goes to Matthew first.

3. Log every touch on the desk

  1. On the company, under Log a call or message, pick how: Call, Nextdoor message, Facebook message, or Email sent by hand.
  2. Pick how it went (No answer, Left voicemail, Talked, Wrong number, Sent, They replied), add a short note, and tap Log it.
  3. One log for every call or message, even when nobody answers.

4. Warm replies go to Matthew at once

  • Anyone who wants a demo, a price, the free map, or asks a real question: under Warm reply? write what they said and the best way and time to reach them, then tap Flag for Matthew now. He gets an alert straight away.
  • Tell them "Matthew will be in touch today," and leave the rest to him.

5. Stop at a no

  • "Not interested": thank them, stop, and tap Not interested. Never try that company again.
  • "Don't email us": tap They said no more emails as well.

6. Who never gets contacted

Your lists already leave these out, but check the card before you reach out, and skip the company if it says:

  • Asked not to be emailed, or its email doesn't work.
  • Not interested, or knocked off the list.
  • Its Nextdoor page has an error or no owner.
  • Messaged, called or emailed in the last 14 days (the History on the card shows the dates).
  • Not a roofing or lawn business after all: skip it and say so in a note.

One way in per company at a time: if you messaged them on Nextdoor this week, don't also call and email them.

7. Daily limits

  • Nextdoor and Facebook: about 15 to 20 a day each, so the accounts don't get flagged.
  • Calls: weekdays in business hours. Emails: one at a time, never to several people at once.
Never
  • Never promise a price, a discount or a free trial. Point to the demo: "Matthew goes through pricing in the demo."
  • Never say you are Matthew, or write as him.
  • Never contact anyone on the rules in section 6, or anyone who said no.
  • Never use people-search sites or personal cell numbers: only what's on the desk card.
