**Prepared:** 2026-09-13
**Scope:** Colorado Secretary of State entity records, open-web company and trademark search, domain DNS check.
**Status:** Name selected. Nothing registered, nothing purchased, nothing printed.

> **This is a preliminary screen, not a legal clearance.** The USPTO trademark database could not be
> searched — the portal is a JavaScript application, the same blocker hit by the Sixfold screen

> A real clearance means a USPTO search plus common-law and state review, and it is an attorney's job.
> What follows is what public records establish, and it is enough to say whether that call is worth making.

## 1. Why this name

The offering's differentiator is not the delivery method — it is that **every factual claim traces to a
primary source, and every deliverable carries a written fact-check log**
([../services-pricing.md](../services-pricing.md) §6). The name had to carry that without claiming a
proprietary process, which §6 also bars.

A **stringline** is the taut string a mason, framer or concrete crew pulls between two stakes to set a
dead-true line. Every measurement on the job references it. It is the fixed thing you check against —
which is the offering, stated in a word the buyer already owns. The intended client sectors are roofing,
restoration and home services, so the metaphor is native to them rather than borrowed from publishing.

Rejected direction: growth words — Summit, Apex, Elevate, Velocity, Pinnacle. Crowded, and they promise
an outcome that cannot be guaranteed. Guarantees of ranking, lead volume or revenue are barred in
writing at any tier (§7).

## 3. The conflicts to be aware of

**"StringLine Construction" exists twice, out of state.** One in Wakefield, Kansas (BBB-listed;
remodelling, flooring, decks, garages). One targeting the Gulf Coast region, on a Wix site. Neither is
in Colorado and neither sells marketing.

**"Stringline" is also a generic product term in construction** — Stringliner PRO mason's line reels,
an Aldon product line, and multiple patents on string-line tools.

**Why this probably does not block the name:** trademark rights attach to goods-and-services classes and
to real market overlap. Advertising and marketing services sit in class 35; construction services sit in
class 37. Two businesses routinely hold the same name in unrelated classes — the same reasoning that
lets Sixfold coexist with Sixfold AI. The generic use in construction also means nobody holds a strong
mark on the bare word *in construction*, while in class 35 the word is arbitrary and therefore more
protectable, not less.

**Why it still deserves the attorney call:**
- The intended clients **are contractors**, so a marketing firm called Stringline may be read as a
  construction firm. That is a positioning question as much as a legal one. It can be an asset — it
  signals the trade is understood — but it has to be handled in the tagline, not left to chance.
- No USPTO search was possible from here. That is the one gap that matters.

**Not a conflict:** the string-line tool patents. Patents do not restrict a service name.

phone call to a roofer.

### The Sharpeline finding

He chose **Sharpeline** — his surname plus the line. Colorado is pristine: zero records for either
spelling. The problem is not the register, it is the spoken word.

**"Sharpeline" is a homophone of a crowded US field:**

| Company | What it does |
|---|---|
| Sharpline Converting Inc — Wichita KS, since 1976 | **Vinyl graphics**, dimensional emblems, automotive restyling. Holds `sharpline.com`. Design-adjacent. |
| SharpLine Solutions — Azusa CA | Traffic marking materials |
| Sharp-Line Industries — Spokane WA | Traffic signs and signals |
| Sharpline Inc | Multi-unit renovation and maintenance |
| SharpLine Finishing LLC | Colorado — construction finishing |
| SHARPELINE LTD | UK, hair care. Holds `sharpeline.co.uk`. |

`sharpeline.com` itself already resolves; its owner could not be identified — the fetch failed on a TLS
certificate error, which usually indicates a parked or misconfigured host.

**Why this matters here specifically.** This is not a trademark opinion — class 35 against vinyl
graphics and traffic paint is defensible on the same class reasoning as §3. It is a **findability**
problem, and acquisition for this offering is referral and local search. A name heard on a jobsite gets
typed as `sharpline.com`, which is a Wichita graphics manufacturer; typed with the "e" it is whatever
sits on `sharpeline.com`. **Both obvious spellings lead somewhere that is not him**, leaving
`sharpeline.co` or `sharpelineco.com` as the actual address. For a studio selling websites, being hard
to find by its own name is an unhelpful first impression.

**Left open at his request, 2026-09-14.** Three routes: `Sharpe Layout` (unscreened, keeps his name and
the layout idea, no homophone field); Stringline as the brand with proposals signed in his own name; or
Sharpeline anyway with the tax accepted deliberately.

## 6c. USPTO — partial screen, 2026-09-16

**Name settled as Stringline the same day**, so only that name was screened.

**What could and could not be reached.** `tmsearch.uspto.gov` sits behind an AWS WAF browser challenge
(`challenge.js` in the page shell; the API path returns 405 from S3) — no scripted search gets through.
**TSDR** (`tsdr.uspto.gov/statusview/sn<serial>`) answers plain GETs and is the USPTO's own record, but
only per serial number. Justia and uspto.report return 403 to direct fetches, but their record pages are
indexed, so a search engine restricted to those domains works as a coarse index of USPTO records.

**Found.**

| Mark | Serial / Reg. | Class | Status | Bearing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| STRINGLINE ELECTRONICS | 73602158 / 1425964 | 037 — repair of electronic devices used in construction | **DEAD** — registered 20 Jan 1987, cancelled 26 Jul 1993, Section 8 not filed. Owner Stringline Electronics Inc., Atlanta GA. Confirmed on TSDR | None — wrong class, dead 33 years |

No STRINGLINE or STRING LINE mark in class 035 surfaced. The other "stringline" hits are construction
goods (Power Curbers stringline guidance components 2020; Anvil American clamps 1996; a "Rapid Reel"
winding tool 2024) — goods classes, unrelated. Nearest live class-035 *-LINE* marks: BRANDLINE (reg.
5031357), THRULINE MARKETING (reg. 5597320), THROUGHLINE STRATEGY & BRAND (ser. 97343061) — none
confusingly similar.

**Standing.** Preliminary, same as the Colorado screen. It would have caught a direct conflict and found
none. It is **not** a full USPTO search: the mirrors do not guarantee completeness, and a proper search also
covers phonetic equivalents and design-plus-word marks. **Before filing:** ten minutes on
tmsearch.uspto.gov in a browser — "stringline" and "string line", live only, look at 035 — or an attorney.
