## Websites, booking and marketing tools for local businesses
Matthew Sharpe

### Get found
- **Website.** A fast, mobile site with a booking button, and visitor stats that need no cookie banner. From $2,200.
- **Google Business Profile.** Set up and kept current: hours, photos, posts, answers to questions.
- **Monthly report.** One page: visitors, where they came from, bookings, and email results.

### Get booked
- **Online booking.** Clients pick a time from a link on your site, in an email or in a text. They get a confirmation email and a calendar invite, and you see it on your calendar.
- **Website chat.** Answers the common questions (hours, prices, insurance, how to book) and hands anything else to you.

### Stay in touch
- **Email follow-ups.** Sent from your own address, with opens, clicks and replies counted. Anyone who asks to stop is taken off.
- **Review requests.** After a visit, every customer gets the same short "how did we do?" message with a link to your Google reviews.
- **Social posts.** Drafted for you, posted only after you approve them.

### Keep track
- **Leads and customers in one place.** Who you've contacted, who's due a follow-up, notes and where each deal stands.

### Have a door-to-door or field sales team?
Ask about Traverse, a field-sales app for roofing, solar, pest, security and lawn companies.
