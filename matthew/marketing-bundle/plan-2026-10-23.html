Swaps

  • Rosie instead of Retell (turnkey phone answering, nothing to build): ≈ $86.82/mo
  • BrightLocal Manage ($40/mo yearly) instead of Moz + Whitespark: ≈ $62/mo
  • Telnyx instead of Twilio for texts: ~$1.50 less a month
  • Skip the phone receptionist (if the client answers calls): ≈ $38/mo

One-time costs per client

  • Texting registration (10DLC): brand $4.50 + campaign vetting $15 = $19.50, plus $41.50 if a standard brand needs vetting. Each client registers as its own business.
  • Website and hosting are not included above. Clients like Peak usually have a site already.
  • No setup fee from any of the services listed.

What it's worth

At Seopoint's lowest plan ($399), the outside services take about $52 of each $399. The rest pays for your time: setup, posts, reports and support. Seopoint's own cost isn't public: Vendasta charges agencies $99–$999 a month, but its per-client wholesale prices are not published, so their markup can't be worked out exactly.

What can stretch the calendar

  • Google Business Profile API: free, but needs an application. The client's profile must be verified and active for 60+ days and list a website.
  • Texting registration (10DLC): per client; approval can take days to weeks.
  • Meta: each client connects their Facebook and Instagram to our app, the same way we did for Sixfold 365 today.

Needed from you

  • Test each piece as it lands.
  • Approve the wording clients' customers will see (review request text, chat answers).
  • Sign up for the outside services in your name (Moz Local, Whitespark, Twilio, Amazon SES, Retell or Rosie) when we reach them.
  • The client's access: their Facebook, Google profile and website.
