Horizon Outreach Partners and Events: online presence audit

28 October 2026
Prepared by Matthew Sharpe

Horizon has a clean, secure website, one consistent address and phone, and posts on four accounts every weekday. But the site recruits without ever saying what the job is, hides its only apply form two clicks deep, and is slow on phones; the four accounts reach 41 followers between them. What Horizon does right: HTTPS everywhere with automatic certificate renewal · the same address, phone and email on every page and on Facebook · a short apply form (4 fields, right phone keyboards, every field labelled) · text-to-apply, which suits field recruiting · no ad or analytics trackers · posting on all four accounts every weekday · real team photos on social, no income or "be your own boss" claims in anything we could see.

Website: what is wrong, and the fix

  • No way to apply from the home page. The menu has 7 items, 2 of them legal pages, and no Apply. The form is reachable only from Our Team → "Join The Team!". Apply button in the header, the hero and the menu.
  • The site never says what the job is: no role, pay, hours, location, duties or requirements, no jobs page, no Indeed link. A jobs page per role with honest pay facts.
  • Numbers can't be tapped. 0 call or text links; "Text Apply to 704-765-0308" is plain text. Tap-to-call and tap-to-text (APPLY pre-filled).
  • The chat window opens by itself over 27% of a tablet screen and sits over the phone form's Submit button; it sets a 13-month cookie nobody is told about. Open on tap only; name it in the privacy policy.
  • Legal pages are unfilled templates. Terms: "Country refers to: STATE OF OFFICE", dated January 2025, before the domain existed. Privacy says no data goes to third parties; the form and chat send it to an outside service. Rewrite with the real company; attorney review.
  • Dead ends: "Learn More" goes to a site that doesn't exist (http://team); 18 links go nowhere, including the footer "Contact" on every page. Fix or remove.
  • Testimonials can't be checked: 4 first-and-last names, no company or photo, each shown twice. Real quotes with permission, or none.
  • Hard to read and find: white on the brand orange is 2.23:1 (4.5:1 needed), Submit 1.87:1; no main heading on any of the 8 pages. Dark text on orange; one heading per page.

Trust and housekeeping: what is wrong, and the fix

  • The name changes from place to place: "Horizon" (site, X, LinkedIn, Google), "Horizon Outreach Partners and Events" (Instagram, Facebook, privacy policy), handles @horizonhopeinc and @Horizon_opsales; "HOPE" appears nowhere yet. Settle one public name, then use it everywhere.
  • Another "Horizon Outreach" owns the name online: a Houston nonprofit holds the registered trademark HORIZON OUTREACH, both domains, @horizonoutreach on Instagram and X, and the Glassdoor page that comes up for the name (about 40 reviews, about 2.2 stars). Always use the full name; claim Indeed and Glassdoor pages under it.
  • The owner's personal username is public in the site's user list, author page and page code on every page. Change the author name and hide the author page.
  • Email may land in spam: the anti-spoofing policy (DMARC) is set to "none" with no reports. Turn on reports, then tighten it.
  • Two form systems: contact goes to WordPress, apply to an outside service, so leads arrive in two places; the contact message is capped at 180 characters. One form system, one inbox.
  • The blog speaks to business owners: 3 long posts at exact clock times (17 Jul, 7 Aug, 15 Sep), nothing since. Posts for recruits: the work, the training, the pay structure.
  • Search basics: page titles are "<Page> - Horizon" with no city or job; 23 of 40 images have no description; theme demo images are listed for Google. "Field Sales Jobs in Charlotte, NC" style titles; clean sitemaps.

The HOPE site and a second Indeed account

The idea: a second website under HOPE so Horizon can open a second Indeed employer account and get more applicants. Indeed's own guidance speaks to this directly:

Among reasons a job post may be flagged: "posting identical or very similar roles from multiple employer accounts", and "listing the same vacancy multiple times with different or only slightly varied titles." Indeed, "Why is my job flagged", updated 26 Oct 2026
"Each position should typically be represented by a single, active post." "Ensure the company name and job title are accurate reflections of the business and role." Indeed, "Job Posting Rules and Policies", updated 8 Apr 2026

What can follow: posts hidden or pushed down, paid sponsorship required, free visibility lost, and Indeed "may disable any company's account, for any or no reason." With the same jobs on both accounts, the main Horizon account is exposed too. That it has worked elsewhere may only mean it hasn't been caught yet.

Three ways to get more applicants within the rules

1. HOPE as a real second brandWithin the rulesHOPE hires for genuinely different roles (for example event staff) with its own posts, once per real location, under an accurate company name.
2. One account, much better postsWithin the rulesReal titles, pay shown and explained, the actual location, door-to-door or event work stated plainly. Sponsor the best posts. The rules above already restrict vague "marketing" titles.
3. A HOPE careers site that feeds the same accountWithin the rulesThe way Vector Marketing runs workforstudents.com: a recruiting site and its own ads, naming the parent company, sending applicants to the same Indeed account and form.

Is the HOPE name free?

Quick checks, 28 Oct (a knockout search, not legal clearance)

  • Trademarks: 450 live US marks in marketing and business services contain HOPE; HOPE EVENTS is registered for arranging promotional events (Hope Media Group, reg. 8137790). Nothing filed as HORIZON OUTREACH PARTNERS.
  • Handles: @hope, @hope_outreach, @hope.outreach, @horizonhope taken on Instagram; @HOPEOutreach and @HorizonHOPE taken on X. @hopeoutreach and @hopeoutreachpartners look free on Instagram (confirm signed in).
  • Domains: hopeoutreachpartners.com, joinhopeteam.com and careerswithhope.com have no registration record; hopeoutreach.com, joinhope.com and hopeevents.com are taken. Nothing was bought.
  • Advice: a compound name ("HOPE Outreach Partners"), checked by an attorney before any filing or printing.
