2. Instagram's 12 visible posts got **9 likes and 4 comments in total**, and all 4 comments are spam bots. X has 84 posts and **0 followers**; its 5 visible tweets have 0 likes.

4. The name differs on every account: "Horizon Outreach Partners and Events" (IG, FB), "Horizon" (X, LinkedIn, Google), and the handles horizonhopeinc and Horizon_opsales. **@horizonhopenic does not exist.**

## Right
- Consistent posting on all four accounts.
- The same orange-sun skyline logo everywhere.
- The site footer links all four accounts, and every link works.
- The hiring post gives a URL and a text-to-apply number, and the /apply/ form works.
- No income claims and no "be your own boss" wording in any visible post or bio.
- Real team photos, not stock.
- Google's AI overview already links the name to @horizonhopeinc.

## Wrong, with the fix for each
1. **41 followers in total, 0 on X.** Put the effort into IG and LinkedIn. Ask staff to follow and reshare: LinkedIn lists 11–50 staff but the page has 27 followers (inferred: most staff don't follow).
2. **Spam comments left up.** Delete them and turn on Instagram's comment filter.
3. **Copy-pasted captions with up to 12 hashtags.** Write one post per platform and use 3–5 hashtags on IG.
4. **One hiring post in 19 days, missing role, work, pay, hours and employee/contractor status.** Make hiring a recurring post with those facts and the Charlotte office.
5. **IG apply link can't be tapped, and the bio goes to the homepage, which has no Apply in its menu.** Point the bio link at /apply/ and add "Now hiring" to the bio.
6. **Two bio versions, both client-facing.** Pick one and add a hiring line.
7. **Facebook has no username, and the site uses a tracking share URL.** Claim a username and update the site link.
8. **LinkedIn says "Public Company" and has no headquarters; the numeric-id link hits the login wall.** Set it to Privately Held (inferred: it isn't publicly traded), add the address, and link the slug URL.
9. **Google listing named "Horizon", with Ste 321 that the site and FB leave out, and no reviews.** Match the name and suite everywhere and ask for reviews. Google suggests "Horizon outreach partners reviews" as a related search.
10. **No video.** Try Reels before opening TikTok or YouTube.

## Recruiting-content review
Path from Instagram: profile with a client-facing bio → homepage (no Apply in the menu; footer "Text Apply") → /apply/ reachable only from Our Team. The hiring post's /apply/ URL can't be tapped on IG, but it is clickable on X and LinkedIn. No post or bio mentions pay, role, hours, employment type or where the work happens. Location appears only as hashtags and once as "Charlotte and beyond".

Honesty check: no income claims. The team outings are team-building, not displays of wealth. FTC MLM guidance Q15 calls images of houses, cars and luxury vacations implied earnings claims; that's the standard for HOPE to stay clear of (captures/ftc-mlm-guidance-excerpt.txt). The guidance is MLM-specific, so this is not a finding against Horizon.
