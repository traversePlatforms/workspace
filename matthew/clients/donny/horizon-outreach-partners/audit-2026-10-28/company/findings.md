# Horizon Outreach Partners: company, listings and recruiting footprint
Audit date 2026-10-28. Read-only: nothing was logged into, applied to, posted or bought. For Matthew Sharpe.

## Summary
1. **The company is about five months old online.** horizonoutreachpartners.com was registered 2026-05-11 (RDAP), and every site image was uploaded in May 2026. An aggregator lists a North Carolina LLC, **HORIZON OUTREACH PARTNERS & EVENTS LLC** (Charlotte), but the state registry blocked automated reads. Formation date, officers and agent are **not confirmed**.
2. **There is a Google Business Profile, but reviews are switched off.** It is listed as "Horizon", category "Business to business service", at 2459 Wilkinson Blvd **Ste 321**, phone (704) 615-0371. It has 7 photos and hours of Mon–Fri 8:30–7:30 and Sat 10–7. Google shows "Posting is currently turned off", so there is no rating and there are no reviews.
3. **No job postings could be found under the company's name.** LinkedIn shows 0 jobs for its company ID. SimplyHired (Indeed network) returns 0 for "horizon outreach partners" and "horizon outreach" in Charlotte. Indeed and Glassdoor refused automated reads and need a check by hand.
4. **The name collides on the sites applicants check.** "Horizon Outreach", a Houston nonprofit, owns the registered mark HORIZON OUTREACH (USPTO reg. 4391024), horizonoutreach.org and .com, and the Glassdoor and Indeed pages that rank for the name (about 40 Glassdoor reviews at about 2.2 out of 5).
5. **The "HOPE" plan runs into Indeed's rules.** Indeed's help page (updated 26 Oct 2026) lists "posting identical or very similar roles from multiple employer accounts" as a flag trigger and requires the company name to be an "accurate reflection of the business". HOPE is crowded: 450 live Class 35 marks contain HOPE, and HOPE EVENTS (reg. 8137790) covers event services. hopeoutreachpartners.com has no registration record.

**Not confirmed by any source:** Donny Parada's ownership, and any tie to the Veterans Association of America or Project Liberty. The only mention of either is an unverified search summary of a job post (see Recruiting).

## Right
1. A professional WordPress site on its own domain, with a consistent address, phone and email.
2. A managed Google profile with full hours, the website linked and a matching phone.
3. Text-to-apply, which keeps friction low for mobile, entry-level applicants.
4. Named staff with titles.
5. No complaints, AG actions or BBB file found (absence of data).

## Wrong, and the fix
| # | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Horizon Outreach" belongs to a Houston nonprofit: registered mark, both domains, 40 bad Glassdoor reviews ranking for the name. | Use "Horizon Outreach Partners & Events" everywhere. Claim the Indeed and Glassdoor employer pages under that exact name so they outrank Houston. |
| 2 | A second "HOPE" Indeed account for the same jobs is what Indeed's flag rules describe. HOPE is also crowded. | Use one account: one post per role per location, sponsor the best ones, show pay, and add a converting careers page. If a separate recruiting site is wanted, do it Vector's way: a careers site (e.g. joinhopeteam.com, unregistered) that names Horizon Outreach Partners and feeds the same Indeed account. Take legal advice before filing any HOPE mark. |
| 3 | The site talks to businesses, not recruits. | Add a Careers page: a day in the role, the pay structure in numbers (base vs commission, weekly range), the schedule including Saturdays, and text-to-apply. |
| 4 | The Google profile is named just "Horizon", with reviews off and a suite mismatch. | Rename it to the real business name. Make the suite match. Check in the profile manager why posting is off and request review if it's a short-term restriction. Add office and team photos (with consent). |
| 5 | Template leftovers and testimonials that can't be verified. | Fill in the Terms (North Carolina). Use real, attributable testimonials or remove them (FTC 16 CFR Part 465, to re-verify before quoting). |
| 6 | No owner or legal entity shown. | Put the LLC name in the footer, Terms and privacy pages, plus an owner line. |
| 7 | Possible NC solicitor licence. | Ask whether staff collect donations; if so, get the licence number. Describe the work honestly in ads. |
| 8 | No confirmed postings (LinkedIn 0, SimplyHired 0). | Post on the LinkedIn page. Make Indeed posts carry the same company name so they join up on one employer page. |
