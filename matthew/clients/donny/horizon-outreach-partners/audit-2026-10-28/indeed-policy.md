# Indeed's rules that bear on a second account (checked 2026-10-28)

The ask (Donny's assistant, 28 Oct): "Wed like to build a secondary websiite in order to create a second indeed account to increase applicants. I under that this has worked really well for brandons offices."

## What Indeed's own pages say

**"Why is my job flagged on Indeed?"** (US, last updated 26 Oct 2026): https://www.indeed.com/hire/resources/howtohub/job-flagged-on-indeed?co=US
- Lists, among reasons a post may be flagged: "posting identical or very similar roles from multiple employer accounts".
- Also: "listing the same vacancy multiple times with different or only slightly varied titles"; "Don't repost the same job multiple times. Duplicate listings can be flagged as spam."
- "Indeed requires that jobs be posted to their actual working location."
- "may disable any company's account, for any or no reason".

**"Indeed's Job Posting Rules and Policies for Employers"** (US, last updated 8 Apr 2026): https://www.indeed.com/hire/resources/howtohub/indeed-rules-and-policies-for-employers
- "Each position should typically be represented by a single, active post." Duplication "can lead to lower ranking in search results" or "require sponsorship to maintain visibility."
- "Ensure the company name and job title are accurate reflections of the business and role."
- "Posts for unpaid work, misleading commission-only structures or vague "marketing" roles aren't typically allowed."
- "Commission-only or other variable-pay roles can sometimes mislead job seekers if the earnings are not accurately described." The commission-only policy requires sponsoring "any job with guaranteed or inflated pay ranges".
- "Roles tied to multi-level marketing companies aren't allowed on Indeed".
- Posting one job in cities where it isn't located ("location blasting") "may require sponsorship or result in a post being removed from organic search results."

**"Job posting integrity and duplicates"** (help centre; the US copy did not come up, the AU/UK/IN copies read the same): https://au.indeed.com/help/employers/articles/job-posting-integrity-and-duplicates
- "each real job opportunity should appear only once on Indeed. Posting the same role more than once, or reposting in a way that confuses search results, may cause posts to be hidden, removed or require sponsorship."

## What that means for the HOPE plan

- A second account posting the same field-sales roles as Horizon is behaviour Indeed names as a flag reason. The likely cost is posts hidden or pushed down, being made to pay to sponsor, losing free visibility, and at worst the account disabled. Because the jobs are the same, the main Horizon account is exposed too, not only the new one.
- That it "worked for Brandon's offices" is second-hand, and it may simply not have been caught yet. Indeed updated its flag page two days ago.
- What stays within the rules:
  - HOPE as a genuine second brand with its **own distinct roles**, for example an events division hiring event staff, posted once per real location.
  - Or one account with better posts: real titles, pay shown and explained, the real location, the "door-to-door / commission" facts stated plainly. Vague "marketing" titles and inflated pay are themselves policy problems.
  - Plus channels that are not Indeed: a careers page with Google for Jobs markup, ZipRecruiter, LinkedIn, Handshake, referrals, and contacting applicants fast.

Sources checked by fetching Indeed's pages directly on 2026-10-28. Third-party summaries (HigherMe, Discovered, TeamEngine) agree but are not relied on.
