Peak Pickleball: new website, price and timeline

3 November 2026
Prepared by Matthew Sharpe

A rebuilt peakpickleball.us with one job: turn visitors into class sign-ups, since most people who take a class become members. It fixes what the 22 October audit found: no way to book from the site, slow phone pages, prices and rules that disagree from page to page, forms that can't be sent, and tracking with no privacy policy.

What we build

  • Try a class and Book a court on every page, plus the phone number. On phones, a bar fixed to the bottom of the screen: Book, Classes, Call.
  • A home page that sells in one screen: what Peak is, the 4.9 stars from 585 Google reviews, and the two buttons.
  • One set of prices, hours and rules, stored once and shown everywhere, so pages can't disagree again.
  • An edit screen for Cammy: schedule, events, announcements and photos, with no code and no waiting on a developer.
  • Buttons that open the right CourtReserve page (class sign-up, open play, court booking), not CourtReserve's front page.
  • Forms that work: corporate events and parties, scholarships, and an email sign-up that feeds Peak's email list.
  • Fast on phones: compressed photos, no 14 MB autoplay video, the map only on the Contact page. Target: the main image in under 2.5 seconds (it takes 6 to 14 today).
  • Privacy, terms, waiver and accessibility pages. Peak's Google Analytics and Meta Pixel stay, with clicks on Book and Class tracked for the coming ads.
  • Old page addresses redirected to the new ones, so Peak keeps its place in Google.
  • Real photos only, from Peak's own events and daily play. No stock people.

What we need from Peak to start

  • Logins or invites: the domain and hosting, the current WordPress site (built by Lighthouse: who controls it?), Google Analytics, Meta (Pixel), Google Business Profile, the email platform.
  • One answer per price and rule: the family rate, cancellation notice, membership notice, walk-ins and closing time. The pages give two answers for each today.
  • Photos and video from Cammy: events, classes, daily play.
  • The waiver and terms wording Peak uses, approved by Peak's own lawyer. We build the pages; the legal wording is Peak's.
  • CourtReserve: whether first-time players can book without waiting for approval. That's a CourtReserve setting.
  • Who signs off: one person for design and content decisions, so each week ends on time.
