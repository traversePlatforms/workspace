Peak Pickleball: a new website

3 November 2026
Prepared by Matthew Sharpe

A ground-up rebuild of peakpickleball.us, architected around one objective: converting visitors into class sign-ups. Mobile-first, performance-optimized, and driven by a centralized content layer that acts as the single source of truth for every price, hour and rule.

Scope and deliverables

  • Persistent Try a class and Book a court calls to action site-wide, with click-to-call on mobile
  • A conversion-optimized landing page with above-the-fold social proof from Peak's 4.9-star Google reviews
  • A rationalized information architecture: about 13 purpose-built pages consolidated from today's 34
  • A unified data model for prices, hours and rules, rendered dynamically so no two pages can drift out of sync
  • A secure content-management back end so Peak can publish schedule, event and photo updates without a developer
  • Validated, server-side lead-capture forms for corporate events, parties and scholarships
  • Compliance pages (privacy policy, terms of service, liability waiver) and conversion-tracking instrumentation ready for paid campaigns
  • A 301 redirect map for every legacy URL, preserving existing search equity in Google

Delivery roadmap: six one-week sprints

Sprint 1: DiscoveryInformation architecture and sitemap. Reconcile every price, hour and rule into one canonical data set.
Sprint 2: UX/UI designLanding-page design system and visual direction, through to Peak's sign-off.
Sprint 3: Front-end buildResponsive templates for every page, wired to the shared data layer.
Sprint 4: IntegrationsContent-management back end, form handling and booking-flow integration.
Sprint 5: Optimization and QAAsset optimization, page-speed tuning, redirect mapping, and user acceptance testing on Peak's devices.
Sprint 6: LaunchResolve UAT findings, then production deployment and go-live.

Sprint 1 begins once Peak provides website and Google account access, a single answer for each price and rule, and photo assets.

Out of scope

Paid media management, social content publishing, migration off the current email platform or CourtReserve, and photo or video production. Each can be scoped and quoted as a separate engagement when Peak is ready.
