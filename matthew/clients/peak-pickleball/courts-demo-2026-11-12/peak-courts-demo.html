What the member is told

  • Other members' bookings show as "Booked". Names stay hidden.
  • The price on the confirm screen is the member's own: $12 an hour for drop-in, $7 an hour on Starter and Play, nothing extra on Flex (in Flex hours), Unlimited and Platinum.
  • The cancel time is spelled out on every booking: 4 hours before the start.
  • Flex hours are marked for everyone, so the quiet times are easy to spot.

Open Play and classes

Peak said members hold back when they see only one or two names on a session, and that it has tried showing and hiding the list. Here that becomes a setting Peak controls. Full sessions keep a waitlist, and a cancelled spot is offered to the next person by text with 30 minutes to claim it.

        Cards on file

        How payment would be handled.

        • Members save a card once, on the payment processor's own secure form (Stripe or Square).
        • Peak's system keeps only the processor's reference and the last four digits. It never sees or stores the card number.
        • Monthly dues, court fees and drop-ins all charge that saved card, or the card reader at the desk.
        • Memberships can't be paused, and cancelling needs 15 days' notice, as on Peak's page today.
        Moving over means members re-enter their card once. Saved cards usually can't be moved from one booking system's processor to another.

        How a switch would work

        The honest version. Booking is how the club runs day to day, so nothing would switch until Peak says so. Both systems would run side by side first.
