Your Peak email signature

  1. Click and drag over everything inside the dashed box (from the logo to the address), then press Ctrl+C.
  2. In Outlook on the web: Settings (the gear) › Mail › Compose and reply › Email signature.
  3. Click + New signature, name it "Peak", click in the box and press Ctrl+V.
  4. Under "Select default signatures", pick Peak for new messages and for replies/forwards, then Save.
