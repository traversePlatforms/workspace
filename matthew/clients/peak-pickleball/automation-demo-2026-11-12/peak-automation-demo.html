Approval queue

Every message waits here until someone at Peak approves it. Approve sends it at the time shown. Edit changes the wording, and an edited message needs approving again. Hold keeps it back until it is released.

Nothing goes out without Approve.

The only exception is a flow Peak switches to "Send automatically" below. Held messages never send, in either mode.

How each flow sends

Every flow starts on "Hold for approval". Peak can switch one to automatic once it trusts the wording, and switch it back at any time.

How it's handled

The rules every flow runs under, whatever it sends.

CourtReserveMembers, bookings, billing. Stays the system of record.
The flowsRead who joined, booked or went quiet, then draft the right message.
Peak approvesThen it sends from Peak's name, to people who opted in.

Today Peak's email and text go through Patch Retention ($499 a month, per Tyler on 11 Nov). These flows are that kind of work: they sit beside CourtReserve and never replace it.

Cancellation rules (4 hours for Open Play and courts, 24 hours for classes, $10 late fee for University classes): Peak's website and Tyler, 11 Nov 2026.

Already running elsewhere

This isn't a new idea being tried on Peak. I already run automated, approval-first email systems for other businesses. The same pieces would carry over.
