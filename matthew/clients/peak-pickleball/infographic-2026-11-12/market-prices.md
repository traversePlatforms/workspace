# Peak Pickleball: market prices for the cost-breakdown infographic

Researched 2026-11-12. These are market prices only. Matthew's own prices are deliberately left out.

Scope: what a small-to-mid US business typically pays. **No Colorado or Colorado Springs rate data was found.** Every service-labour figure is national, and the Upwork figures are global marketplace medians, so they include offshore freelancers. Software figures are list prices that each vendor publishes on its own pricing page, read on 2026-11-12. Those pages show no effective date, so the date given is the read date.

How "typical" is set: for software it is the middle of named vendors' list prices. For services, no survey reports a true average, so the typical figure is the **midpoint of the sourced range**. On the infographic it should read "typical" or "midpoint", never "average", unless the row says the source measured an average.

### Notes for the infographic

- **CourtReserve, Peak's own line:** $549/mo is CourtReserve's published **monthly-billing** price for Momentum. The same plan is published at **$499/mo billed annually**, a $600/yr saving that CourtReserve states on its pricing page. This is a fact about the vendor's list, not a recommendation, and it is worth showing.
- **CourtReserve tier fit:** Advance ($369/mo) is capped at 16 courts, so a 24-court club is on Momentum by court count, not by choice of features.
- **Klaviyo step:** Klaviyo's slider steps from 15,000 to 20,000 active profiles. At ~16,000 profiles the 20,000 tier ($375) applies, and 15,000 profiles is $350.
- **Brevo is priced on emails sent, not contacts.** Contacts are unlimited. $139 is the Standard plan at 80,001-100,000 emails/month, which is enough for ~6 sends a month to 16,000 contacts. Fewer sends cost less.
- **Mailchimp's first-year promo:** Mailchimp shows $242.25/mo for 12 months (15% off for 10,000+ contacts) before the $285 list price. Use the list price.
- **SMS volume:** only opted-in mobile numbers can be texted, so the cost depends on the opted-in count, not on 16,000. At ~1.2 cents a text, one text to 5,000 opted-in members is roughly $60 in carrier charges, before any platform fee.
- **Service ranges are wide** because buyers range from sole traders to enterprises. Clutch's *mean* project and monthly figures (e.g. $38,105 per web-design project) are skewed by large clients and are **not** used as a "typical" figure.
