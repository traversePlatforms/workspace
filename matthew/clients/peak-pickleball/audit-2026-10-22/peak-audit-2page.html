Peak Pickleball: online presence audit

22 October 2026
Prepared by Matthew Sharpe

Players love Peak: 4.9 stars from 585 Google reviews, the best rating in Colorado Springs pickleball. Online, though, the club is hard to book, slow on phones, inconsistent from page to page, and spread across 12 accounts with only two kept up. Almost every fix is setup work, not ad spend.

Website: what is wrong, and the fix

  • No way to book a court. A first-timer must find CourtReserve, make an account and wait for approval. "Book a Court" button in the header.
  • The corporate-event form can't be sent: 17 fields, only "Print / Save PDF", and the events email sits on a page no menu links to. A real enquiry form with phone and email.
  • Slow: a 14 MB autoplay video and 4–6 MB photos. Compress; load the map only on Contact.
  • Tracking with no privacy policy: Google Analytics and Meta Pixel on all 34 pages. Privacy, terms, waiver, accessibility pages.
  • Pages disagree: family rate $6 vs $7; cancel 24 h vs 4 h; "cancel anytime" vs 15 days' notice; walk in vs reservation; close 9pm vs 10pm. One set of rules.
  • Dead ends: sponsor package buttons go to a missing page; the scholarship "application" has no form; staff personal emails and cell numbers used to book.
  • Missing: Peak's iPhone app, the Wellhub partnership, schedules, reviews, lesson prices, a corporate page.
  • Hard to read: the cyan buttons fail contrast (2.8:1; 4.5:1 needed); tap targets as small as 21 px.

Reviews: Peak's biggest strength

But no visible owner replies, no "leave a review" link on the site, and Peak is missing from every "best of" list.

Google, maps and listings

  • Google's "Book online" lands on a page saying "Briargate, CO", open from 5 AM.
  • Peak Golf's hours and description appear on Peak's MapQuest and Yellow Pages pages; its own Google profile rates 3.0 (4 reviews).
  • Unclaimed: Nextdoor, Yellow Pages, Apple Maps (Peak Golf). Hours differ on 4 platforms.
  • Wrong details elsewhere: "16 courts" (DNVR), "Public / Free" (Pickleball Plus), the tournament listed twice at the wrong price (Bounce).

Social: what we would change

  • Rename Instagram to "Peak Pickleball | Colorado Springs" (no "Peak" in it today).
  • Post on a calendar, 4–5 a week, offer and price in the first line.
  • Repost every reel to Facebook, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Threads: one Facebook reel reached 84,000 plays.
  • Run the 963-member group: weekly open courts, sign-ups, results.
  • Claim a Facebook username; fix the link page (it points to a Greensboro club's TikTok).
  • One tagline everywhere, not five ("top", "Colorado's largest", "nation's largest"…).
