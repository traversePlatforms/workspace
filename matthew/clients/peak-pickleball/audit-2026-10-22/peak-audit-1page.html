Peak Pickleball: online presence audit

22 October 2026
Prepared by Matthew Sharpe

Players love Peak: 4.9 stars from 585 Google reviews. Online, though, the club is hard to book, slow on phones and inconsistent from page to page. Most fixes are quick and need no ad spend.

Website: what is wrong

  • No way to book a court. First-timers must find CourtReserve, create an account and wait for approval. Add a "Book a Court" button.
  • The corporate-event form can't be sent. 17 fields, only "Print / Save PDF"; the events email is on a page no menu links to. Real enquiry form + phone.
  • Slow on phones. A 14 MB autoplay video means the main image takes up to 14 seconds (good is under 2.5). Compress the video and images.
  • Pages disagree: family rate $6 vs $7; cancel 24 h vs 4 h; "cancel anytime" vs 15 days' notice; walk in vs reservation needed. One set of rules.
  • Tracking with no privacy policy (Google Analytics and Meta Pixel on all 34 pages). Privacy, terms, waiver pages.
  • Dead ends: sponsor package buttons go to a missing page; the scholarship "application" has no form; staff personal emails and cell numbers used for bookings.
  • Not mentioned anywhere: Peak's own iPhone app, the Wellhub partnership, schedules, reviews, lesson prices.

Google, maps and reviews

  • Google's "Book online" goes to a page listing the city as "Briargate" and hours from 5 AM. Correct CourtReserve.
  • Peak Golf's hours and description appear on Peak Pickleball's MapQuest and Yellow Pages listings. Fix at the source (Yelp).
  • A separate "Peak Golf" Google profile rates 3.0 (4 reviews), one alleging fraud, at the same address and phone.
  • No visible owner replies to reviews, and no "leave a review" link on the site. Reply weekly; add a review link.
  • Unclaimed listings: Nextdoor, Yellow Pages, Apple Maps (Peak Golf). Hours differ on 4 platforms.
  • Missing from every "best of" list, including the Gazette's Top 7; the city tourism site calls Springs Pickleball "premier".

Social media

  • One real channel. Instagram (918 followers, about 5 posts a week); Facebook 1.7K followers with no custom web address; Threads 12 followers; LinkedIn tagline still "24 Pickleball courts in 24".
  • No YouTube or TikTok, though the FAQ tells beginners to watch YouTube. Springs Pickleball livestreams tournaments.
  • The name changes by platform: Instagram's display name has no "Peak"; five different taglines ("top", "Colorado's largest", "nation's largest").

What we would do

Weeks 1–2: quick fixesBook-a-Court button; one set of prices and rules; privacy, terms and waiver pages; fix broken links; claim and correct every listing; one tagline everywhere.
Month 1: make it easy to say yesFast, phone-first home page; working corporate and scholarship forms; schedules, lesson prices, app and Wellhub pages; Google posts and review replies.
Ongoing: growA weekly content plan across Instagram, Facebook, YouTube Shorts and TikTok from tournaments and leagues; review requests after visits; local press and "best of" nominations.
