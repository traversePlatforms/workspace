Current site vs the new mock, side by side

For MatthewScreens taken 26 Nov 2026
In one line
The new site is built around one job: get new people to the free class, which turns 31.9% of new people into members within 60 days (Open Play: 3.1%, court bookings: 2.0%). It also fixes the dead ends, the conflicting prices and rules, and the slow phone load.

What changes on every page

  • Same header everywhere with two buttons: Try a free class and Book now. On a phone, a fixed bottom bar: Free class, Book, Call.
  • Menus follow Cammie's map: Play, Free class, Events & Parties, Memberships, Beyond the Court, Visit.
  • Every enquiry has a form that reaches a real inbox and is counted. Today, events, contact and sponsors have no working form.
  • One source for prices and rules, so the pages can't contradict each other.
  • Readable and easy to tap: text on Peak cyan is navy (white on cyan is 2.07:1, below the 4.5:1 standard), and buttons are at least 48 px tall (some taps are 21 px today).
  • Faster on phones: the home video is compressed and has a pause button. Today it's a 14.1 MB autoplay, and the main image took 5.9–14.3 s on a phone in October.

Also fixed

  • Dead links: Leagues (redirects away), the court sponsor package (page not found), the professor dashboard (page not found), old hotel booking dates.
  • Out-of-date content: the Ziggi's coffee button, 2024 construction posts.
  • One tagline instead of five. Cammie picks it.
  • Privacy policy before any more ads. Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel run today with no policy posted.
  • Real Peak photos from Cammie's brand library, no stock or AI. Cammie confirms permission for the people shown (including the kids in Youth Clinic shots).
Where it stands: a private mock only; nothing on peakpickleball.us has changed. Yellow [brackets] in the mock mark wording or prices waiting on Peak. The platform decision is Mon 30 Nov. Every price and rule needs Tyler's sign-off before launch.
