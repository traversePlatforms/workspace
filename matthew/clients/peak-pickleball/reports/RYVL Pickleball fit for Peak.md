# RYVL wants Peak's courts: read the terms first

*Briefing for Nneka, Peak Pickleball partnerships, ahead of the RYVL meeting on Monday 30 Nov 2026. Researched 25 Nov 2026. Unless a line says otherwise, every RYVL fact below is RYVL describing itself on its own website or app-store pages, as they read on 25 Nov 2026. Almost nothing has been checked by anyone independent.*

**Bottom line:** RYVL Pickleball is a brand-new Colorado Springs-area app, about two months old, that keeps a player's game record, runs rec nights, leagues and ladders, and puts on its own paid "Gauntlet" events at host venues. It is not a good replacement for CourtReserve, and Peak should not move bookings, memberships or payments onto it. At most it is a cheap add-on worth a small, written, time-limited trial, for one rec night or league, after RYVL answers the questions at the end of this briefing. The company is very thin on paper. No founder is named. Its legal company could not be found in Colorado's business records. The apps went live on 28–30 Oct 2026 and Android shows "10+" downloads. RYVL publishes no club contract and no live CourtReserve connection. The most urgent item has nothing to do with software: **RYVL's site already has a registration page for "Golden Ring Championship 2027", labelled "Run by Peak Pickleball", with the entry fee "TBD" and "Payment opens soon"** ([RYVL event page](https://ryvlpickleball.com/events/10)). Before Monday, Nneka should find out inside Peak who agreed to that page, and on what terms. Peak's ~800 members likely outnumber RYVL's entire claimed user base, so Peak goes into this meeting with the leverage.

## RYVL in one page: a player app with a club add-on

RYVL sells itself to players as "Your whole pickleball life. Organized." It keeps one record of every game: rec sessions, regular playing groups (RYVL calls them "crews"), leagues, a city Challenge Ladder and tournaments. It ranks players on city and regional leaderboards ([RYVL home](https://ryvlpickleball.com/)). It also runs its own rating, the **RYVL Rating**, on the same 2.0–8.0 scale as DUPR and shown next to it. RYVL says "a win never lowers it" and that it "accounts for which player the other team targets", but it does not publish how the rating is worked out ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ryvlpickleball.app)).

For clubs there are two plans. **Compete** runs rec nights, leagues, club tournaments, live standings on club TVs and a club leaderboard "beside your current booking software". **Club OS** does all of that plus court booking, memberships, dues, check-in, lessons, waivers and payments, which makes it a full CourtReserve replacement ([RYVL Clubs](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/)). On a night at the club, players check in, get court draws, enter scores on their phones and watch standings update on the TV. Formats include rec nights, mixers, round robins and "Moneyball" (same source).

There is also **the Gauntlet**, a RYVL-branded competitive event series it calls a "national pro-am pickleball circuit", for players rated about 3.5 to 5.5 ([RYVL Gauntlet](https://ryvlpickleball.com/events/gauntlet)). A venue supplies the courts, a date and a director. RYVL runs registration, payments, seeding, scoring and the brand, and pays the venue a share. "The brand, format, points engine and payment rails stay central" ([RYVL Partners](https://ryvlpickleball.com/partners)).

## What it costs, and how RYVL makes money

Club prices are published. The money that matters most to Peak, the split on events, is not.

| What | Price (RYVL's site, 25 Nov 2026) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Player app, free tier | $0 | Join open games, profile, win-loss record, leaderboards ([Membership](https://ryvlpickleball.com/membership)) |
| RYVL+ (players) | $7.99/month, **free for everyone "during launch"** | No end date given. RYVL says it will give notice and "you won't be charged without agreeing" ([Terms](https://ryvlpickleball.com/terms)) |
| "City Founder" (players) | "Coming soon", no price | ([Membership](https://ryvlpickleball.com/membership)) |
| **Compete** (clubs) | **$99/month per location, or $990/year** | Runs beside CourtReserve ([Clubs](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/)) |
| **Club OS** (clubs) | **$299/month per location + 1% of payments, or $2,990/year** | Replaces CourtReserve. Payments go to the club's own Stripe account ([Clubs FAQ](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/#faq)) |
| Free trial | 30 days, no card, cancel anytime | Afterwards you pick a plan or stop ([Clubs](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/)) |
| Gauntlet venue share | **Not published** | "Set in your pilot terms, in writing, before the first session" ([Partners](https://ryvlpickleball.com/partners)) |

RYVL's only money example is a made-up one: a sold-out weekend of "72 players × $65 ≈ $4,700 gross entries", settled as "gross · platform fee · your share" ([Partners](https://ryvlpickleball.com/partners)). It publishes no actual entry fee, platform fee or venue percentage. The site mentions no sponsorship or advertising money either.

On payments: with Club OS, members pay into the club's own Stripe account and RYVL takes 1% ([Clubs FAQ](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/#faq)). Event entries work differently. Players pay "on our website through Stripe" ([Support](https://ryvlpickleball.com/support)), so **RYVL appears to collect event money and pay the venue afterwards**. That is an inference. RYVL does not say who the seller of record is, how long payouts take, or who pays card-processing fees and chargebacks.

## Traction so far: one month on the app stores

RYVL's numbers are small and come with no dates. Its club page claims **"220+ members playing on RYVL" and "800+ rec games tracked"**, with no date and no explanation of what counts ([RYVL Clubs](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/)). The apps are about four weeks old. **iOS version 1.0 came out 28 Oct 2026 and has 5.0 stars from 5 ratings** ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ryvl-pickleball/id6821790245)). **Android came out 30 Oct 2026 and shows "10+" downloads** ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ryvlpickleball.app)). All three written reviews were posted between 28 Oct and 1 Nov 2026 and are glowing ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ryvl-pickleball/id6821790245)). Launch-week reviews like these usually come from the founders' own circle, so they say little about how the app really works.

The one real sign of activity is in-person events. RYVL lists a **Colorado Springs Gauntlet pilot on 25–26 Jul at Springs Pickleball East, "SOLD OUT — 88 entries"**, and a second Springs Gauntlet on 26–27 Sep 2026, marked complete ([RYVL Gauntlet](https://ryvlpickleball.com/gauntlet)). Note that **this is another Colorado Springs venue**. A "Denver Gauntlet No. 1" on 22 Aug still says "registration opening soon", so that listing looks out of date. No paying club customer is named anywhere, and RYVL's own club directory showed "No clubs in your city on RYVL yet" ([RYVL Clubs directory](https://ryvlpickleball.com/clubs)).

The point for Nneka: **Peak's ~800 members are likely more than RYVL's whole claimed user base.** RYVL needs Peak more than Peak needs RYVL, so Peak can ask for good terms.

## Fit with Peak: an extra app beside CourtReserve, with no link between them

Peak runs bookings, memberships, events and payments in CourtReserve, member email in Patch Retention, and DUPR-rated leagues. Here is how RYVL lines up.

| Peak need | What RYVL offers | Fit |
|---|---|---|
| Bookings, memberships, payments (CourtReserve) | Compete "runs beside" CourtReserve. Club OS replaces it and can **import** from CourtReserve ([Clubs FAQ](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/#faq)) | **No live sync published.** With Compete, members would likely sign up in two places and use two apps and two logins |
| DUPR-rated leagues | Shows DUPR next to its own rating. Sending results to DUPR is optional, chosen event by event. "Rec games never go to DUPR" ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ryvlpickleball.app)) | **Unverified** whether RYVL is an official DUPR partner. No DUPR-side listing was found |
| Member email (Patch Retention) | No member email tool. Its own email runs through Postmark ([Privacy](https://ryvlpickleball.com/privacy)) | No link. Patch stays as it is |
| Open play, rec nights, leagues, ladders | Its strongest area: check-in, court draws, phone scoring, live TV boards, ladders, city leaderboards ([Clubs](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/)) | Real overlap with what CourtReserve already does (see below) |
| Peak's own tournaments (Golden Ring) | Event listings and registration on RYVL. Golden Ring 2027 is already up ([event page](https://ryvlpickleball.com/events/10)) | Needs urgent clarification (next section) |

**CourtReserve already does much of this.** Its feature list includes leagues and ladders, rankings, round robins, a player match-maker, a member app and DUPR rating sync ([CourtReserve features](https://courtreserve.com/features/)). Two league tools also plug straight into CourtReserve, so sign-ups and payments stay where they are. **Swish** connects to CourtReserve, runs live scores and sends results to DUPR. It costs about $25/month as a CourtReserve add-on plus a Swish plan, and its webinar cites $85 and $125 options (CourtReserve page, Apr 2025, [CourtReserve: Swish](https://courtreserve.com/?p=12718)). **Pickleheads** launched a CourtReserve connection on 28 Jul 2026, with live standings and one-click DUPR submission ([Pickleheads](https://www.pickleheads.com/blog/pickleheads-courtreserve-integration-launch)). DUPR says its own club tools cost clubs nothing ([DUPR clubs](https://dupr.com/clubs), seen in search results only). Peak's CourtReserve plan is not known. On the top "Momentum" tier, CourtReserve lists "All integrations included" ([CourtReserve pricing](https://courtreserve.com/pricing)), so RYVL would be competing with tools Peak may already have at no extra cost.

**What RYVL adds that the others do not:** its own rating, city-wide leaderboards, a season race, live TV boards and a local competitive "scene" built around the Gauntlet. That is a real engagement idea. It also cuts both ways. **RYVL's leaderboards and Gauntlets work across venues**, it has already run events at Springs Pickleball East, and **The Picklr** (16 courts at 1685 Briargate Pkwy, about a block away) runs DUPR ladder leagues in its own app ([CourtSource: Picklr](https://www.courtsource.us/pickleball/co/colorado-springs/the-picklr-colorado-springs-f10014), seen in search results only). An app that takes Peak's players into a city-wide scene can also send them to other clubs' events.

## The Golden Ring page is the first thing to sort out

RYVL's event page lists **"Golden Ring Championship 2027"**, 3rd annual, **15–17 Jan 2027 at Peak**, as a "Club tournament · Run by Peak Pickleball". It uses RYVL's Register button, shows **"Entry fee: TBD" and "Payment opens soon"**, and says registration closes Tue 12 Jan at 5:00 PM ([event page](https://ryvlpickleball.com/events/10)). It is also the "Next Event / Registration Open" card on RYVL's Gauntlet page ([RYVL Gauntlet](https://ryvlpickleball.com/gauntlet)), and RYVL's homepage shows "528 spots open" ([RYVL home](https://ryvlpickleball.com/)). Divisions use DUPR, capped at 12 players or teams each. The page names Hotel Polaris as the official hotel and a Saturday social at the Aviator Bar.

Four problems with it. **First**, it puts Peak's name on a RYVL page, and no published agreement covers that. **Second**, refunds are sent to Peak: "Refunds, withdrawals and anything else about the day: ask Peak Pickleball" (same page). Meanwhile RYVL's payment model suggests RYVL would collect the entry money. Peak would carry the customer-service load for money it might not be holding. **Third**, the page contradicts itself on playoffs: top 2 per pool in one section, top 4 to a medal bracket in the "From Peak Pickleball" section. **Fourth**, earlier Golden Ring editions were listed on pickleballtournaments.com ([pickleballtournaments.com](https://pickleballtournaments.com/tournaments/golden-ring-national-championship)), and the RYVL page does not mention it. Two live registration pages for one event invites double sign-ups and confusion.

It may well be that someone at Peak set this up with RYVL on purpose. If so, Nneka needs to know before Monday who agreed, what was agreed, and whether it is written down. If nobody did, Peak should ask RYVL to take the page down or mark it clearly until terms exist.

## Terms and data: written for players, with no club contract published

RYVL publishes one player Terms of Use and one Privacy Policy, **both dated 12 Oct 2026**, from RYVL Sports Inc. under Colorado law ([Terms](https://ryvlpickleball.com/terms), [Privacy](https://ryvlpickleball.com/privacy)). **No club, organiser or venue agreement is published**, and no data-processing agreement either. The player terms have no arbitration clause and no indemnity clause, and they cap RYVL's liability at whatever the user paid in the past 12 months. Players must be 13 or older, and under-18s need a parent or guardian to agree. Peak's junior players would need that sign-off on top of Peak's own waiver.

On data, RYVL's position is that **the player owns the record, not the club**. "Their games, rating and record are theirs and follow them" ([Clubs FAQ](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/#faq)). Clubs see "what they need to run their club or event", such as rosters and results ([Privacy](https://ryvlpickleball.com/privacy)). Players can download their own record as CSV or JSON. Nothing published says Peak can export its full member or event data, or that RYVL won't market Gauntlets, RYVL+ or other clubs' events to Peak's members. RYVL says it does not sell personal data or use it for advertising ([Privacy](https://ryvlpickleball.com/privacy)). Its App Store privacy label, though, lists email as used for "developer's advertising or marketing" ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ryvl-pickleball/id6821790245)). That probably means RYVL's own emails, but it is unverified.

No exclusivity, signage or promotion duties are published for partner clubs. Those would sit in the unpublished pilot terms. In practice, members join "with its code or the QR poster" ([Clubs FAQ](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/#faq)), so Peak would end up promoting RYVL to its members either way.

**Still unverified:** where RYVL Sports Inc. is incorporated and whether it is in good standing. Whether it has any tie to the Englewood RYVL LLC. Who the founders are. What the "220+ members" and "800+ games" cover. Whether it is an official DUPR partner with automatic result upload. Whether any club pays for it today. How the RYVL Rating is calculated. Who set up the Golden Ring 2027 page.

## Questions for Monday, most important first

**Before the meeting (inside Peak):** find out who, if anyone, set up the Golden Ring 2027 page with RYVL and what was agreed. Also check which CourtReserve plan Peak is on, and whether Swish or Pickleheads would come free or cheap on it.

1. **Golden Ring 2027:** Who created the "Run by Peak Pickleball" page, and with whose approval? Who sets the entry fee? Who collects the money, and when and how does Peak get paid? What is RYVL's fee? Who handles refunds? Can the page come down, or be clearly marked as not open, until terms are signed? Is it meant to replace pickleballtournaments.com or run alongside it? Which advancement rule is correct?
2. **Who are we dealing with?** What is the exact legal company, which state is it incorporated in, and when was it formed? Can RYVL send a certificate of good standing? How do RYVL Sports Inc., "RYVL Sports" and Garlew Inc. relate? Who are the founders and full-time staff, and what is Nick Hagan's role?
3. **The contract:** Please send the written club or venue agreement and the pilot terms before anything is agreed. What are the minimum term, auto-renewal, notice period and refund rules on yearly plans? Is there a data-processing agreement?
4. **Member data:** Who owns Peak's roster, member contact details and event data? Can Peak export all of it, any time and at the end? Will RYVL email or push-notify Peak's members about Gauntlets, RYVL+ or other clubs' events, and can Peak switch that off?
5. **CourtReserve:** Is there any live sync of members, registrations, payments or check-ins, or only the one-time import? Will members have to register twice?
6. **DUPR:** Is RYVL an official DUPR partner with automatic result upload, or is it manual? Do league results go to DUPR by default?
7. **Gauntlet money:** What is the venue's percentage and RYVL's platform fee per entry? Who is the seller of record on the card statement? Who pays card fees and absorbs chargebacks? How fast are payouts?
8. **Competition and exclusivity:** Would RYVL keep running Gauntlets at Springs Pickleball East or other Springs venues? Could Peak get local exclusivity? Does RYVL expect Peak to stop using other league tools?
9. **Promotion asked of Peak:** posters, member emails, signage, social posts, use of Peak's name and logo. Which are expected, and which are optional?
10. **Liability on our courts:** Whose waiver applies at a RYVL event at Peak? What insurance does RYVL carry? Who covers injuries or disputes?
11. **Traction and references:** What do "220+ members" and "800+ games" cover, and since when? Which clubs pay today, and can Peak speak to one?
12. **Money and runway:** How is RYVL funded, and what happens to Peak's events and players if it shuts down?
13. **Player pricing:** When does RYVL+ stop being free? Can Peak buy or give out seats for its members? What will "City Founder" cost?
14. **Sponsorship:** Is there any sponsorship or advertising money on leaderboards or events, and how would it be split with Peak?

## Conclusion

The meeting is less about choosing software than about getting a new partner to put things in writing. RYVL's product idea, a local competitive scene with a portable player record, is reasonable. But Peak already pays for a system, and add-ons such as Swish and Pickleheads cover most of the same ground with a documented CourtReserve link. That leaves RYVL's value in its events and community energy, which is also where the unpublished money split, the cross-venue pull and the Golden Ring listing all sit. A sensible outcome for Monday: commit to nothing, get the Golden Ring page settled, and ask for the club agreement and the venue share in writing. If the answers come back clean, run a 30-day Compete trial on one rec night or league, kept away from tournament payments, and judge it on member uptake. If RYVL can't produce a contract or name its own company, that is the answer.
