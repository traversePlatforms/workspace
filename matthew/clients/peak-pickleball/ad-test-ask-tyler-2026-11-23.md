Subject: Ad test for new members: budget and a few things first

Hi Tyler,

I went through Peak's numbers to see where new members actually come from. The free class is the clear winner: 31.9% of people whose first visit was a free class paid dues within 60 days. For Open Play it was 3.1%, court bookings 2.0%, leagues and events 1.8%.

So the plan is to run Meta ads (Facebook and Instagram) for the free class, to people within driving distance of the club.

What I'm asking for:

1. A budget for a 4-week test. I'd suggest $500 to $1,000 for the month. A new member is worth about $148 in dues over their first 6 months, so the ads pay for themselves if a free-class booking costs under about $37. We'll know the real cost by week 2, and we stop or scale from there.
2. Access to Peak's Meta ad account (or the OK to set one up under Peak's Business Manager).
3. Your OK on the ad wording and photos before anything runs. Real photos from the club only.

Before any ad goes live:

- The website needs a privacy policy. The Meta Pixel is already running on peakpickleball.us and Meta requires one. This is overdue whether or not we run ads.
- The free-class follow-up emails in Patch need to be back on. They stopped sending on 29 Sep, so new people from the ads wouldn't get the reminder or the Starter offer.
- If possible, make "How did you hear about us?" required at sign-up in CourtReserve. It's blank on 95% of accounts right now, and it's how we'll see which sign-ups came from the ads.

Free-class capacity is the other thing to watch. At $2,000 a month the ads could fill every open free-class seat, so if it works we may want to add class times back.

Let me know the budget you're comfortable with and I'll get the rest lined up.

Matthew
