The facility

Two levels, 24 courts, all indoors

Professionally surfaced, oversized concrete courts in an air-conditioned building, with spectator areas and noise-dampening technology.

  • 4 championship courts (34' x 64') with spectator seating
  • 19 recreational courts (32' x 62')
  • 1 private court
  • 2 dink practice courts with full-width nets
  • Free loaner paddles; balls on every court
  • ADA accessible, with guest Wi-Fi
  • Whistle Pig Brewing pouring on site
  • Free daily coffee at the self-serve station

Memberships

Play your way: drop in or join

Month-to-month plans with no long-term contract. Drop in from $12 an hour, or join from $39 a month.

  • Unlimited and Platinum: no hourly court fees
  • Family Plan: under-18s join an eligible adult plan with no monthly fee, paying only court fees
  • 5% military and senior discount on eligible plans
