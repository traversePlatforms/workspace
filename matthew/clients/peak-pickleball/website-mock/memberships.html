The fine print

Membership rules, in one place

In the full build these come from one "Peak facts" sheet, so the FAQ, this page and the welcome emails always agree.

Reservations and cancellations

  • Court and Open Play reservations: cancel at least 4 hours ahead.
  • Classes, clinics and lessons: cancel at least 24 hours ahead.
  • Payment is taken when you book online.
  • Peak may move a reservation to another court to make the best use of the courts.

Check-in

  • Check in with the QR code in your CourtReserve app.
  • No phone? Show a valid photo ID.
  • Without either, we can't let you play.

Guest passes

  • For private reservations, not Open Play.
  • No rollover: unused passes expire each month.
  • Non-transferable; the member must come too.
  • Blackout dates: Thanksgiving week (Mon-Sun) and 18 December to 1 January.

Cancelling or pausing a membership

  • Memberships can't be paused or put on hold for any reason.
  • Notice to cancel a monthly membership: [15 or 30 days? The site says "cancel anytime" and 15 days; welcome emails say 30: Tyler]
  • Annual paid memberships are non-refundable.
  • Re-joining is at the prices offered at the time.
