Peak Golf

A TrackMan indoor golf simulator upstairs by the private courts, with a lounge of couches and tables for your group to watch. Order food and drinks from Whistle Pig and Joey's Pizza during your session. Bring your own clubs or use Peak's.

$50 an hour: Mon-Fri 7am-9pm, Sat-Sun 7am-8pm. Book a tee time in CourtReserve, just like a court.

Personal training

Peak Fitness

Professional personal training in the private gym inside Peak, with ISSA-certified trainer Alex Sistar: strength, mobility and injury prevention, conditioning, and pickleball-specific performance.

30- or 60-minute sessions in packages of 5 or 10, from $125 for five 30-minute sessions. Partner, online and hybrid options too. Book at least 24 hours ahead in CourtReserve, once you have a package.

The Ernie

A supportive weekly fitness class for adults 55 and over: build strength, improve balance and move with more confidence, on and off the court.

To join, ask at the front counter to be added to The Ernie Fitness Group. Then sign up for sessions in CourtReserve at no charge; the monthly program billing is handled separately, based on your membership. [The Ernie's monthly price by membership type: Peak]

Pro shop

The Paddle Demo Program gives you 30 days to test demo paddles at Peak. Buy a paddle by the end of your trial and your demo fee is refunded. [demo fee: Peak]

In the shop: Komodo paddles and the Komodo Bio Ball, Montis pickleball shoes, and Dingk Sports paddles and gear.

Amenities
