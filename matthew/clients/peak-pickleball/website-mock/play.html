Leagues & tournaments

Play for something

Leagues and ladders put you with the same group each week, so you build team spirit and see your skills grow over a season.

Peak Premier League (PPL)

Peak's elite coed league for high-level players: drafted teams, weekly matchups, standings, stats and playoffs, on Monday or Tuesday nights. New players earn a place through the PPL Combine, a skills evaluation in front of the league's general managers.
