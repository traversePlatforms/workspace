Questions

Before your first class

I've never played. Is that OK?

Yes. The class is for complete beginners, and no experience is needed.

Do I need my own paddle?

No. We have a whole bucket of loaner paddles, and every court is stocked with balls provided by our sponsor Selkirk.

Can I bring a friend or my kids?

Yes, all ages are welcome. Each person books their own spot on the class list. [minimum age, if any: Peak]

Is it really free?

Yes, the Learn to Play class is free. After it, Peak Pickleball University classes are $25 each, and Guided Open Play is free.

Where do I go when I get there?

Peak is at 1730 Briargate Blvd, connected to Chapel Hills Mall between Dillard's and American Freight. Come into the parking lot from Jamboree Drive and use the entrance from the parking lot. There is no entrance from inside the mall.

What if I need to cancel?

Classes need to be cancelled at least 24 hours before the start, in your CourtReserve account. [any fee for a late cancel on the free class: Tyler]
