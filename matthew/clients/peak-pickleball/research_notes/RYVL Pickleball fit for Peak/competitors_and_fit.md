# RYVL Pickleball: competitors and fit for Peak Pickleball

Researched 25 Nov 2026. I fetched each source unless it is marked "(search summary only)". Those came from search-result summaries I did not open, so treat them as lower confidence. Most pricing and scale figures are vendor-stated.

## 1. What category is RYVL in?

### Takeaway
RYVL is a hybrid. It is a competitive player app (challenge ladder, leagues, its own rating shown beside DUPR, live scoring, city leaderboards, "Crews") with a club tier on top ("RYVL Compete" and "Club OS"). The club tier claims court booking, dues and check-in, so it overlaps CourtReserve as well as Swish and Pickleheads. RYVL looks local to Colorado Springs, and its site already lists the Golden Ring Championship 2027.

### Cited Findings
- RYVL lists a Challenge Ladder, a "Gauntlet" event series, leagues with standings, sub-finding and a season race, a "RYVL Rating" shown beside DUPR, live scoring on court, city and regional leaderboards, "Crews", open-game finding and rec nights. Its tagline is "Your whole pickleball life. Organized." — [RYVL homepage](https://ryvlpickleball.com/) (fetched 25 Nov 2026)
- Club tools are "RYVL Compete" and "Club OS", which claim court booking, member dues, check-in and live TV standings boards. The site says RYVL works "alongside your booking software" and never names CourtReserve. — [RYVL homepage](https://ryvlpickleball.com/)
- Pricing: players get a free tier or RYVL+ at $7.99/mo, "currently free for a limited time". Clubs pay "from $99/mo per location" with a 30-day free pilot. — [RYVL homepage](https://ryvlpickleball.com/)
- The site lists the Golden Ring Championship (15–17 Jan 2027), a "Winter Crazy Nights" 8-week league starting 8 Dec in Colorado Springs, and a Nicaragua trip (5–11 Dec). Copyright 2026. — [RYVL homepage](https://ryvlpickleball.com/)
- The site gives no user, club or game counts. — [RYVL homepage](https://ryvlpickleball.com/)
- A general web search for "RYVL pickleball app" (25 Nov 2026) found no app-store listing or press coverage. — search result, no RYVL hits

### Inferences
- RYVL already shows Peak's flagship event (Golden Ring) and a local league. It may already have a relationship with Peak or its event staff. Nneka should ask on Monday what that relationship is.
- "Club OS" (booking, dues, check-in) competes head-on with CourtReserve, which Peak already pays for. The realistic fit is RYVL as the competition and engagement layer only.
- With no scale data and no app-store footprint, RYVL looks early-stage. Ask about the user count, the team, how long it has run, and data ownership and export.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whether RYVL syncs with CourtReserve (members, registrations, payments) or submits scores to DUPR, as opposed to only showing DUPR beside its own rating.
- I could not find RYVL's app-store listing, its company entity, or its founding date.

## 2. Closest competitors (dated)

### Takeaway
The main alternatives either plug into CourtReserve already (Swish, Pickleheads Ultra, DUPR sync) or are booking marketplaces Peak doesn't need (Playtomic, Playbypoint). DUPR's own club tools are free. On the evidence, RYVL's distinct offer is a branded local competitive scene: its own rating, a city leaderboard, a season race and TV boards. It is not the league or ladder engine itself.

### Cited Findings
**Swish (league and event runner, integrated with CourtReserve)**
- Integration dated 25 Apr 2025 (available from 24 Apr 2025). It syncs CourtReserve registrants into Swish, and Swish handles scheduling, court assignments, live scores and player check-in on their own phones. Formats: rotating and set-partner round robins, ladder leagues, King of the Court, team formats. Tournaments and brackets don't sync yet. — [CourtReserve: Swish](https://courtreserve.com/?p=12718)
- Cost: about $25/mo add-on on CourtReserve Start/Grow, included on Scale/Enterprise, plus a separate Swish account (Pro Club plan; the webinar cites $125 and $85 options). Legacy-priced CourtReserve customers must move to a tiered plan. — [CourtReserve: Swish](https://courtreserve.com/?p=12718)
- DUPR: player names and DUPR IDs linked in CourtReserve sync into Swish, and results go to DUPR when a game is completed. Partner DUPR IDs from custom fields don't sync. — [CourtReserve: Swish](https://courtreserve.com/?p=12718)

**Pickleheads (Ultra, for facilities)**
- CourtReserve integration launched 28 Jul 2026. One dropdown links a recurring CourtReserve event series to a Pickleheads league, standings update in real time, changes sync automatically, and players see their court, matchups and score entry on their phones. Automated check-in and two-way booking are "still in development". The closed beta had 10 facilities. — [Pickleheads launch post](https://www.pickleheads.com/blog/pickleheads-courtreserve-integration-launch)
- Scale (self-reported, Jul 2026): 1M+ monthly players, 24,000+ locations, 720,000+ registered players; 12 league and event formats and 20 round-robin formats. Founding facilities get a 30-day free Ultra trial. — [Pickleheads launch post](https://www.pickleheads.com/blog/pickleheads-courtreserve-integration-launch)
- Ladders move players up or down a step after each round. — [Pickleheads leagues](https://www.pickleheads.com/leagues) (search summary only)
- DUPR: staff can send scores to DUPR in one click at session end, and can add DUPR IDs later. — [Pickleheads launch post](https://www.pickleheads.com/blog/pickleheads-courtreserve-integration-launch)
- Pricing page lists a $75 facility-integration add-on. Unverified: the page returned 403 when fetched. — [Pickleheads pricing](https://www.pickleheads.com/pricing) (search summary only)

**DUPR (rating plus free club tools)**
- 8,500+ clubs use DUPR to organise programming "at no cost". Clubs get a member roster, club leaderboard and club feed in the DUPR app, and club matches are auto-validated. Undated page. — [DUPR clubs](https://dupr.com/clubs) (search summary only)
- About 2 million players in 190+ countries (DUPR, undated Main Court post). — [DUPR / Main Court](https://www.dupr.com/post/main-court-and-dupr-launch-full-integration-bringing-rated-round-robin-play-to-main-court-community) (search summary only)
- A 8 Jul 2026 post cites "DUPR's 14,000 global clubs". This conflicts with the 8,500 figure above; the gap is probably dating or definition. — [DUPR / TeamReach](https://www.dupr.com/post/dupr-and-teamreach-announce-strategic-partnership-to-power-player-ratings-and-engagement-across-one-of-pickleballs-largest-communities)
- DUPR's CourtReserve sync was announced 12 Sep 2023. Ratings sync into club accounts, and clubs can run events and leagues by DUPR range. — [DUPR / CourtReserve](https://www.dupr.com/post/courtreserve-transforms-pickleball-experience-with-strategic-dupr-partnership)

**TeamReach (team and club messaging)**
- 8 Jul 2026: three-year deal making DUPR TeamReach's exclusive rating. It covers rating-based open play and tournaments, result submission and leaderboards. TeamReach claims "millions of clubs and teams", with "nearly a quarter" of users in pickleball. — [DUPR / TeamReach](https://www.dupr.com/post/dupr-and-teamreach-announce-strategic-partnership-to-power-player-ratings-and-engagement-across-one-of-pickleballs-largest-communities)

**Playtomic (booking marketplace and open matches)**
- 12 Mar 2026: DUPR ratings embedded for level-based open matches. Playtomic claims 4.7M+ players, 6,500+ partner clubs and 66+ countries. — [DUPR / Playtomic](https://www.dupr.com/post/playtomic-partners-with-dupr-to-power-the-next-era-of-community-driven-pickleball)
- A comparison article describes it as a marketplace across 18,000+ padel and pickleball courts that "doesn't handle club finances or rosters". Pricing is commission-based. — [ThePickleHub, updated 15 Jun 2026](https://www.thepicklehub.net/blog/pickleball-club-management-software-comparison)

**Playbypoint (club management with in-app matchmaking)**
- Claims "more than 1,000 clubs across padel, tennis, and pickleball". Its "Game Match" lets players post open games by skill range. Vendor-supplied article dated 16 Apr 2026. — [Florida Tennis / Playbypoint](https://floridatennis.com/blogs/pickleball-news/the-club-retention-problem-playbypoint-bets-its-about-connection-not-price)
- Pricing page shows add-ons (Priority Access +$49.99, TV Screen +$29.99) and "No training or setup costs". Base price not captured. — [Playbypoint pricing](https://playbypoint.com/pricing) (search summary only)

**Other ladder and league tools (search summary only, vendor prices, Oct 2026 or undated)**
- Sports Carnival: $199/mo Growth, $499 Marquee, per location; its ladders post to DUPR. — [Sports Carnival](https://sportscarnival.com/industries/pickleball-software)
- Picklebeast: free tier, then $299, $650 and $975 per month. — [Picklebeast pricing](https://www.picklebeastpickleball.com/pricing)
- PickleLeagues: free for organisers, with a 10% fee on paid registrations capped at $15 (CAD). — [PickleLeagues pricing](https://pickleleague.pro/pricing)
- Pickle Pathway: from $39/mo. — [Pickle Pathway](https://www.picklepathway.com/)
- PlayTime Scheduler: free or freemium, drop-in and open play only, "not a true ladder tool". — [Weekly Pickleball comparison](https://www.weekly-pickleball.com/best-pickleball-league-apps); [ThePickleHub](https://www.thepicklehub.net/blog/pickleball-club-management-software-comparison)

**The Picklr (franchise app)**
- The official Picklr app (PBC Labs) does booking, live chat and club notifications. Picklr runs "DUPR Play" leagues, including a Super DUPR Ladder League, a weekly doubles ladder. — [Picklr app listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1663342732); [Picklr DUPR Play](https://thepicklr.com/leagues-and-socials/dupr/) (search summary only)

### Inferences
- For Peak, the closest like-for-like comparisons with RYVL are Swish and Pickleheads Ultra. Both have documented CourtReserve integrations, so registrations and payments stay in CourtReserve. RYVL documents none.
- RYVL's $99/mo per location is below Swish (about $25 + $85–125/mo), but only if Peak buys Compete without Club OS.

### Gaps
- I could not verify Global Ladder, Ladderly, Rankedin, PickleConnect or pickleballtournaments.com/Pickleball Brackets within the time I had.
- I could not get current Pickleheads Ultra or Playbypoint base prices; the pages were blocked or only partly captured.

## 3. What CourtReserve already gives Peak that overlaps RYVL

### Takeaway
CourtReserve already covers most of RYVL's club-side list: leagues and ladders, rankings, round robins, player match-maker, member app, alerts and SMS, DUPR sync, plus Swish and Pickleheads plug-ins. RYVL's non-overlapping pieces are its own rating, city and regional leaderboards, live TV standings boards, "Crews" and the season-race layer.

### Cited Findings
- "Leagues & Ladders: Automate match scheduling, track scores and standings, and support team-based or individual play." "Ladders & Rankings: Track and display member rankings, match outcomes, and player progression." — [CourtReserve features](https://courtreserve.com/features/)
- "Events & Programs" covers round robins, socials and drop-ins. "Player Match-Maker" helps members find partners "based on availability, skill level, and preferences." — [CourtReserve features](https://courtreserve.com/features/)
- The free CourtReserve app is co-branded "Powered by CourtReserve", and a fully branded app is available. Also listed: alerts and messaging by SMS or in-app, automated reminders, and announcements and news feeds. — [CourtReserve features](https://courtreserve.com/features/)
- "Player Ratings: syncing with rating systems like DUPR, WTN, and WPR." — [CourtReserve features](https://courtreserve.com/features/)
- Pricing (fetched 25 Nov 2026), monthly / billed annually: Launch $199 / $159, Advance $369 / $329 (includes "Flex leagues"), Momentum $549 / $499 ("Branded mobile app", "All integrations included"). The Player Match-Maker appears on all tiers and DUPR sync is a $25/mo integration. The page is inconsistent on which tiers get the app. — [CourtReserve pricing](https://courtreserve.com/pricing)
- Many clubs ship CourtReserve-branded apps that combine booking with league, round-robin and ladder sign-up (e.g. Pickleball Backyard, Rally House, LevelUp). — [Pickleball Backyard app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6748526634); [Rally House app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/rally-house-pickleball-club/id6741866554); [LevelUp app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6743761858) (search summary only)

### Inferences

### Gaps

## 5. Benefits and pitfalls of adding a separate player-engagement app

### Takeaway
I found no independent, dated owner reports, forum threads or case studies on adding a second player app; the searches returned only app-store listings. What I did find is vendor material. It points one way: vendors sell integration and one login because fragmentation is the known cost, and the benefit claims (retention, extra fill, staff hours saved) are self-reported.

### Cited Findings
- Pitfall, implied: club tools "solve different problems" and are "sometimes used together". Most platforms are weak at tournament brackets, so clubs add a second app. A heavy platform can import a workflow members won't use, because coordination often happens in Facebook or other chat apps. — [ThePickleHub, updated 15 Jun 2026](https://www.thepicklehub.net/blog/pickleball-club-management-software-comparison)
- CourtReserve markets single sign-on (MembersFirst) to cut login confusion and lost-password requests. This is vendor evidence that duplicate logins cause support load. — [CourtReserve integrations guide](https://courtreserve.com/tennis-pickleball-software-integrations/) (search summary only)
- Swish's pitch is that players "don't need a separate CourtReserve login" and that registrants sync automatically, so there is no re-entry. — [CourtReserve: Swish](https://courtreserve.com/?p=12718)
- Benefit claim: Pickleheads says its formats can fit more than four players per court, "up to 50% more paying members" in prime time. A beta customer estimated saving 8+ staff hours a week. Both are anecdotal or vendor claims. — [Pickleheads launch post](https://www.pickleheads.com/blog/pickleheads-courtreserve-integration-launch)
- Retention claim: Playbypoint says "more than half of new players don't return after their first booking" and that easy game-finding improves return rates. The article cites no method or sample and is marked "Courtesy of Playbypoint". — [Florida Tennis, 16 Apr 2026](https://floridatennis.com/blogs/pickleball-news/the-club-retention-problem-playbypoint-bets-its-about-connection-not-price)

### Inferences
- Questions for RYVL, framed by the documented pitfalls:
  1. Does it read CourtReserve registrations, or do members register twice?
  2. Do payments stay in CourtReserve? RYVL's "member dues" would fragment payments.
  3. Does it submit scores to DUPR, or only show DUPR? Peak runs DUPR-rated leagues.
  4. Who owns the member data, and can Peak export it? Peak also uses Patch Retention for email.
  5. What is the active-user count after the RYVL+ free period ends?
- If RYVL has a sponsorship or leaderboard-advertising angle, it could earn Peak money. I found no RYVL evidence of this, so ask.

### Gaps
- I found no independent case studies, Reddit or owner-forum threads, or industry surveys (The Dink, USA Pickleball facility resources, Sports Facilities Companies) on adoption rates or two-app confusion, despite three targeted searches.
- I found no data on sponsorship revenue from engagement apps.
