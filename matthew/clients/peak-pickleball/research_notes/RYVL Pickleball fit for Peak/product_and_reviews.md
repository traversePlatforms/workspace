# RYVL Pickleball: product, club offer, app-store traction and reviews

All pages and listings below were read on 25 Nov 2026 (Mountain time) unless a different date is shown. RYVL's own pages are vendor claims. No independent press, review sites or social accounts could be found to check them (see Gaps).

## What the app does for players

### Takeaway
RYVL is a free player app (iOS, Android, web) that keeps one record of every game a player plays: rec sessions, crews, leagues, a city Challenge Ladder and tournaments. It shows its own "RYVL Rating" beside DUPR and ranks players on city boards. A paid tier, RYVL+ ($7.99/month), is free to everyone during launch. Feature descriptions, all as of 25 Nov 2026:
- **Matchmaking:** open games, rec sessions, find-a-sub.
- **Competition:** ladders, leagues, tournaments, the Pro-Am Gauntlet.
- **Ratings, rankings, stats:** RYVL Rating, DUPR shown and optionally sent, city and regional leaderboards, head-to-heads, partner chemistry.
- **Social:** crews, chat, messaging.
- **Rewards:** season badge, year-end awards, Gauntlet discounts.
- **Payments:** Stripe, on the website.
- **Not found:** no video feature on any page.

### Cited Findings

Player features listed: Record (every score, partner and opponent), RYVL Rating beside DUPR, leagues, ladders, tournaments and rec nights all feeding one record, season race, city rank, city and regional leaderboards, find a game, find a sub, crews, partner chemistry, live scoring, "Your week." — [ryvlpickleball.com](https://ryvlpickleball.com/)

  - RYVL's own "national pro-am pickleball circuit." It pairs amateurs with the best players in their city in short events with guaranteed matches, aimed at players rated about 3.5 to 5.5.

### Inferences
- RYVL's core idea is a portable player record and a city ranking, not court booking. Players use it to organise their own play and to compete across venues, which can pull them toward events outside the home club.
- The two consumer revenue streams are RYVL+ (not charging yet) and RYVL-run Gauntlet entry fees. The Gauntlet is RYVL's own event product, so it could compete with a club's tournaments or complement them.

### Gaps
- How the RYVL Rating is calculated is not published.
- No video, rewards beyond badges and discounts, or paddle and retail tie-ins were found.

## What it does for clubs, facilities and organizers

### Takeaway
RYVL sells two club plans at clubs.ryvlpickleball.com.
- **Compete** ($99 per location per month): runs rec nights, leagues and tournaments alongside existing booking software such as CourtReserve.
- **Club OS** ($299 per location per month plus 1% of payments): a full club system with court booking, memberships, dues, check-in, lessons, waivers and payments. It is a direct CourtReserve replacement and offers CourtReserve import.

Both start with a free 30-day pilot. Prices as of 25 Nov 2026.

### Cited Findings
- **The two modes:** RYVL Club can run "alongside existing booking software (Compete) or run the whole club (Club OS)." Tagline: "The night your members never skip." — [clubs.ryvlpickleball.com](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/)
- **Compete pricing:** $99/mo per location, or $990/yr. Compete Enterprise (10+ locations) is up to $199/mo per location, billed yearly, with custom work. It covers:
  - rec nights and leagues (sign-ups, matchups, scores, standings)
  - club board
  - season race and scorecard
  - networks with one board across several locations — [clubs.ryvlpickleball.com](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/)
- **Club OS pricing:** $299/mo per location plus 1% on payments, or $2,990/yr. It adds:
  - court booking with member rates and booking windows
  - members, plans, dues and front-desk check-in
  - lessons, clinics, waivers and guest fees
  - "switch-over imports" from **CourtReserve, PodPlay or a spreadsheet**
  - Payments go to the club's own Stripe account, and RYVL takes 1% of member payments made through it. — [clubs.ryvlpickleball.com](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/)
- **Running a night:**
  - check-in, court draws, rounds and live standings
  - players enter scores from their phones
  - auto-rolling matchups and a self-filling waitlist
  - formats: rec nights, mixers, round robins, Moneyball
  - live board on paired club TVs, the front-desk iPad and the member app — [clubs.ryvlpickleball.com](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/)
- **Admin modules:**
  - Insights: active players, visits, suggested next actions
  - Members and desk: plans, dues, waivers, ratings, last visit
  - Courts and schedule
  - Money: collections, past-due balances, payment history
  - Staff: weekly shift schedules for desk, pros and managers
  - Lessons and clinics, managed from the club iPad
  - Leaderboard ranked by RYVL Points — [clubs.ryvlpickleball.com](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/)
- **Team console:** for college club teams, campuses and bases. Covers rosters, eligibility, practice plans, lineups, duals, DreamBreaker and seat ladders. — [ryvlpickleball.com](https://ryvlpickleball.com/)
- **Onboarding:** sign up with an email, then enter the club name and location. Set up courts, a first session, player invitations and scoring, with a sample session available. Players join through the free app with a club code or a QR poster. — [clubs.ryvlpickleball.com](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/)
- **Pilot:** 30 days free, no card, cancel anytime. After that the owner picks a plan or stops, and "players keep their game records either way." Call requests are answered within two business days. A live demo is at demo.ryvlpickleball.com. — [clubs.ryvlpickleball.com](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/)
- **Usage claim:** the club page says "220+ members playing on RYVL" and "800+ rec games tracked." Undated and unverified. — [clubs.ryvlpickleball.com](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/)
- **Not offered:** no sponsorship or ad inventory, and no member email or newsletter tool is described. Messaging exists only as crew and club chat in the player app. — [clubs.ryvlpickleball.com](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/); [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ryvl-pickleball/id6821790245)
- **Data shared with clubs:** "only what they need to run their club or event, such as rosters and results." — [ryvlpickleball.com/privacy](https://ryvlpickleball.com/privacy)

### Inferences
- For a CourtReserve club like Peak, the low-commitment option is Compete: it adds rec, league, ladder and leaderboard features on top of CourtReserve. Club OS is a platform switch, and the CourtReserve import path shows RYVL is aiming at that switch.
- With no CourtReserve sync described, running Compete beside CourtReserve may mean members register in two places. Worth asking on Monday.
- The 1% Club OS fee is on top of Stripe's own processing fee. The site does not say that outright, but payments run through the club's own Stripe account.

### Gaps
- No integration or API with CourtReserve beyond a one-time import is described.
- No SLA, uptime figures, data-export terms for clubs, or contract length beyond monthly or yearly billing were found.
- No testimonials or named Club OS customers. "Springs East" appears only as a sample board in a mockup.

## Partner program

### Takeaway
The /partners page is a venue partner program built around hosting RYVL's Gauntlet events. The venue supplies the courts, a date, a director and its community. RYVL handles registration, seeding, scoring, standings and payments, and pays the venue an operator share. The share is not published and is set in the pilot terms.

### Cited Findings
- **What partners get:**
  - online registration and payments, with caps enforced and walk-ups handled at the desk
  - automatic seeding by DUPR and generated schedules
  - live standings on venue TVs, with per-court QR score entry
  - optional automatic DUPR submission
  - a city hub site with leaderboards, a ladder and event listings
  - a director console for creating and settling events
  - per-event settlement statements showing gross, platform fee and the operator's share
  - for multi-location operators, a regional qualifier with a final at one location — [ryvlpickleball.com/partners](https://ryvlpickleball.com/partners)
- **What partners are asked to do:**
  - provide courts, a date and their community
  - supply a director (staff, or one RYVL helps find)
  - attend a demo session of about 90 minutes with about eight of their people
  - run a 30-day pilot, then choose a plan or stop — [ryvlpickleball.com/partners](https://ryvlpickleball.com/partners)
- **Example figure only:** "72 players × $65 ≈ $4,700 gross entries" for a sold-out City Gauntlet weekend. Not a fee schedule. The operator's share is not stated as a number. — [ryvlpickleball.com/partners](https://ryvlpickleball.com/partners)
- **Who answers:** inquiries go to a named RYVL contact, Nick Hagan, who replies within two business days. The page names Denver and Birmingham as upcoming markets. — [ryvlpickleball.com/partners](https://ryvlpickleball.com/partners)
- **No sponsor offer:** the page mentions no sponsorship or advertising inventory. — [ryvlpickleball.com/partners](https://ryvlpickleball.com/partners)

### Inferences
- The questions to bring Monday: the platform fee and the operator split per event, who owns the player and registration data, and whether Gauntlets at Peak would compete with Peak's own events.

### Gaps
- The revenue-share percentage, the platform fee and the contract terms are not published.

## DUPR versus RYVL's own rating

### Takeaway
RYVL runs its own rating (RYVL Rating, same 2.0–8.0 scale as DUPR) and shows DUPR beside it. Players can connect DUPR to verify their rating and can choose, event by event, to submit results. Rec games never go to DUPR. Partner events seed by DUPR and offer optional automatic DUPR submission.

### Cited Findings
- **Side by side, opt-in:** the RYVL Rating "sits beside your DUPR on the same 2.0 to 8.0 scale"; "Rec games never go to DUPR. Sending results is your call, event by event." — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ryvlpickleball.app)

- **What RYVL shares with DUPR:** only if the player connects DUPR, "to verify your rating and submit your results." — [ryvlpickleball.com/privacy](https://ryvlpickleball.com/privacy)
- **DUPR in partner events:** automatic seeding by DUPR and optional automatic DUPR submission. — [ryvlpickleball.com/partners](https://ryvlpickleball.com/partners)
- **DUPR at Golden Ring 2027:** the event uses DUPR for every division. — [ryvlpickleball.com/events/10](https://ryvlpickleball.com/events/10)

### Inferences
- The connection is probably an official DUPR API or club-partner link, since RYVL can verify ratings and submit results, but no DUPR-side confirmation was found.

### Gaps
- DUPR's own site was not checked for a listed RYVL partnership.
- The RYVL Rating's algorithm is unpublished.
