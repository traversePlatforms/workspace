# RYVL Pickleball (ryvlpickleball.com): company and team profile

Researched 25 Nov 2026 for Nneka's meeting with RYVL on Mon 30 Nov 2026. Business and professional sources only. About 20 tool calls; several registries (OpenCorporates, the Colorado SOS search page) refused automated fetches, so some checks were done through other routes, as noted below.

**Bottom line:** RYVL Pickleball is a very young, thinly documented operation. The domain was registered on 25 Sep 2026. The legal pages are dated 12 Oct 2026. No founder is named anywhere public. There is no press, no funding record and no LinkedIn presence that a search can find. Its own traction claims are small: 220+ members and 800+ rec games. Peak Pickleball itself is the featured organiser on RYVL's homepage. Nothing negative turned up either. The record is simply close to empty, so most of what Nneka needs has to be asked in the meeting.

## Legal entity, incorporation, registration and trademarks

### Takeaway
The site names its operator as "RYVL Sports Inc." and sets Colorado law as governing. No "RYVL Sports Inc." appears in Colorado's business-entity open data, which is current to 21 Nov 2026, so the state and date of incorporation are unverified. A separate, delinquent "RYVL LLC" (Englewood, CO, formed 2022) exists, and its link to the pickleball business is unknown. None of the USPTO "RYVL" filings found belong to RYVL Sports Inc.

### Cited Findings
- The Terms say RYVL Pickleball is provided by **RYVL Sports Inc.** and are "governed by the laws of the State of Colorado, USA". Last updated **12 Oct 2026**. No mailing address is given. The only contact is info@ryvlpickleball.com. — [RYVL Terms](https://ryvlpickleball.com/terms)
- The Privacy Policy says RYVL Pickleball "is run by RYVL Sports Inc." Last updated **12 Oct 2026**. No address is given. The processors it names are Supabase (database and sign-in), Vercel (hosting), Stripe (payments), Postmark (email), Apple and Google push, DUPR (only if a user links an account) and Google Analytics (website only). — [RYVL Privacy](https://ryvlpickleball.com/privacy)
- Every page carries the footer "© 2026 RYVL Sports Inc." — [RYVL home](https://ryvlpickleball.com/), [Support](https://ryvlpickleball.com/support)
- **Domain:** ryvlpickleball.com was registered on **25 Sep 2026** through NameCheap and expires 25 Sep 2027. — [Verisign RDAP record](https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/ryvlpickleball.com), queried 25 Nov 2026
- **Colorado business registry (open data):** a search for names containing "RYVL" returned one record only. It is **RYVL LLC**, entity ID 20221102341, a Colorado domestic LLC formed **26 Jan 2022**, principal city Englewood, CO, status **Delinquent** (named in the record as "Delinquent June 1, 2024"). — [Colorado Information Marketplace, Business Entities dataset](https://data.colorado.gov/resource/4ykn-tg5h.json?$where=upper(entityname)%20like%20%27%25RYVL%25%27)
- The same dataset holds filings up to **21 Nov 2026**, so a Colorado-formed "RYVL Sports Inc." from before that date would normally appear. It does not. — [same dataset, latest records](https://data.colorado.gov/resource/4ykn-tg5h.json?$where=entityformdate%3E%272026-10-01%27&$order=entityformdate%20DESC&$limit=3)
- **USPTO "RYVL" filings (seen through third-party aggregators, not TSDR):**
  - Serial 99535881, filed 8 Dec 2025 by RS Manufacturing LLC (Geneva, IL), for paintball gear and apparel; pending. — [trademarkdir](https://trademarkdir.com/trademark/ryvl-99535881)
  - Serial 99805853, filed 5 May 2026 by **RYVL, Inc.** (Whitefish, MT), for fitness and nutrition services; pending. — [markinton](https://markinton.com/trademark/ryvl-99805853), [trademarkdir owner page](https://trademarkdir.com/owner/ryvl-inc)
  - Serial 97874175, filed 2023 by an individual, for game software; abandoned Aug 2024. — [markinton](https://markinton.com/trademark/ryvl-97874175)
- **SEC EDGAR:** a full-text search for "RYVL" in Form D filings returned **0 results**. — [EDGAR full-text search](https://efts.sec.gov/LATEST/search-index?q=%22RYVL%22&forms=D)

### Inferences
- RYVL Sports Inc. is either incorporated outside Colorado (Delaware is common for an "Inc.") and maybe registered in Colorado as a foreign entity under another name, or it was formed very recently, or it has not been formed. The registry gives no way to tell which.
- "RYVL, Inc." in Montana is a fitness and nutrition trademark applicant. Nothing ties it to the pickleball app, and the name differs ("RYVL, Inc." versus "RYVL Sports Inc."). Treat it as unrelated unless RYVL says otherwise.
- RYVL Pickleball appears to hold no USPTO filing for its name. RS Manufacturing's pending Class 28 sporting-goods application could cause a naming conflict later. That is a minor brand-stability point, not a legal finding.

### Gaps
- No state of incorporation, formation date or good-standing status was found for RYVL Sports Inc. The Delaware and other state registries were not searchable by automated fetch. OpenCorporates returned 403.
- Whether the Englewood "RYVL LLC" (2022, delinquent) is connected to RYVL Sports Inc. is unknown. The registry record gives no officers.
- USPTO details come from aggregators. They are not confirmed on USPTO TSDR.
- **Ask in the meeting:** the legal name and state of incorporation, the formation date, who the officers are, and whether they hold or plan a trademark filing.

## Product, pricing, traction and partnerships

### Takeaway
RYVL's own numbers are small, undated and unaudited: "220+ members" and "800+ rec games tracked". No club customer is named. No tour, DUPR, USA Pickleball or brand partnership appears beyond an optional DUPR account link. Peak Pickleball is the only organisation the site shows publicly.

### Cited Findings
- **Product:** a free player app (game record, crews, leagues, ratings, rankings) and club software. "RYVL+" costs $7.99/mo and is "free for a limited time". — [home](https://ryvlpickleball.com/)
- **Club pricing:**
  - Compete: $99/mo per location or $990/yr. For 10+ locations, up to $199/mo per location billed yearly.
  - Club OS (booking, members, dues, check-in, lessons, waivers, payments): $299/mo per location plus 1% of payments, or $2,990/yr. Payments go to the club's own Stripe account.
  - It can import from CourtReserve, PodPlay or a spreadsheet.
  - 30-day free pilot, no card needed. — [clubs site](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/)
- **Traction (self-reported, undated, as read 25 Nov 2026):** "220+ members" and "800+ rec games tracked". — [Partners](https://ryvlpickleball.com/partners), [clubs](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/)
- **"The Gauntlet":** a recurring competitive session run by RYVL on a facility's courts. Multi-site networks can feed a regional qualifier. "Denver is on the calendar, Birmingham is next", with no dates. An illustrative City Gauntlet weekend shows 72 players at $65, about $4,700 in gross entries. That figure is hypothetical. The operator's share is "set in each pilot agreement". — [Partners](https://ryvlpickleball.com/partners)
- **Peak Pickleball** is named on the homepage as organiser of the "Golden Ring Championship 2027". — [home](https://ryvlpickleball.com/)
- "Springs East" appears only as a label in a product mock-up. It is not a named customer. — [clubs](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/)
- DUPR appears only as an optional account link in the privacy policy, not as a stated partnership. — [Privacy](https://ryvlpickleball.com/privacy)

### Inferences
- Peak may be one of RYVL's first, or most prominent, partners. Nneka should confirm what Peak has already agreed to, if anything, for the Golden Ring Championship listing.
- The "Springs East" mock-up label and the Colorado governing law suggest a Colorado Springs or Front Range focus. That is an inference only.

### Gaps
- No press coverage was found in pickleball media (The Dink, Pickleheads, PickleballCentral, Pickleball Magazine) or in local news. Search results returned nothing on RYVL Pickleball.
- No named club customers, no dated traction and no city count. **Ask** for reference clubs, how many paying locations they have, and what the 220/800 figures cover and since when.
