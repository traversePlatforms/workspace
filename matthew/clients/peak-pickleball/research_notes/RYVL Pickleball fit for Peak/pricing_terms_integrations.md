# RYVL Pickleball: business model, pricing, legal terms and integrations, as they affect a club partner (Peak Pickleball)

Researched 25 Nov 2026 (America/Denver) for Nneka's meeting with RYVL on Mon 30 Nov 2026. All figures come from RYVL's own pages as they read on 25 Nov 2026, unless marked otherwise. Pages read: ryvlpickleball.com (home, /membership, /partners, /terms, /privacy, /support, /gauntlet, /events/10, /clubs) and clubs.ryvlpickleball.com (pricing and FAQ). Third-party coverage is almost nil. Web searches for "RYVL" with pickleball, DUPR, Gauntlet or App Store found no press, no DUPR partner listing and no app-store listing. Nearly everything below is RYVL describing itself and has not been checked independently.

**Most important finding for Monday:** RYVL already has a registration page up for **"Golden Ring Championship 2027"**, labelled **"Club tournament · Run by Peak Pickleball"**, with a RYVL Register button ([ryvlpickleball.com/events/10](https://ryvlpickleball.com/events/10)). It is also the "Next Event / Registration Open" card on [/gauntlet](https://ryvlpickleball.com/gauntlet). Nneka should confirm whether Peak approved this, who will take the entry money, and how it fits with Peak's usual pickleballtournaments.com listing. Details are under the integrations question below.

## 1. How RYVL makes money: player and club pricing

### Takeaway
RYVL has three revenue streams. (1) Player subscription RYVL+, priced at $7.99/mo but free during launch. (2) Per-location club SaaS: Compete at $99/mo, or Club OS at $299/mo plus 1% of payments. (3) Paid competitive events (the "Gauntlet"), where RYVL takes a platform fee and the venue gets a share agreed privately. Club pricing **is** published. The Gauntlet venue split and the Team console price are **not**.

### Cited Findings
**Players**
- Rookie tier: "Free". It covers joining open games in your city, RSVPs and messaging, a profile and W–L record, and city and regional leaderboards. — [RYVL /membership](https://ryvlpickleball.com/membership)
- RYVL+: the homepage lists it at **$7.99/mo**, "currently free for a limited time". The membership page says "RYVL+ is free during launch" and "Every player has RYVL+ right now — there's nothing to buy and nothing to set up." — [RYVL home](https://ryvlpickleball.com/); [RYVL /membership](https://ryvlpickleball.com/membership)
- RYVL+ adds: hosting (free accounts can't host), the full stat suite, **$10 off every Gauntlet entry**, 48-hour priority Gauntlet registration, the Challenge Ladder (free accounts get one partner match a month), and a season badge with year-end awards. — [RYVL home](https://ryvlpickleball.com/); [RYVL /membership](https://ryvlpickleball.com/membership)
- "City Founder" tier: "Coming soon", price not listed. It covers RYVL+ plus a free City Gauntlet entry each season, a Founder badge, a shirt, merch drops and Founders-only events. "Founder spots are limited per city." — [RYVL /membership](https://ryvlpickleball.com/membership)
- The membership page mentions no annual player plan and no in-app purchases. — [RYVL /membership](https://ryvlpickleball.com/membership)

**Clubs and facilities**
- **RYVL Compete: $99/mo per location, or $990/yr** ("two months free"), self-serve. It runs rec nights, leagues, club tournaments, standings and a club board, and "runs beside your current booking software". — [RYVL Clubs](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/)
- **Compete Enterprise (10+ locations): "up to $199/mo per location, billed yearly"**, with custom work. — [RYVL Clubs](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/)
- **RYVL Club OS: "$299/mo per location + 1% on payments", or $2,990/yr.** It includes Compete plus court booking with member rates, members and plans, dues, front-desk check-in, lessons, clinics, waivers, guest fees, staff scheduling and money reporting. — [RYVL Clubs](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/)
- "The plan itself is billed monthly or yearly per location." — [RYVL Clubs FAQ](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/#faq)
- Team console (college club teams, campuses, bases): "Every player gets RYVL+ seats". No price is published. — [RYVL home](https://ryvlpickleball.com/)
- Pilot: "30 days free · no card · cancel anytime". After 30 days, "You choose a plan in your console, or you stop… nothing is charged unless you pick a plan." — [RYVL Clubs](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/); [RYVL Clubs FAQ](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/#faq)

**Gauntlet venue partner program** (separate from the SaaS plans)
- It is aimed at facilities that host a recurring "Gauntlet" competitive event. Partners bring "Courts and a date" and their community. RYVL handles "registration and payments, seeding and scheduling, live boards and QR scoring, optional DUPR submission, a city hub site, and a director console." "The brand, format, points engine and payment rails stay central." — [RYVL /partners](https://ryvlpickleball.com/partners)
- Their example of the money involved: "72 players × $65 ≈ $4,700 gross entries" for a sold-out City Gauntlet weekend. Each event settles as "gross · platform fee · your share." — [RYVL /partners](https://ryvlpickleball.com/partners)
- **The venue's share is not published:** "the operator share is set in your pilot terms, in writing, before the first session." — [RYVL /partners](https://ryvlpickleball.com/partners)
- Offered before signing: a demo session ("Eight of [your] people, real accounts, and a live game on [your] courts, about 90 minutes, no deck") and a "Free for 30 days, no card" pilot. — [RYVL /partners](https://ryvlpickleball.com/partners)
- Partners with more than one site can run a regional qualifier, with one location hosting the final. — [RYVL /partners](https://ryvlpickleball.com/partners)
- RYVL has already run Gauntlets in Colorado Springs at another venue: "The Colorado Springs Pilot · Jul 25–26 · Springs Pickleball East · SOLD OUT — 88 entries", and "Springs Gauntlet 2" on Sep 26–27, 2026 (marked Complete). Denver "Gauntlet No. 1" is listed for Aug 22 with "registration opening soon". The year isn't shown, and an August date is past as of 25 Nov 2026, so the listing may be stale. "16 names triggers a pilot" in a new city. — [RYVL /gauntlet](https://ryvlpickleball.com/gauntlet)
- No Gauntlet entry fee, prize money or platform fee is published on /gauntlet. The $65 entry appears only as the /partners example. — [RYVL /gauntlet](https://ryvlpickleball.com/gauntlet); [RYVL /partners](https://ryvlpickleball.com/partners)
- Sponsorship or advertising revenue and how it is split: not mentioned on any page read. — [RYVL /partners](https://ryvlpickleball.com/partners)

### Inferences
- For an ~800-member, single-location club like Peak, the published list price is $99/mo ($990/yr) for Compete alongside CourtReserve, or $299/mo ($2,990/yr) plus 1% of everything members pay through it if Club OS replaced CourtReserve.
- RYVL+ being free "during launch" means the $7.99 price has not started. Peak members asked to use RYVL would be using a free app that may later charge for hosting, stats and Gauntlet discounts.
- The Gauntlet is RYVL's own branded event product. A venue that hosts one runs a RYVL event on its courts and does not own the brand, the format or the payment rails.

- City Founder pricing, and when RYVL+ stops being free.
- Whether there is any sponsorship or advertising revenue and how it is split with the venue.
- Whether Compete can be priced per court or per member instead of per location (only per-location is published).

## 2. Payments: processor, fees, merchant of record, payouts

### Takeaway
Payments run on Stripe. On Club OS, members pay **into the club's own Stripe account** and RYVL takes 1% on top. For Gauntlet and event entries, players pay "on our website through Stripe" and RYVL settles the venue's share after each event, so RYVL appears to hold the money for those. Stripe's own fees, the merchant of record, Stripe Connect details and payout timing are **not published**.

### Cited Findings
- "On Club OS, members pay you through your own Stripe account: court time, dues, lessons, guest fees. The money lands with you; RYVL's 1% is taken from those payments." — [RYVL Clubs FAQ](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/#faq)
- Club OS payments go "straight to your own Stripe" account. No other processor is named. — [RYVL home](https://ryvlpickleball.com/)
- Terms of Use: event entries and club dues are paid through Stripe on the website. Fees, merchant-of-record status and payout terms are not stated. — [RYVL Terms (updated 12 Oct 2026)](https://ryvlpickleball.com/terms)
- Support page: "Entries are paid on our website through Stripe." "Refund rules are shown on each event before you pay." — [RYVL Support](https://ryvlpickleball.com/support)
- Default refund rule in the Terms: "unless an event says otherwise, entries are refundable up to the registration deadline." — [RYVL Terms](https://ryvlpickleball.com/terms)
- For Gauntlets, "payment rails stay central" with RYVL, and each event settles as "gross · platform fee · your share". — [RYVL /partners](https://ryvlpickleball.com/partners)
- Privacy Policy: RYVL keeps a Stripe customer ID and purchase history. "we never see or store your card number." — [RYVL Privacy (updated 12 Oct 2026)](https://ryvlpickleball.com/privacy)
- The Golden Ring 2027 page shows "Entry fee: TBD" and "Payment opens soon". Each partner pays their own entry. On refunds it says: "Refunds, withdrawals and anything else about the day: ask Peak Pickleball." — [RYVL /events/10](https://ryvlpickleball.com/events/10)

### Inferences
- Club OS appears to use a connected-account model, where the club is the Stripe account holder and RYVL takes an application fee. That is an inference from "your own Stripe" plus "1% taken from those payments". RYVL does not say Stripe Connect.
- For RYVL-hosted event entries, including the Golden Ring 2027 page, RYVL seems to collect the money and pay the organiser out later. The fee and payout timing are unknown. Meanwhile the page sends refund questions to Peak, so Peak would carry the customer-service side without having published the policy.

### Gaps (ask on Monday)
- Who is merchant of record for event entries (RYVL Sports Inc. or the club), and whose name appears on the card statement.
- RYVL's fee on event entries. Whether Stripe's processing fees (roughly 2.9% + 30¢ on standard US cards, per Stripe's public pricing; not checked in this session) come on top of the 1%, and who pays them.
- Payout timing after an event. Who absorbs chargebacks and refunds.
- Whether Compete (not only Club OS) can take league or event fees into the club's own Stripe.
- Sales-tax handling. Whether a 1099-K or other tax reporting goes to the club.

## 3. Terms of Service and Privacy Policy

### Takeaway
RYVL has one consumer-style Terms of Use and one Privacy Policy, **both last updated 12 Oct 2026**, from **RYVL Sports Inc.** under **Colorado law**. They are short and player-facing. **There is no club, organizer or partner agreement published.** The documents say nothing about who owns club or member data, nothing about RYVL's role toward the club's data (processor or controller), and nothing about indemnity or arbitration. RYVL says it does not sell personal data or use it for advertising. Players can export and delete their own records.

### Cited Findings
**Terms of Use**, "Last updated October 12, 2026" — [RYVL Terms](https://ryvlpickleball.com/terms)
- Legal entity: **RYVL Sports Inc.** No mailing address is given. Contact is info@ryvlpickleball.com. The support footer confirms "© 2026 RYVL Sports Inc." — [RYVL Support](https://ryvlpickleball.com/support)
- Governing law: **Colorado, USA**. No venue or court is named.
- **Arbitration and class-action waiver: none in the Terms** (not addressed).
- Liability cap: "our total liability for any claim is limited to the amount you paid us in the 12 months before it". Consequential losses are excluded to the fullest extent the law allows.
- **Indemnity: none** (not addressed).
- Minors: users must be at least 13. "If you're under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to these terms for you."
- Subscriptions: RYVL+ is free during launch. If pricing changes, users are told first, and "you won't be charged without agreeing." The Terms give no auto-renewal language.
- Content: "You own what you post." Users grant RYVL permission to store, display and share it within the product.
- Clubs: "Venues, clubs and organizers may set their own rules" and may require their own waivers. Play is at the user's own risk.
- Termination: users may stop at any time and delete their account in Me → Settings. RYVL "may suspend or close accounts that break these terms or put others at risk." Results remain in other players' records after deletion.
- Apple is named a third-party beneficiary for app users.

**Privacy Policy**, "Last updated October 12, 2026" — [RYVL Privacy](https://ryvlpickleball.com/privacy)
- Data collected: name, email and password (held by the sign-in provider); optionally phone and photo. City, home club, skill level, and optionally DUPR ID and rating. Sessions, leagues, events, games, scores, and calculated ratings and standings. Direct messages, crew chat and reports. Stripe customer ID and purchase history. Push token. In-app screen usage linked to the account (RYVL says it does not record what you type or where you are). IP address and app version logs. Location, health and contacts data are not mentioned.
- Named service providers: Supabase and Vercel (database, sign-in, hosting), Stripe (payments), Postmark (email), Apple and Google push, DUPR "only if you connect your account", and Google Analytics on the website only.
- "We don't sell your personal information and we don't use it for advertising." There is no CCPA- or Colorado Privacy Act-specific section.
- Marketing: the policy covers only emails and notifications the user requests, which can be turned off in Settings. It does not address marketing to a club's members.
- Retention: account information is kept while the account is open. Payment records are kept as long as tax law requires. Logs and account-linked usage data are kept up to 90 days. Backups are overwritten within 30 days of deletion.
- Export and deletion: "Me → Settings → Download your record" in CSV or JSON. Delete account in Settings or by email. Deleted players remain in others' results as "Deleted player."
- Children: not directed to under-13s, and RYVL does not knowingly collect their data.
- Clubs: "Club and event organizers see what they need to run their club or event" (rosters, results). Nothing on who owns club data, and nothing on RYVL acting as processor or controller.

**Club-facing statements on data** (marketing copy, not contract)
- "Their games, rating and record are theirs and follow them, which is why they keep playing." "Your members' game records stay theirs either way" after a pilot ends. — [RYVL Clubs FAQ](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/#faq)

### Inferences
- Under RYVL's model, each player holds their own record and RYVL holds the platform. The club gets operating visibility ("what they need"), not documented ownership. If Peak runs leagues or events on RYVL, the play history sits in player-owned RYVL accounts that move with the player, including to a competing club on RYVL.
- No arbitration clause and Colorado law are both relatively friendly to a Colorado club. But these are player terms; a club contract could say something different, and none is published.
- Because a player under 18 needs a guardian's consent, Peak's junior programs would need guardian sign-off on RYVL as well as Peak's own waiver.

### Gaps (ask on Monday)
- **Is there a club or organizer agreement or a DPA?** None is published. Ask for it before any pilot.
- Who owns the club's roster, member contact details and event data. Can Peak export all of it (not just each player's own record) at any time and when it leaves?
- Can RYVL email, push or market to Peak's members (Gauntlets, RYVL+, City Founder, other clubs' events)? Can Peak opt out?
- Indemnity and insurance for RYVL-run events on Peak's courts. Whose waiver applies at a Gauntlet?
- Club-side liability cap, termination notice and auto-renewal on annual plans.
- RYVL Sports Inc.'s state of incorporation and address. Neither is published; a Colorado Secretary of State lookup was not done in this session.

## 4. Integrations: CourtReserve, DUPR, others; any public API

### Takeaway
RYVL does **not** advertise a live CourtReserve integration. Compete "runs beside" booking software, and Club OS **imports from** CourtReserve, PodPlay or a spreadsheet in order to replace them. DUPR shows up as an optional account link and an optional results submission. **Neither DUPR nor anyone independent lists RYVL as an official DUPR partner.** No public API, calendar sync, PlayByPoint, Pickleheads, Swish or Patch integration is mentioned. RYVL already lists a Peak event on its own site.

### Cited Findings
- "Do I have to leave my booking software? No. Compete runs beside it: your courts stay booked where they are, and RYVL runs the nights, leagues and scores. If you later want one system, Club OS replaces it and imports from CourtReserve, PodPlay or a spreadsheet." — [RYVL Clubs FAQ](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/#faq)
- On the club pricing page: PlayByPoint, DUPR, calendars and an API are not mentioned. — [RYVL Clubs](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/)
- Players need RYVL's own app: "the free RYVL Pickleball app, on iPhone and on Android, or the web. They join your club with its code or the QR poster in a minute." — [RYVL Clubs FAQ](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/#faq)
- DUPR: player profiles show the RYVL Rating "beside" DUPR. Players can optionally link a DUPR ID. DUPR receives data "only if you connect your account". The Gauntlet offers "optional DUPR submission" and "your DUPR only moves if you say so." — [RYVL home](https://ryvlpickleball.com/); [RYVL Privacy](https://ryvlpickleball.com/privacy); [RYVL /partners](https://ryvlpickleball.com/partners); [RYVL /gauntlet](https://ryvlpickleball.com/gauntlet)
- A web search for RYVL and DUPR returned only DUPR's other partnerships (Playtomic, Pickleball Superstore, PPA/UPA) and nothing about RYVL. — [DUPR news (Pickleball Superstore post)](https://www.dupr.com/post/dupr-and-pickleball-superstore-align-to-support-clubs-organizers-and-collegiate-programs-with-virtual-proshop-offerings-and-super-affiliate-program)
- RYVL uses its own rating, the "RYVL Rating", alongside DUPR. — [RYVL home](https://ryvlpickleball.com/)
- No App Store or Google Play listing for RYVL turned up in searches. The site says the app is on iPhone and Android, but no store links were found on the pages read. — [RYVL Clubs FAQ](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/#faq)
- **Existing Peak listing on RYVL:** "Golden Ring Championship 2027" (3rd annual), January 15–17, 2027, at Peak Pickleball, Colorado Springs ("24 indoor, climate-controlled courts"). It reads "Club tournament · Run by Peak Pickleball". Registration is through RYVL and closes "Tue, Jan 12, 5:00 PM". Entry fee "TBD", "Payment opens soon". DUPR bands are used for divisions, and "unrated players play Up to 3.49". Caps are 12 per singles division and 12 teams per doubles or mixed division. The page does not mention pickleballtournaments.com. It also contradicts itself on playoff advancement (top 2 per pool in one section, top 4 to a medal bracket in the "From Peak Pickleball" section). — [RYVL /events/10](https://ryvlpickleball.com/events/10); [RYVL /gauntlet](https://ryvlpickleball.com/gauntlet)
- Earlier Golden Ring editions were listed on pickleballtournaments.com. Search snippets show Jan 30–Feb 1, 2026 and Feb 6–8, 2026 (2nd annual) at Peak Pickleball. These were seen in search results only and the pages were not opened. — [pickleballtournaments.com Golden Ring](https://pickleballtournaments.com/tournaments/golden-ring-national-championship); [pickleballtournaments.com Golden Ring (alt)](https://pickleballtournaments.com/tournaments/golden-ring-pickleball-championship)
- RYVL's /clubs directory showed "No clubs in your city on RYVL yet" (the city selector did not resolve), with "Summit Pickleball Club" as a demo. Peak was not listed. — [RYVL /clubs](https://ryvlpickleball.com/clubs)

### Inferences
- With CourtReserve, Peak would be running two systems. Bookings, memberships and payments stay in CourtReserve. Leagues, rec nights and scoring, plus a separate player app and sign-in, would sit in RYVL, with no published data sync between them. The only CourtReserve link RYVL publishes is a one-way import for clubs leaving CourtReserve.
- Nothing published connects RYVL to Patch Retention. Member email would stay in Patch, and RYVL's own email goes through Postmark.
- The Golden Ring 2027 page suggests either a pilot or partnership is already under way, or RYVL listed the event ahead of a deal. Either way it is the most concrete thing to raise on Monday.

- Is RYVL an official DUPR partner with API access (automatic result upload), or are submissions manual? DUPR's own partner listing was not found.

## 5. Contract length, exclusivity and marketing obligations for partner clubs

### Takeaway
Published terms are light. There is a free 30-day pilot with no card. Plans are monthly or yearly with "cancel anytime" on the pilot. The Enterprise tier is "billed yearly". **No exclusivity, signage, branded-court or promotion obligations are published**, but Gauntlet partner terms are "set in your pilot terms, in writing", so any obligations would be in a document Peak hasn't seen yet.

### Cited Findings
- "30 days free · no card · cancel anytime." — [RYVL Clubs](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/)
- Plans are "billed monthly or yearly per location". Enterprise (10+ locations) is "billed yearly". — [RYVL Clubs FAQ](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/#faq); [RYVL Clubs](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/)
- Self-setup: "You do, from this page: your email, then your club's name and where you play." Members join "with its code or the QR poster". — [RYVL Clubs FAQ](https://clubs.ryvlpickleball.com/#faq)
- Gauntlet partner economics are set "in your pilot terms, in writing, before the first session". The brand, format, points engine and payment rails "stay central", while partners keep "the courts and the community". — [RYVL /partners](https://ryvlpickleball.com/partners)
- RYVL pitches the partner role as "Somebody who wants their venue to be where the city plays." — [RYVL /partners](https://ryvlpickleball.com/partners)
- Contacts: info@ryvlpickleball.com. RYVL says facility partnership inquiries get a reply "within two business days", and support usually answers "within one business day". — [RYVL /partners](https://ryvlpickleball.com/partners); [RYVL Support](https://ryvlpickleball.com/support)

### Inferences
- The "QR poster" and the app-only join flow mean, in practice, that Peak would be promoting RYVL to its members (posters, the app ask) even though no contract obligation is published.
- RYVL has already run two sold-out Gauntlets at another Colorado Springs facility (Springs Pickleball East). Partnering does not appear to come with city exclusivity. Whether Peak could get exclusivity, or would be competing with that venue, is unknown.

### Gaps (ask on Monday)
- Ask for the written pilot terms and the club agreement before agreeing to anything.
- Minimum term, auto-renewal, notice period and refund on annual plans.
- Any exclusivity, either way: Peak not using other league or ranking tools, or RYVL not partnering with nearby clubs.
- Required promotion: posters, emails to members, branded courts or signage, social posts, or Peak's name and logo on RYVL's site (already in use on the Golden Ring page).
- What happens to Peak's events, schedules and member links if the pilot ends.
