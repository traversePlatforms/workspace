Matthew's work for Peak Pickleball: scope and value

Matthew Sharpe · 24 Nov 2026

For $300 a week (about $1,300 a month), Peak gets a website rebuild, a sales desk for the sponsor and expo team, the outreach email that feeds it, and ongoing marketing and reporting. Bought separately, year one would cost about $35,300. At $300 a week, Peak pays $15,600.

Doing now

  • Sales desk for Brandon, Cai, Nneka, Lance and two outreach helpers: sponsor and expo pipeline, a shared reply inbox, tour booking, a daily report.
  • Outreach email from partnerships@peakpickleball.us: paced sends, with opens, clicks, bounces and unsubscribes tracked.
  • Research and sales materials: sponsor, expo, in-club tenant and coffee-partner prospect lists; the sponsor sheet, sponsor brief and payment process.
  • Audits of the website, socials, website forms and member email automations.
  • Data: a nightly read-only pull from CourtReserve and Patch, a day-one baseline (23 Nov), a weekly results report.
  • Helpers: trains and runs the outreach helpers (call scripts, how-to guides, look-up sheets).

Starting next

  • Website rebuild, live by 1 Jan ($3,000 quoted, included in the $300).
  • Member email automations redesigned, with Cammie.
  • Sponsor invoicing and autopay in QuickBooks.
  • Meta ads for new members (ad budget is Peak's).
  • Google Business Profile reviews and posts, once Google approves access.

Optional

  • Replace Patch Retention ($499 a month) with Matthew's email system: about $480 a month saved.
  • Later: a booking and membership system to replace CourtReserve, run side by side first.

Why $300 is worth it

  • Peak pays about 44% of the normal price for year one.
  • $300 buys about 3.5 hours at Matthew's $85 hourly rate, and this work takes most of his week.
  • Freelance marketers average $71.59 an hour and agencies $98.90 (Ahrefs, 439 providers, updated Aug 2024).
  • The optional Patch swap alone would save Peak more than a third of the fee every month.
