**How the day-one measures are defined** (in full: `impact-measurement-2026-11-23.md` §2.9-2.13 and the DAY-ONE MEASURES block of
`tools/impact.mjs`):

- **Still paying N days later:** a new member (first-ever dues) is still covered by dues on day N+5 after the first payment.
- **Starter upgrade:** the first dues were $35 and a later day within 90 days paid $39 or more. Read from the amount paid.
- **Regular:** 3+ check-ins in 60 days, not covered by dues, and not on a staff, comp or family plan. Joined = paid dues within 30 or 60 days.
- **Win-back:** dues ran out with nothing paid for 30 days, then paid again within 90 days.
- **Birthday month:** spend by members in their birthday month against their own average month. Members not in their birthday month
  that same month are the control.
- **Missed payments:** late = paid more than 24 hours after the due day. The suspension point is 10 days unpaid and the cancel point is 11.

**Corrected**

- **"27 November dues charges unpaid ($1,690)" (patch-automations and the Tyler list, item 28) counted charges raised ahead of their
  due day.** Only 2 of them ($194) were past due on day one. The Tyler list and patch-automations still carry the old figure; correct
  them before either is sent.
- **426 regulars versus 414.** The audit's 426 isn't reproducible exactly: its script isn't saved. Near variants on today's member list
  give 417 to 436. The scorecard's rule works for any past Sunday, not just today, which the member list can't do:
  - it reads membership from dues cover, not from the member list
  - it leaves out guest rows
  - it leaves out staff, comp and family plans
  - it gives 414 on Sun 22 Nov
- **The 5,001-person Thanksgiving blast is in Week 0, not Week 1.** Patch logs it at 00:01 on 23 Nov UTC, which is 5:01 pm on Sunday
  22 Nov in Colorado. The impact document calls it "the blast of 23 Nov", by its UTC date. The scorecard counts by Colorado day.

**Unresolved**

- **Paying members (dues) uses a 35-day cover, so it reads higher than the calendar-month count.**
  - A 31-day cover reproduces impact.json's October count of 665 exactly.
  - The 35-day cover keeps members who are a few days late on a card retry, so it gives 744 on 31 Oct. It moves less from week to
    week.
  - Compare it only with itself.
- **Lapsed members and the 7-day join rate change after the fact.**
  - Lapsed members settle 30 days after the Sunday: a late payment takes someone off the list.
  - The 7-day join rate settles 7 days after the Sunday.
  - The report shows them as they stand when it runs.
- **The member list exists only from 23 Nov 2026,** so the 807 and 21 have no last-week or last-year value. From Week 1 on, the
  report compares them with the previous Sunday's report.
- **New accounts come from today's member list,** so accounts deleted since are not counted. Older weeks may read a little low.
- **Dues collected counts cash in the week,** so prepaid annuals count in full the week they are paid. There were none in Week 0,
  the week before, or the same week last year. Dues are also lumpy by billing day, so read the trend over several weeks.
- **Suspensions read low in the dues data.** CourtReserve doesn't keep every failed charge: of the 21 accounts suspended on day one,
  only 7 have an open dues charge. The rest simply have no charge raised after their last payment.
  - The member list's Suspended count is the true figure from 23 Nov on.
  - The "unpaid at 10 days" row undercounts.
  - Suspended accounts also aren't cancelled at day 11 in practice. 5 of the 21 have been suspended since January 2026 (missed payment
    month: Jan 5, May 2, Jun 1, Jul 1, Aug 4, Sep 2, Oct 3, Nov 3).
- **The lagging rows are small weekly cohorts** (8 to 15 new members, 1 Starter, 14 lapses), so a single week swings by 10 to 20
  points. The normal is what to beat. The monthly cohorts are in `impact.json` `dayOneMeasures.byMonth`.
- **The birthday index carries tenure and season.** Without the control, the plain index reads about 100% for all spend. Members spend
  less over time, especially on gear in their first months, so the control sits at 57% for gear. The lift (birthday ÷ control) is the
  number to watch.
  - Single months are noisy: gear in Oct 2026 was $0 across 60 birthday members.
  - POS refunds carry no item, so gear is counted before refunds.
- **Retention is a snapshot on day N+5, not a running count.** Someone who renews late can be off on day 35 and back on day 65, so a
  small cohort's 60-day rate can sit above its 30-day rate.
- **Starter upgrades miss small prorations.** An upgrade billed only as a prorated difference under $39 isn't seen.
- **Family members:** whether family members on a paying parent's plan count as members is still Tyler's call. They are in the 807
