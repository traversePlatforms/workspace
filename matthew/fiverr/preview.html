I will set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC for your domain

Title 49/80 fits on the gig card Description 845/1200 5 tags, each 20 or under

About this gig

If your emails land in spam, or customers say they never got your invoice, your domain is often missing the records that prove your mail is really from you. I'll check your domain and set up the three that matter: - SPF: lists who is allowed to send as you - DKIM: signs each email so it can't be faked - DMARC: tells inboxes what to do with mail that fails No passwords needed. You paste the records in while I guide you, or you add me as a helper on your domain settings (GoDaddy, Cloudflare, Namecheap, Squarespace and most others allow this) and remove me after. Works with Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Zoho, and services that send for you like Mailchimp, Square or your CRM. Every order ends with a test send and a short report showing each check passing. More than one domain or more than three sending services? Message me first.

Questions for the buyer

  1. Your domain name (the part after @ in your email).
  2. Where your domain is managed, if you know (GoDaddy, Cloudflare...). If not, I will look it up.
  3. Your email provider (Google, Microsoft, Zoho...).
  4. Any other service that sends email as you: newsletters, invoices, your CRM, your website form.
  5. If mail is going to spam now: one example (who it went to and roughly when).

FAQ

Do you need my email password?

No. These records live in your domain settings. You can paste them in yourself, or give me helper access to the domain settings only and remove it after.

Will this guarantee my emails reach the inbox?

Nobody can honestly promise that. These records remove the most common reason mail is refused or marked as spam, and the report shows they pass.

I don't know where my domain is managed.

That's normal. Send me the domain name and I'll find out and tell you where to log in.

Do you use AI?

I use AI tools to draft and double-check. I do the setup and the testing myself.
