## 5. AI assistant

- **Duration:** 1 week · **Started:** 11 / 2026
- **Images:** gallery/ai-assistant-2.png
- **Description:**

## 7. Email setup
- **Project name:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC setup with inbox testing (45)
- **Industry:** Software / SaaS, Technology
- **Duration:** 1 week · **Started:** 10 / 2026
- **Images:** gallery/email-records-2.png
- **Description:**
  > Email authentication for my own domain: SPF, DKIM and DMARC. I tested it with real sends to Gmail, Google Workspace and a university mail system.
  >
  > The testing showed that an email with a large image landed in spam on one system while a plain version reached the inbox. I switched the campaign to plain emails before scaling it up.
