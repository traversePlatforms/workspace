Sponsor add-ons: time to build and run

For MatthewEstimates, 26 Nov 2026
How to read it
Build once is done one time and serves every sponsor after (Built = already running for Peak or Traverse). Set up is the time each new sponsor takes. Monthly is the hours per sponsor, per month, to keep it running. Price mostly on set-up plus monthly; the build is paid back over the first few sponsors.
