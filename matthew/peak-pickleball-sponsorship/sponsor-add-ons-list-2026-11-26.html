Sponsor add-ons: what we can offer

The tech sideof a Peak Pickleball sponsorship

At Peak: proof and visibility

  1. Tracked QR codesEach sponsor gets its own QR code for court signs, banners and table tents. Every scan is counted.
  2. Monthly sponsor reportA one-page report each month showing scans, link clicks and email clicks for the sponsor.
  3. Member deals with codesA special offer for Peak members with its own code, so the sponsor sees real redemptions.
  4. Sponsor slides on Peak's TVsThe sponsor in a rotating reel on the screens around the club, including "Court 4 presented by" boards.
  5. Partner spot in member emailsA "This month's partner" feature in Peak's emails to members.
  6. Sponsor page on Peak's websiteA profile of the sponsor on peakpickleball.us with links to their business.
  7. Staff sign-up linkFor sponsors who include memberships for their team: a sign-up link that shows how many staff joined and played.
  8. "Presented by" tournaments and leaguesThe sponsor's name on brackets, results, the TV boards and the results email.
  9. Event sign-upsA tablet sign-up at events, such as a raffle or a free lesson. Entries go to the sponsor only from people who opt in.
  10. Court cameras and replaysMatch replays with the sponsor on screen.
  11. Wi-Fi sign-in pageThe sponsor featured on the page members see when they join Peak's Wi-Fi.

Your website

  1. Website health checkA look at speed, mobile, broken links, Google basics and forms, with a clear fix list or the fixes done.
  2. Landing page for your Peak offerA simple page for your member deal and QR codes to point to, with visits counted.
  3. Visitor statsSimple, cookie-free counts of who visits your site, added to your monthly report.
  4. Forms that alert youContact and quote forms that email or text you the moment someone fills one in.
  5. Local search for your businessWork on your Google listing and local search results, with a monthly report.
  6. New website or redesignA new site built for you, quoted as its own project.
  7. Hosting and upkeepYour site kept updated, backed up and fixed each month.

Your social media

  1. Profile cleanupBios, links, hours and photos tidied and matched across Facebook, Instagram and Google.
  2. Monthly postsA month of posts drafted for you. Nothing goes out until you approve it.
  3. Spotlight posts with PeakPeak features your business on its socials, ready for you to share.
  4. Short videos from PeakClips of your name on the courts, at events and with the winners, for your own pages.
  5. Local ads on Facebook and InstagramAds set up and run for your area only, with results reported.
  6. Monthly social statsReach, followers and clicks from your pages, added to your monthly report.

Reviews and email

  1. Google review requestsA QR code or text link that asks happy customers for a review, with new reviews counted each month.
  2. Review alertsA heads-up whenever a new review lands, good or bad.
  3. Customer email newsletterA monthly email to your own customers, with opens and clicks counted.
