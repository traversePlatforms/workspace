Sponsor add-ons: what we can offer

The tech side of a Peak Pickleball sponsorship
  1. Tracked QR codesEach sponsor gets its own QR code for court signs, banners and table tents. Every scan is counted.
  2. Monthly sponsor reportA one-page report each month showing scans, link clicks and email clicks for the sponsor.
  3. Member deals with codesA special offer for Peak members with its own code, so the sponsor sees real redemptions.
  4. Sponsor slides on Peak's TVsThe sponsor in a rotating reel on the screens around the club, including "Court 4 presented by" boards.
  5. Partner spot in member emailsA "This month's partner" feature in Peak's emails to members.
  6. Sponsor page on Peak's websiteA profile of the sponsor on peakpickleball.us with links to their business.
  7. Staff sign-up linkFor sponsors who include memberships for their team: a sign-up link that shows how many staff joined and played.
  8. "Presented by" tournaments and leaguesThe sponsor's name on brackets, results, the TV boards and the results email.
  9. Local search for the sponsor's businessWork on the sponsor's own Google listing and local search results, with a monthly report.
  10. Event sign-upsA tablet sign-up at events, such as a raffle or a free lesson. Entries go to the sponsor only from people who opt in.
  11. Court cameras and replaysMatch replays with the sponsor on screen.
  12. Wi-Fi sign-in pageThe sponsor featured on the page members see when they join Peak's Wi-Fi.
