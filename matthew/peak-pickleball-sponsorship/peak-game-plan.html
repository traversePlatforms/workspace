Peak Pickleball · meeting one-sheet

Game plan: sponsorships, memberships and the nonprofit

For Brandon and Matthew · prepared 19 Oct 2026 · Peak Pickleball, 1730 Briargate Blvd, Colorado Springs (owners Scott and Tina Wilson)
Leave with
  1. Access to Peak's member and visit numbers. Every sponsor pitch needs them.
  2. A written 90-day trial on sponsorship commissions.
  3. A defined Surge trial for memberships.

1Open with their track record

It's Peak's strongest sponsor story, and their own site barely tells it.

  • First USA Pickleball Wheelchair Nationals: 94 players, 19–21 June 2026, Numotion as title partner.
  • Colorado Springs' first USA Pickleball Golden Ticket qualifier, 9–13 July 2025.
  • The Pickle Bowl, Feb 2025: 156 players, sponsored by Selkirk.
  • Free weekly veterans class: about 60 a session, 400+ taught.

3Show what makes sponsors renew

Their signs already carry QR codes, but nothing shows scans being reported back. Bring a mock monthly sponsor report (scans by week, event attendance, photos) and the pipeline. Reporting is the gap we fill, and it's why a $12,000 court sponsor pays again next year.

4Propose the deal

  • 90-day trial.
  • 15–20% on new sponsorships, about 10% on renewals.
  • A registered prospect list, so nobody argues over who found a sponsor.
  • Peak signs every sponsor contract; office space included.
  • Member and visit data shared from day one.

5Name a quick win

The 5th Annual Veterans Pickleball Tournament runs 6–8 November 2026 at Peak, with no outside sponsor listed. Offer to find one within two weeks. It's the fastest proof.

If they say yes: the first 30 days

  • Week 1: member and visit numbers; every sold and open asset with its renewal date; sponsor QR codes switched to tracked links.
  • Week 2: rate card with the new assets; a sponsor deck built on the audience and the national events; a prospect list by category.
  • Weeks 3–4: first sponsor meetings, first corporate-membership pitches, first monthly reports to current sponsors.

Don't, in this meeting

  • Quote prices for new assets before seeing their numbers.
  • Promise donation canvassing before the paid-solicitor registration ($15,000 bond) is sorted.
  • Agree to commission terms without them in writing.

Check by hand before going

  • Peak's Google and Yelp ratings.
  • Facebook and Instagram follower counts.
