# How sponsors pay Peak Pickleball

DRAFT, 23 Nov 2026. For Matthew to review first. After that it may go to Nathan, Tyler and Brandon. Nothing here has been sent, and nothing in QuickBooks has been changed.

## Who does what

- **Seller** (Brandon, or whoever owns the sponsor on the desk): gets the agreement signed, marks the sponsor Signed on the desk, and is the sponsor's contact if a payment is late.
- **Matthew** (Sales Manager access in Peak's QuickBooks, given for sponsor invoicing): sends the first invoice, helps the sponsor switch autopay on, and keeps the desk card up to date.
- **Nathan** (Peak's accountant): runs the monthly invoices on the 1st, sees payments come in, records prepaid payments, and says when a payment has failed.
- **Tyler and Scott**: set the prepaid terms and decide what happens to a sponsorship that stays unpaid.

## 1. Signing: the first invoice

1. The sponsor signs the agreement.
2. The seller marks them Signed on the desk: what they bought, the start date, how many months, who signed, and whether they pay monthly or prepaid.
3. **Monthly sponsors:** Matthew makes a QuickBooks invoice for the first payment, using the product for their tier (table below) and typing in the agreed amount. He sends it with the pay link.
4. **Prepaid sponsors:** skip QuickBooks and go to section 5.

## 2. First payment in: autopay during month one

1. The sponsor pays the first invoice through the pay link, by ACH. Card is fine on smaller invoices, as a backup.
2. Nathan or Matthew sees the payment in QuickBooks, and Matthew ticks "first payment received" on the desk card.
3. During the first month, autopay by ACH is switched on for that sponsor in QuickBooks. Autopay can be switched on per customer, but nobody at Peak has used it yet, so the exact steps (and who does them) are an open question below.
4. Matthew ticks "autopay on" on the desk card.

## 3. Every month: the invoice on the 1st

1. QuickBooks sends each monthly sponsor's invoice automatically on the 1st.
2. Sponsors with autopay on are charged by ACH. Anyone without it pays through the pay link.
3. Nobody needs to do anything unless a payment fails or is late (section 4).

## 4. A missed or failed payment (PROPOSAL, not agreed)

This is a suggested routine for Nathan, Tyler and Brandon to change or agree.

1. **Day 3 after the due date, or the day an ACH payment fails:** Nathan tells Matthew and the seller who owns the sponsor (one email is enough).
2. **Same week:** the seller contacts the sponsor in a friendly way ("the payment didn't go through"). Matthew resends the invoice with the pay link. If an ACH payment failed, the sponsor checks their bank details or pays by card, if the invoice is a small one.
3. **Day 15:** if it's still unpaid, the seller follows up again, and Matthew notes it on the desk card.
4. **Day 30:** Tyler decides what happens next (for example, pausing the sponsor's benefits). Nobody pauses signs or listings without his say.

## 5. Prepaid sponsorships

- These **don't go through QuickBooks**, because the fees are too high on large amounts.
- The sponsor pays by **check or ACH transfer**.
- **The terms are still to come from Tyler and Scott** (for example, any discount for paying up front, and when the money is due).
- Nathan records the payment when it arrives. Matthew ticks "paid in full" on the desk card.

## 6. Which QuickBooks product each tier uses

The amount is typed on each invoice. No prices are set in this document: every invoice uses the agreed amount from the sponsor's agreement.

| Tier | What it includes (Tyler, 23 Nov) | QuickBooks product |
|---|---|---|
| Title | Large, prominent signage | Title Sponsor |
| Court | Large court signage, and the court named in CourtReserve | Monthly Court Sponsorship |
| Logo | Smaller signage, plus digital advertising | **Open:** Nathan to say which product |
| Signage on its own or as an extra | Signs | Sponsorship Signage |
| A tournament or event | Presenting a tournament or event | Tournament Sponsorship |

Tyler says sponsors usually also get their logo in the biweekly newsletter, on the website and on the rotating digital signage, plus two Unlimited memberships. Brandon is standardising the packages, so the contents above may change. It's also open when "Corporate Sponsorship" is used.

## 7. Fees

QuickBooks charges 1% on ACH and 2.99% on card. **Peak absorbs the fees** (as it did with CourtReserve before). Sponsors pay the agreed amount and nothing extra.

## 8. The desk's Signed step

**What the desk does today:**

- A sponsor moves along the stages: To contact, Called, Left voicemail, Emailed, Texted, Meeting / tour booked, Proposal sent, Signed (or Not now, or Lost).
- **Proposal:** the seller picks packages from the desk's package list, and the desk saves a proposal that can be printed. It says "Draft until signed". The desk doesn't make or hold the agreement itself.
- **Mark Signed** opens a form: what they bought (package lines with amounts), the start date, how many months (12 unless changed), who signed, and a free note ("logo files, when signs go up, payment terms"). Saving it moves the card to Signed and counts it in the reports.
- **The Signed sponsors tab** lists every signed sponsor with its yearly value, the renewal date (flagged inside 60 days) and a free "To do for them" list that anyone can add to and tick off.
- Cards for Peak's existing sponsors show Peak's own tracker (sent 25 Oct), read only, which includes a "Payments" (monthly or annual) and a "Recurring invoice" column.
- **Nothing happens on its own when a sponsor is marked Signed:** no email, no alert, no invoice. The desk has no payment fields and no link to QuickBooks.

**What would need adding (PROPOSAL, not built):**

1. **"How they pay" on the Mark Signed form:** Monthly (QuickBooks) or Prepaid (check or ACH transfer), plus the QuickBooks product for monthly sponsors.
2. **A payment checklist added to the card automatically when it's saved,** in the existing "To do for them" list, so it needs no new screen:
   - Monthly: first invoice sent; first payment received; autopay on; signage ordered; signage up.
   - Prepaid: terms agreed (Tyler and Scott); payment received; signage ordered; signage up.
   - Each tick keeps who ticked it and when.
3. **A payment line on the Signed sponsors tab:** "Autopay on", "Waiting on first payment" or "Prepaid: paid", and a count of sponsors still waiting for autopay.
4. **A "Payment late" mark** the seller or Matthew can set by hand, with the date, so the follow-up in section 4 shows on Today.

The ticks would be done by hand. The desk wouldn't read or write QuickBooks. Nathan has no desk login, so he'd tell Matthew or the seller, and they'd tick it.
