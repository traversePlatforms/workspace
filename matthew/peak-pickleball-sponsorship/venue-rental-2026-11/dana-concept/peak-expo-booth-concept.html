Peak Pickleball: expo booth concept

Nets and court equipment removed · 10 x 10 ft booths · 10 ft aisles · drawn 24 Nov 2026
About 96 booths on the lower level (208 x 124 ft of court floor).
About 48 booths on the upper level (176 x 124 ft of court floor), plus a stage, food and seating area.
Concept only, not a measured floor plan. Each level is drawn as one open rectangle with the same area as its courts (13 lower, 11 upper, at Peak's 32 x 62 ft recreational court size). The real shape, walls, columns, stairs, entrances and fire exits are not shown, and floor outside the courts is not counted. Booth counts and crowd limits must be checked against the building's permit drawings (Regional Building permit P39019) and its fire occupancy limit before anything is promised.
