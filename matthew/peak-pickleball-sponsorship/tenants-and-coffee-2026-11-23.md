## How to approach them (tenants)

**What Peak can offer a tenant:**
- **Players already in the building.** Use Peak's own numbers only once Tyler OKs them (item 5): [MEMBERS PAYING DUES] and [SIGN-UPS A YEAR]. Until then, say what Peak's public site says: "thousands of active members" and "one of the largest privately owned indoor pickleball clubs in the U.S." (peakpickleball.us/partner-with-peak). From the data, for Brandon only and not for sharing: about 630 paying members in Oct 2026; busy weekdays 9 am–noon and 5–7 pm; quiet 5–8 am, weekday 3–5 pm and after 8 pm (peak-baseline-2026-11-04.md).
- **A built-in reason to visit.** Players get sore, and recovery or care right after play is easier than a separate trip.
- **Tracked proof.** The sponsor add-ons (QR codes, member codes, monthly report) work the same way for a tenant: each tenant's member perk gets its own code, so the tenant sees real redemptions.
- **Free parking.** The mall's lot (Gazette, May 2024).

**Deal shapes to offer:**
1. **Flat rent.** Simple for both sides. Best for a full build-out (a sauna or contrast room) where the tenant wants certainty.
2. **Rent plus revenue share.** Lower rent plus a share of what the tenant sells at Peak. Best for a cooler, counter or part-time room, where sales are uncertain at the start. It needs a way to count sales, such as the tenant's own point-of-sale report or member codes.
3. **Member-perk value.** The tenant gives Peak members free sessions or a members' rate in place of part of the rent. Agree on a fair value per perk, and count redemptions with codes, so neither side is guessing.
4. **Hours only.** A clinic day or two a week in a shared room. The lowest commitment, and a good first step for health systems and small practices.

**What to ask in a first call:**
- How much space they'd need, and what it needs (water, drains, power, ventilation, privacy). Who would pay for the build-out?
- Which days and hours they'd staff it.
- What they'd give Peak members, and how long that would last.
- Whether they'd want rent, rent plus a share, or hours to start.
- Their licensing and insurance (for health providers), and whether they'd add Peak as additionally insured. Brandon or Peak's own advisers own the legal side.
- Any exclusivity they'd want, and any they already have with other clubs (for example, Life Time Colorado Springs lists pickleball and a stretch and recovery service on its own site).
- Who signs, and how soon they could start.

**Don't promise:**
- Visit or sale numbers.
- Member lists or contact details (never, same as for sponsors).
- Rents, until Peak sets them.
- Anything before the lease question (item 1) is answered.
