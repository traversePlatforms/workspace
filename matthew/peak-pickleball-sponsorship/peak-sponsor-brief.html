Ideas to borrow

  • Bundle memberships into court sponsorships. Springs Pickleball’s $1,000/month Court Partner includes 4 memberships, paddles and gear. Peak’s $12,000 court could include staff memberships: it costs Peak little and the sponsor’s staff become players.
  • Cheaper ways in. Springs Pickleball starts at $250 (event tables) and $1,500 (a league season). Peak’s lowest published step is $12,000. A league, tournament or event-table tier lets small local businesses start, then move up.
  • Category exclusivity. Springs Pickleball sells it as an add-on: one bank, one hospital, one car dealer. Charge for it, or use it to close.
  • Publish the audience. Springs Pickleball shows 20,000+ players, 4,000+ followers, 65+ at every Paddle Up night, 100–300 at events. Peak needs its own real numbers on its sponsor sheet (visits, members, event counts, followers).
  • Tournaments and livestream. Springs Pickleball sells tournament tiers with livestream and social coverage. Peak’s national events are a bigger story: sell them as their own packages.
  • Socials as sponsor space. Peak’s Instagram has 918 followers against Springs Pickleball’s 4,000+. Taking over and growing Peak’s socials is what makes posts, stories and video worth selling to sponsors.

Wellhub

  • Where Peak sits. On Wellhub from the Titanium plan ($159.99 a month) only, per its Wellhub page. Employees on cheaper plans cannot use Peak.
  • What Wellhub users get. 24 indoor courts; beginner classes and a "Peak Pickleball University" track; open play, leagues, tournaments; members can reserve courts. Hours 7 am to 9 pm weekdays, 7 am to 8 pm weekends. No visit limits or rules listed.
  • Idea: a lower tier. Ask Peak whether it can join a lower Wellhub plan. More employees could walk in, which means more players for sponsors to reach. First ask Peak what Wellhub pays it, and how that would change on a lower plan.
  • Idea: Wellhub nights. A beginner clinic or league night for Wellhub users, presented by a sponsor (e.g. a health or fitness company): the sponsor gets the room, Peak gets new players.
  • Idea: direct employer plans. Wellhub only covers companies that buy it. A direct group plan (10% off for 1–5 staff, 20% for 6–50, 25% for 51+, as Pickleball Kingdom does; see the Claude Doc) reaches the rest, and the same companies are sponsor prospects.
  • Not known. What Wellhub pays Peak, how many Wellhub visits it gets, and which local employers offer Wellhub. Ask Peak.
