Owner keeps legal title throughout that term. This Agreement sets out those terms.

4. Possession and Use

Owner delivers possession of the Vehicle to Holder on the Effective Date. Holder may use the Vehicle for ordinary personal and business driving during the Term.

Holder shall:

  1. Hold a valid, unrestricted driver’s license at all times while in possession.
  2. Operate the Vehicle lawfully and keep it in the condition it was delivered in, ordinary wear and tear excepted.
  3. Keep the Vehicle principally garaged at and give Owner written notice before moving it out of Colorado for more than 30 days.
  4. Allow Owner to inspect the Vehicle on 48 hours’ written notice, at a reasonable time and place, not more than once a month.
  5. Tell Owner within 48 hours of any accident, theft, damage, mechanical failure, citation, impoundment or claim involving the Vehicle.

Holder shall not:

  1. Sell, trade, transfer, assign, pledge, lend, rent or pawn the Vehicle, or offer to.
  2. Pledge the Vehicle as collateral or allow any lien, levy or security interest to attach to it. Clearing any such lien is Holder’s responsibility, at Holder’s cost.
  3. Let anyone else drive it, except a licensed and insured driver with Holder’s permission for occasional use. No commercial passenger or delivery service, and no rideshare or car-sharing platform, without Owner’s prior written consent.
  4. Modify, alter, wrap, repaint or remove parts from the Vehicle without Owner’s prior written consent. Bolt-on accessories Holder adds at Holder’s own cost may be removed by Holder before return, provided the Vehicle is restored to its prior condition.
  5. Use the Vehicle for racing, for competition, for towing beyond the manufacturer’s rating, or for any unlawful purpose.

6. Maintenance and Condition

  1. Holder shall service the Vehicle on the manufacturer’s schedule, at Holder’s cost, using a qualified shop, and shall keep the receipts. Holder shall give Owner copies of the service records on request and at the end of the Term.
  2. Holder shall repair, at Holder’s cost, any damage occurring during the Term beyond ordinary wear and tear — including body damage, glass, interior damage, and mechanical damage caused by neglect or misuse.
  3. Ordinary wear and tear means what a reasonable person would expect from twelve (12) months of normal driving. It does not include dents, cracked glass, torn upholstery, unrepaired warning lights, bald tires or missing equipment.
  4. Mileage — check one: the Parties set no mileage cap; the Parties set a cap of miles for the Term, above which Holder pays Owner per mile at return.
  5. At delivery the Parties complete the condition report at Exhibit A, with photographs. That report is the baseline for any dispute about return condition and for Section 6.6.
  6. Owner’s ninety-day mechanical responsibility. For ninety (90) days from the Effective Date, Owner shall pay the cost of repairing a mechanical fault that clearly arose from the Vehicle’s condition before delivery, on these terms:
    1. Mechanical only. This covers the engine, transmission, drivetrain, braking, steering, suspension, cooling and electrical systems. It does not cover cosmetic condition, paint, trim, upholstery, glass, tires, wiper blades, bulbs or any other wear item, and it does not cover routine maintenance.
    2. Clearly pre-existing. The fault must be one that the Vehicle already had, or was already developing, at delivery. A fault first arising from Holder’s use, from an impact, from neglect, from missed servicing or from a modification is not covered. Exhibit A is the baseline; anything recorded there as a known condition is excluded.
    3. Notice. Holder shall tell Owner in writing within seven (7) days of the fault appearing, and before any repair is authorized except one needed to make the Vehicle safe to stop driving. Owner may inspect the Vehicle, and may require a second opinion from a shop Owner chooses and pays for.
    4. Cap. Owner’s total liability under this Section 6.6 is capped at in aggregate, and Owner is not liable for consequential loss, loss of use, a rental vehicle, towing beyond miles, or any diagnostic charge for a fault that turns out not to be covered.
    5. This Section ends ninety (90) days after the Effective Date and does not revive. It is the only exception to the as is delivery in Section 14.1.

9. No Sale to Anyone Else During the Term

During the Term, Owner shall not sell, offer to sell, list, trade or transfer the Vehicle to any third party, and shall not grant anyone else an option or interest in it. Holder’s position during the Term is exclusive.

If Owner receives an unsolicited offer during the Term, Owner may tell Holder about it, but Owner may not accept it.

10. Right of First Refusal After the Term

This section applies after the Term ends under Section 8(b). It survives the end of this Agreement.

  1. For twelve (12) months after the Vehicle is returned (the “ROFR Period”), if Owner decides to sell the Vehicle and receives a bona fide written offer from a third party that Owner is willing to accept, Owner shall first give Holder written notice of that offer, stating the price and the material terms, with a copy of the offer and the buyer’s personal details redacted.
  2. Holder then has seven (7) days from that notice to tell Owner in writing that Holder will buy the Vehicle at the same price and on the same material terms. If Holder does, Holder shall close within fourteen (14) days of giving that notice.
  3. Proof of funds. With the notice of exercise, Holder shall deliver proof of funds or a written financing commitment for the full price, and pay Owner a deposit of . That deposit is credited at closing, is returned in full if Owner fails to close, and is forfeited to Owner if Holder fails to close within the fourteen days for any reason other than Owner’s default.
  4. If Holder does not respond within seven days, or responds and then fails to close within fourteen days, Owner may sell to the third party on terms no more favorable to that buyer than the terms offered to Holder. If that sale falls through, or Owner later agrees materially better terms with anyone, this right applies again to the new terms.
  5. “Same terms” means the same price and the same payment structure. A trade-in allowance or other non-cash consideration in the third-party offer is converted to its cash value for the purpose of Holder’s match, and Owner shall state that value in the notice.
  6. Remedy. If Owner sells or transfers the Vehicle in breach of this Section, the Parties agree that damages alone would not adequately compensate Holder, and Holder may seek specific performance or injunctive relief in addition to any other remedy.
  7. This right is personal to Brandon Long. It is not assignable, it does not bind any subsequent owner of the Vehicle, and it expires at the end of the ROFR Period.
  8. This right does not apply to a transfer to a member of Owner’s immediate family, to an insurance settlement following a total loss, or to a trade-in to a dealer as part of Owner’s purchase of a replacement vehicle.

11. Total Loss, Theft and Major Damage

  1. Holder bears the risk of loss while in possession.
  2. If the Vehicle is stolen and not recovered, or is declared a total loss, any proceeds recovered for it are payable to Owner as titled owner. Owner shall refund the $7,000.00 Option Payment to Holder within days of receiving those proceeds, and this Agreement then ends. Holder’s option to purchase ends with it.
  3. If the amount Owner actually recovers is less than the Total Purchase Price because of anything Holder did or failed to do, including any breach of the Related Agreement referred to in Section 14.12, Holder shall pay Owner the shortfall.
  4. If the Vehicle is damaged but repairable, Holder shall have it repaired at Holder’s cost and the Term continues unchanged.

12. Default and Remedies

(a) Default by Holder

Each of the following is a default by Holder:

  1. Any breach of the Related Agreement referred to in Section 14.12.
  2. Selling, pledging, concealing or attempting to transfer the Vehicle.
  3. Failure to return the Vehicle when required under Section 8(b).
  4. Driving without a valid license, or use of the Vehicle in a crime.
  5. Any other material breach not cured within ten (10) days of written notice.

On a default, Owner may, by written notice, end the Term early and require the Vehicle to be returned within seventy-two (72) hours. Owner shall still refund the Option Payment under Section 8(b), less the deductions permitted there and less any loss Owner suffers from the default.

Owner may recover the Vehicle only by lawful means and without breach of the peace. Owner shall not enter a locked or enclosed space without permission, and shall not use force. If Holder does not surrender the Vehicle, Owner’s remedy is a court order.

(b) Default by Owner

Each of the following is a default by Owner:

  1. Failing to pay Holder an amount due under Section 8(b) or Section 11 when it falls due.
  2. Selling, listing, transferring, pledging or granting any security interest in the Vehicle in breach of Section 9, or encumbering the title during the Term.
  3. Failing to sign over the title and deliver a bill of sale after Holder has paid the balance in full under Section 8(a).
  4. A representation in Section 1 proving untrue in a material respect.
  5. Any other material breach of this Agreement by Owner.

Notice and cure. Before treating any of the above as a default, Holder shall give Owner written notice describing it. Owner then has ten (10) days from that notice to cure it — except that a failure under Section 12(b)(3) must be cured within five (5) business days, because Holder has by then paid in full. A default that is cured within its period is not a default.

Remedies. On an uncured Owner default, Holder may:

  1. recover any amount due, together with the reasonable costs of collection;
  2. where Holder has paid the balance in full under Section 8(a), seek specific performance of the transfer — the Parties agree damages alone would not adequately compensate Holder for the loss of this particular Vehicle; and
  3. end the Term by written notice, return the Vehicle, and receive the refund under Section 8(b) without the deductions permitted there, other than deductions for damage beyond ordinary wear and tear.

Sections 12(a) and 12(b) are intended to mirror each other. Neither Party may treat a breach as a default without first giving written notice and allowing the cure period to run.
