## Background

Owner owns the Vehicle described in Section 1 and is willing to sell it. Holder wants
the use of the Vehicle now and the right, but not the obligation, to buy it later.
Rather than a sale today, the Parties have agreed that Holder will pay $8,000.00 now,
take possession and use of the Vehicle, and hold an option to complete the purchase at
the end of a six-month term. Owner keeps legal title throughout that term. This
Agreement sets out those terms.

## 9. What Happens at the End of the Term

One of the following three outcomes will occur. There is no fourth.

### (a) Holder completes the purchase

Holder may buy the Vehicle outright by giving Owner written notice **no later than 30
days before the Option Deadline**, and paying the balance on or before the Option
Deadline.

|---|---|

| Less the Option Payment already paid | ( $8,000.00 ) |

The Total Purchase Price is fixed now, at signing. It does not move with mileage, market
value or condition.

On receipt of cleared funds in full, Owner shall sign over the title, deliver a signed
bill of sale, and remove the Vehicle from Owner's insurance. Ownership passes at that
point and this Agreement ends, except for Section 14, which survives.

Holder may pay the balance early. If Holder does, the purchase closes early on the same
terms.

### (b) Holder does not complete, and Owner takes the Vehicle back

If Holder does not give the notice required by Section 9(a), or gives notice but does not
pay the balance in full by the Option Deadline, then:

1. Holder shall return the Vehicle to Owner at ______________________________________ on
   or before the Option Deadline, in the condition required by Section 7, with all keys,
   the owner's manual, all equipment and the service records.
2. Owner shall refund the **full $8,000.00 Option Payment** to Holder within ________
   days of the Vehicle being returned. Holder is not charged for the use of the Vehicle
   during the Term.
3. Owner may deduct from that refund only: (i) the documented cost of repairing damage
   beyond ordinary wear; (ii) any mileage overage under Section 7.4; and (iii) unpaid
   tickets, tolls, taxes or reimbursements Holder owes under Section 6. Owner shall give
   Holder an itemised statement, with receipts, for anything deducted.
4. Nothing further is owed by either Party and this Agreement ends, except for
   Section 11, which survives.

### (c) Both Parties agree to extend

The Parties may agree in writing, before the Option Deadline, to extend the Term. The
extension must state the new Option Deadline and whether the Total Purchase Price
changes. Without a signed extension, outcome (a) or (b) applies.

but Owner may not accept it.

## 11. Right of First Refusal After the Term

This section applies **only** after the Term ends under Section 9(b) — that is, where
Holder did not complete the purchase and Owner took the Vehicle back. It survives the end
of this Agreement.

1. For **twelve (12) months** after the Vehicle is returned (the "**ROFR Period**"), if
   Owner decides to sell the Vehicle and receives a bona fide written offer from a third
   party that Owner is willing to accept, Owner shall first give Holder written notice of
   that offer, stating the price and the material terms, with a copy of the offer and the
   buyer's personal details redacted.
2. Holder then has **seven (7) days** from that notice to tell Owner in writing that
   Holder will buy the Vehicle at the same price and on the same material terms. If
   Holder does, Holder shall close within **fourteen (14) days** of giving that notice.
3. If Holder does not respond within seven days, or responds and then fails to close
   within fourteen days, Owner may sell to the third party on terms no more favourable to
   that buyer than the terms offered to Holder. If that sale falls through, or Owner later
   agrees materially better terms with anyone, this right applies again to the new terms.
4. "Same terms" means the same price and the same payment structure. A trade-in allowance
   or other non-cash consideration in the third-party offer is converted to its cash value
   for the purpose of Holder's match, and Owner shall state that value in the notice.
5. This right is personal to Brandon Long. It is not assignable, it does not bind any
   subsequent owner of the Vehicle, and it expires at the end of the ROFR Period.
6. This right does not apply to a transfer to a member of Owner's immediate family, to an
   insurance settlement following a total loss, or to a trade-in to a dealer as part of
   Owner's purchase of a replacement vehicle.

## 12. Total Loss, Theft and Major Damage

1. Holder bears the risk of loss while in possession.
2. If the Vehicle is stolen and not recovered, or is declared a total loss, the insurance
   proceeds are paid to Owner as titled owner and loss payee. Owner shall refund the
   $8,000.00 Option Payment to Holder within ________ days of receiving those proceeds,
   and this Agreement then ends. Holder's option to purchase ends with it.
3. If the settlement is less than the Total Purchase Price because of a lapse, an
   exclusion, a misstatement to the insurer, or Holder's failure to maintain the coverage
   required by Section 5, Holder shall pay Owner the shortfall.
4. If the Vehicle is damaged but repairable, Holder shall have it repaired at Holder's
   cost and the Term continues unchanged.

## 13. Default and Remedies

Each of the following is a default by Holder:

1. Failure to maintain the insurance required by Section 5, or any lapse in it.
2. Selling, pledging, concealing or attempting to transfer the Vehicle.
3. Failure to return the Vehicle when required under Section 9(b).
4. Driving without a valid licence, or use of the Vehicle in a crime.
5. Any other material breach not cured within **ten (10) days** of written notice.

On a default, Owner may, by written notice, end the Term early and require the Vehicle to
be returned within **seventy-two (72) hours**. Owner shall still refund the Option Payment
under Section 9(b), less the deductions permitted there and less any loss Owner suffers
from the default.

Owner may recover the Vehicle **only** by lawful means and without breach of the peace.
Owner shall not enter a locked or enclosed space without permission, and shall not use
force. If Holder does not surrender the Vehicle, Owner's remedy is a court order.

If Owner fails to refund the Option Payment when it is due under Section 9(b) or
Section 12, Holder may recover it together with the reasonable costs of collection.
