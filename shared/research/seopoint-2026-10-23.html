Seopoint (seopointinc.com)

Research brief · 23 October 2026 · every fact traced to a page opened that day (sources on page 2)

Bottom line: a real but very small Colorado Springs company (incorporated Feb 2021, 2–10 staff). Its main product is a monthly do-it-yourself "Business App" that is Vendasta software resold under its own name (confirmed from their client login page). No reviews, case studies or published terms anywhere, and also no complaints or lawsuits. Most of what it sells overlaps tools we already have.

What they sell

3–6 month contract on every plan: about $1,200 to $5,100 minimum. Extra training $50 each. Done-for-you plans are custom-quoted. Referral program up to $500.

AI receptionist · CRM · automations · local SEO · review management · email & text campaigns · social posting · ad monitoring · citation builder · listing sync

Agency services (no prices shown)

SEO / SEM · social media · website design · brand design · free "Digital Report Card" audit. Targets businesses "of all sizes".

Their pitch: "understand your goals, craft a plan, deliver results." No ranking guarantees, and no result can be checked: they publish no metrics or case studies.

Warning signs

REDNo terms of service or privacy policy. Both links go back to the homepage, so you can't see who owns your listings, reviews, contacts or website.
REDIt is resold Vendasta software — confirmed. Their client login (login.seopointinc.com) runs on Vendasta's systems: it calls Vendasta's login service at apigateway.co, uses Vendasta's product code "VBC" (Business Center) and carries Seopoint's reseller ID, L4F0. Their chat widget also loads from Vendasta. You'd pay their markup, and leaving likely means losing the dashboard, data and automations.
WATCHTheir own details don't match: two phone numbers ((331) 231-2882 and (630) 303-0950), a dead second website and three different ZIP codes. Odd for a company that sells listing clean-up.
WATCH3–6 month minimum. Mild for the industry, but get the exact term, renewal and cancellation in writing.
OKNo ranking guarantees, no sign of link schemes, a real registered company.

Fit for us

We already have our own app (CRM, quoting, pipeline), the website and lead form, Square and Traverse, which cover most of the bundle. The only parts that might add something are review requests and keeping listings consistent across directories. Both can be bought directly or built into our app for less.

If you talk to them, ask

  1. Business App is Vendasta (your reseller ID L4F0): what does it cost you per client, and what do you add?
  2. Send the written contract: 3 or 6 months, auto-renew, cancellation notice, setup fee?
  3. If I leave, do I keep my Google profile, listings, reviews, contacts, website and domain? In what format?
  4. Which accounts are in your name and which in mine?
  5. Who does the work, how many staff, is any of it subcontracted?
  6. Three current field-service clients I can call, with before-and-after numbers.
  7. AI receptionist usage limits? Are texts, emails and 10DLC fees extra?
  8. Why do your own phone numbers and website differ across listings?
  9. Where are your terms of service and privacy policy?
