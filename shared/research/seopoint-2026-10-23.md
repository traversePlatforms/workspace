# Seopoint (seopointinc.com) — research, 2026-10-23

Asked by Matthew: "do a deep dive of this company ... what they offer, what they do, if they are successful ... how their program works." Researched by a Claude research agent the same day; every claim below traces to a page opened on 2026-10-23 (sources at the foot). Not entity-specific: relevant to Sixfold 365, Surge and Traverse alike.

## Verdict

A real, very small Colorado Springs company: a Colorado corporation in good standing since Feb 2021, 2–10 staff on LinkedIn. Its main product is a monthly "Business App" that looks like a rebranded (white-label) copy of the Vendasta platform. No independent reviews, no verifiable case studies, and no published terms or privacy policy. Also no complaints, lawsuits or ranking guarantees found. Treat it as a small reseller with no track record you can check.

## What they sell

- **Agency services** (SEO/SEM, social marketing, website design, brand/design, "Digital Report Cards"): no prices; every page leads to a contact form. Targets businesses "of all sizes", no industries named.
- **Business App** (seopointinc.com/business-app/), month-to-month column, all on a **"3-6 month contract"**, "AI Set-Up & Tech Support Included":

| Plan | Price | Training | Q&A |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | $399/mo | 1 | 1 |
| Pro | $599/mo | 2 | 1 |
| Premium ("Popular") | $849/mo | 3 | 2 |

  Extra training sessions are $50 each. Every plan includes: AI receptionist, CRM, automations, local SEO, reputation management, email/SMS campaigns, social marketing, advertising monitoring, citation builder, listing sync. Minimum commitment works out to about $1,197 (Standard, 3 months) up to $5,094 (Premium, 6 months). A custom done-for-you plan is available through sales; a referral program pays "up to $500".
- Older pricing seen only in a search snippet: $399 / $549 / $749 a month billed annually.
- **Guarantees:** none stated. The local SEO page is hedged ("a better shot at showing up first").

## How the program works (as they describe it)

"Understand your goals → craft a plan → deliver results." For the app: Join → book a discovery call → the onboarding sessions in your tier → you run it yourself, with tech support. Website design starts with a consultation; the Report Card grades your visibility, reputation and performance from basic details. They use Slack and Monday.com. None of the result claims can be verified: no metrics, no case studies.

## Red and green flags

- **Guaranteed rankings:** none. Green.
- **Lock-in:** 3–6 month minimum: mild by industry standards, but get the exact term in writing.
- **Who owns your site and listings:** unknown, and **there are no terms or privacy policy** (/privacy-policy/ and /terms/ redirect to the homepage, though the site runs analytics and a chat widget). Red.
- **Resold platform (inferred, not confirmed):** Vendasta sells a white-label "Business App" with the same feature names, Seopoint's booking page uses Vendasta's scheduler (BookMeNow), and the site loads scripts from Vendasta's CDN (cdn.apigateway.co). If right, leaving likely means losing the dashboard, data and automations.
- **Inconsistent details (odd for a company selling listing sync):** the site shows (331) 231-2882, its hidden structured data says (630) 303-0950 and www.seopoint.com (dead), and ZIP codes differ across listings (80901 / 80921 / 80908).
- **Fake reviews, link schemes:** no evidence either way.
- **Legitimacy:** registered, in good standing, named principal, consistent 2021 start, local. Green.

## Questions to ask before signing

1. Is Business App Vendasta (or another platform)? What does it cost direct, and what are you adding on top?
2. Send the written contract: 3 or 6 months? Auto-renew? How do I cancel, with what notice? Any setup fee?
3. If I leave, do I keep my Google Business Profile, listings, reviews, CRM contacts, website files and domain, and in what export format?
4. Which accounts are registered in your name versus mine?
5. Who exactly does the work, how many staff, is any of it subcontracted?
6. Three current field-service clients I can call, with before-and-after numbers.
7. AI receptionist usage caps or per-minute costs? Are SMS/email sends and 10DLC registration extra?
8. Why do your own phone numbers and website differ across listings?
9. Where are your terms of service and privacy policy?

## Fit for us

Sixfold 365 already has a CRM and quoting in its own app, a website with a lead form, Square, and the Traverse field-sales product; most of the bundle overlaps. The parts that might add something are reputation management (review requests) and listing sync. Compare those against buying the tools directly.
