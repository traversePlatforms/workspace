HB25-1182 · C.R.S. 10-4-124 · In effect 1 July 2026

Your house has a score.
Now they have to show you.

If your insurer uses a wildfire risk model to price, renew or decline your policy, the law now makes them put it in writing once a year.

What the notice has to contain — all five

01
A plain-language explanation, including that insurers use different models and different ranges
02
The full range of scores your property could have been given
03
Where your score sits inside that range
04
Why — naming the primary features of the property that drove it
05
What each mitigation action would do to it

You have done the work

Mitigation finished since you last renewed? You do not have to wait for renewal to be re-scored.

30
days
Ask for a revised score and the insurer has 30 days to produce one.
15
days
New application instead? The notice is due within 15 days of a completed application. Existing policyholders get it with the renewal offer.

You think it is wrong

If the score or the discount looks wrong and you have evidence of work done, you appeal to the insurer directly.

10
days
They must acknowledge your appeal in writing within 10 calendar days.
30
days
And give you a written decision within 30 calendar days. A denied appeal goes to the Commissioner on request.

Not insurance advice — read your own notice and talk to your carrier. The statute entitles you to a re-score, not to a better one, and what any discount is worth is the insurer’s to set. We do not contact insurers on anyone’s behalf.

The part worth keeping

Insurers also have to publish which mitigation actions earn a discount and how much each one is worth. That list is the closest thing there is to a price on this work being written down by somebody with no reason to flatter it.

Source: HB25-1182, enacted act read in full, adding C.R.S. 10-4-124 — notice duty §(6)(a), the five contents §(6)(b)(I)–(V), delivery and re-score timing §(7)(a)–(d), appeal clocks §(8), published discount list §(5). Applies to policies issued or renewed on or after 1 July 2026.
