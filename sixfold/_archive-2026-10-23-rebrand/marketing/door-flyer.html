Lawn rehab

Does your lawn suck?

Thin, patchy, hard as a driveway? Hard ground sheds water instead of sucking it up, and the grass on top thins out no matter what you feed it!

  1. Aerate. Hollow tines pull soil cores two to three inches deep, two to four inches apart. The cores stay on the lawn and break back down into it.
  2. Seed. Overseed goes down while the holes are still open. That is the part that thickens it up.
  3. Feed. Fertilizer the same day — all three in the one visit. Spring and fall are the windows.
