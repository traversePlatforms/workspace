running the ad himself; nothing here is posted, scheduled or placed from this repo.**

Built on the strategy already set in
[../aeration-ad-kit-2026-09.md](../aeration-ad-kit-2026-09.md): there is **no money story

## ⚑ Corrected 2026-09-28 — the first drafts were written for the wrong medium

Options 1–3 below apply the ad kit's two-facts strategy. **That strategy was reasoned out
for Google RSA**, where the user has already typed "lawn aeration" and intent exists. The
job there is to differentiate among people who already want the thing.

**Meta is interruption.** Nobody scrolling the feed is looking for aeration, so the ad has
to create the want *and* say who is asking. A pure tip with no company in it reads as a
stranger's advice and gives a cold reader nothing to hire.

Matthew called this. **Options 4 and 5 are the offer-led rewrites and supersede 1–3 for
any Meta placement.** Options 1–3 stay on the page because they are still the right shape
for a Google search ad, and for retargeting an audience that already knows the name.

of a sentence the headline starts.

## Rules this copy is written to

- **Do not claim to be the honest one.** Earlier drafts of the first line read "the half
  nobody tells you". That is a claim about competitors the reader can go and test, and
  the ad kit rules it out. Carry the fact; say nothing about carrying it.
- **No limit, no urgency device.** No "spots filling", no countdown. Standing rule.
- **No headcount**, no "our team of…".
- **Nothing that turns work away** — no minimum lot size, no "we only do…".
- No sentence explaining why the previous sentence matters; those get cut anyway.
