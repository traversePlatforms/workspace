Services

Fire mitigation.

We fell trees, remove and trim them whole, take out brush and dead fall, pile and chip slash, and cut rounds to length.

What happens to the wood is your call — chipped on site, hauled away, or left stacked where you want it.

Felling · Trimming · Slash & chip · Stumps · Defensible space

Defensible space around the structures, fuel breaks, and thinning, worked to Colorado State Forest Service standards for spacing and clearance.

Those standards are set out in full in the wildfire guide — the three zones, measured out from your wall, and the distances inside each one.

Who pays for it

This is the service the money attaches to.

Colorado's wildfire mitigation tax credit is claimed against qualifying vegetation work, and since 1 July 2026 an insurer that scores your wildfire risk has to show you the score and discount it for mitigation you can document.
