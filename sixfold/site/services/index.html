Services

Three services. Any one of them, or all three.

Fire mitigation

We fell trees, remove and trim them whole, take out brush and dead fall, pile and chip slash, and cut rounds to length.

What happens to the wood is your call — chipped on site, hauled away, or left stacked where you want it.

Defensible space around the structures, fuel breaks, and thinning, worked to Colorado State Forest Service standards for spacing and clearance.

Those standards are set out in full in the wildfire guide.

Land clearing

Brush, undergrowth and dead fall taken off a lot, a pasture or an easement, and the ground left ready for whatever comes next.

What happens to what comes off is settled at the walk: chipped on site, hauled away, or left stacked where you want it.

Landscaping

Mowing, edging, beds and planting, sod and seed, shrub and hedge trimming, clean-up and haul-away, and more.

Tell us how you want the ground kept and we'll walk it with you — the area, the access, and anything you want left alone.

The quote comes off what is actually there, counted at the walk.

How it goes

Walk it, work it, hand it over.

  1. 01

    The walk

    We come out and walk the property with you, counting what is actually there — trees by height, brush by area, what the access will take, and what you want kept. It goes on a sheet, and the quote is built from it.

  2. 02

    The work

    Photographs from fixed positions before we start, then the work you agreed and whatever you chose for what comes off. Anything you asked to keep gets pointed out at the walk and left on the property.

  3. 03

    The handover

    We walk the finished job with you and hand over the folder: a written record of the work and the standard it was done to, an itemised invoice, and dated before-and-after photographs from fixed positions.

What you keep

Every job comes with the paperwork.

  • What we did on your property — a written record of the work and the standard it was done to. This is the page your insurer wants.
  • Your itemised invoice — with the wildfire-mitigation work separated out, because that is what the state credit is claimed against.
  • Before-and-after photos — dated, from fixed positions.
  • Getting money back — the state credit and the insurance re-score, with what each one needs from you. What a discount is worth is the insurer's decision, not ours.
  • Programs in your county — the ones that pay toward future mitigation. All of them need approval before work starts, so they are in here for next time.
  • A card, a magnet and a sticker — so the number is on the fridge when a neighbour asks who did the work.

One document set, and it serves your tax return and your carrier both. You get it at the end of the job as a matter of course.

Sixfold produces the record. It does not file your return, contact your carrier, or handle a claim — those stay with you and your advisers, and the folder is built so they have what they need without a phone call to us.

And if anything about the work is not right, call.
