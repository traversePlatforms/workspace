Services

Four services. Any one of them, or all four.

Lawn & turf care

Core aeration — hollow tines pull plugs out of the turf so water, air and fertilizer reach the root zone instead of running off compacted ground.

The plugs stay on the surface and break down into the lawn. Spring and fall are the best times for it, and it can be done any time the ground is not frozen and not baked dry.

Overseeding after a pass, and the regular keeping alongside it — mowing, edging, fertilizing and seeding.

Tell us roughly how big the lawn is and how it has been kept so far, and we'll walk it with you.

Landscaping

Shrub and hedge trimming, plants and trees taken out, bed clean-up and haul-away.

Land clearing

Brush, undergrowth and dead fall taken off a lot, a pasture or an easement, and the ground left ready for whatever comes next.

What happens to what comes off is settled at the walk: chipped on site, hauled away, or left stacked where you want it.

Fire mitigation

We fell trees, remove and trim them whole, take out brush and dead fall, pile and chip slash, and cut rounds to length.

What happens to the wood is your call — chipped on site, hauled away, or left stacked where you want it.

Defensible space around the structures, fuel breaks, and thinning, worked to Colorado State Forest Service standards for spacing and clearance.

Those standards are set out in full in the wildfire guide.

How it goes

Walk it, work it, hand it over.

  1. 01

    The walk

    We come out and walk the property with you, counting what is actually there — trees by height, brush by area, what the access will take, and what you want kept.

  2. 02

    The work

    Photographs from fixed positions before we start, then the work you agreed and whatever you chose for what comes off. Anything you asked to keep gets pointed out at the walk and left on the property.

  3. 03

    The handover

    We walk the finished job with you and hand over the folder: a written record of the work and the standard it was done to, an itemised invoice, and dated before-and-after photographs from fixed positions.

What you keep

Every job comes with the paperwork.

Every job

  • What we did on your property — a written record of the work and the standard it was done to.
  • Your itemised invoice — line by line, so you can see what each part of the job cost.
  • Before-and-after photos — dated, from fixed positions.

If the job included fire mitigation

  • The state tax credit — what it covers and what you need to claim it. Your invoice separates the mitigation work from the rest of the job, because the credit is claimed against that part and not the whole bill.
  • Your insurer's wildfire risk score — how to ask for a re-score, with the written record as the evidence. What a discount is worth is the insurer's decision, not ours.
  • Programs in your county — the ones that pay toward future mitigation. All of them need approval before work starts, so they are in here for next time.

Sixfold produces the record; it does not file your return or contact your carrier. Not tax or insurance advice — confirm your own position with your accountant and your carrier.
