If it's fire mitigation

Two parties will help pay for it. Most people are never told about either.

Colorado's wildfire mitigation tax credit covers 100% of what you pay a contractor for qualifying vegetation work, up to a cap. And since 1 July 2026, an insurer that scores your wildfire risk has to show you the score — and give a discount for mitigation you can document.

~450

property owners in the entire state claimed the credit in tax year 2023. The legislature's own evaluation says the binding constraint is awareness.
