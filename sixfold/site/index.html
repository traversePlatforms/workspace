Who pays for it

Two parties will help cover this. Most people are never told about either.

The state tax credit

Colorado's wildfire mitigation tax credit covers 100% of what you pay a contractor for qualifying vegetation work, up to a cap. No application and no waiting list — you claim it on your state return.

Income limits apply. Through tax year 2026, any amount above your state tax bill is lost rather than refunded; from tax year 2027 the cap rises, the credit becomes refundable, the income limit goes up, and it runs through 2030.

C.R.S. 39-22-543 · Colorado Dept. of Revenue, Income Tax Topics: Wildfire Mitigation Measures · HB26-1289 for the 2027 changes

Your insurer

Since 1 July 2026, if your carrier uses a wildfire risk score to price your policy, they have to give you that score in writing every year — and a discount for mitigation you can document.

What your own policy qualifies for is a question for your carrier, and the credit's income test is a question for your accountant. But almost nobody asks, because almost nobody knows to.

Colorado HB25-1182, effective 1 July 2026

Before you call anyone

The highest-value work on your property is the part you can do yourself.

Colorado's forest service calls the ground from your wall out to five feet Zone 1, and the guidance for it is unsentimental: nothing in there should burn.

Pine needles cleared off the foundation and the deck. Wood mulch swapped for gravel or rock. Firewood moved out to thirty feet. The first five feet of any wooden fence that touches the house replaced with something that doesn't.

A study of more than 71,000 properties inside wildfire perimeters between 2016 and 2019 found that buildings with a lot of vegetation within five feet of the walls were destroyed 78% of the time — close to twice the rate of buildings with little vegetation in that strip. Embers do that, not a wall of flame.

None of it needs a crew, and we would rather you knew. Call us for what is past it — the trees, the acreage, and the part that wants a saw.

Colorado State Forest Service Home Ignition Zone guidance · Zesty.ai / IBHS analysis of 71,000+ properties, released 8 April 2021. Colorado numbers the 0–5 ft strip Zone 1; the California scheme numbers it Zone 0.

How it goes

Walk it, work it, hand it over.

  1. 01

    The walk

    We come out and walk the property with you, counting what is actually there — trees by height, brush by area, what the access will take, and what you want kept. It goes on a sheet, and the quote comes off that sheet rather than off an impression formed in the driveway.

  2. 02

    The work

    Photographs from fixed positions before anything comes down, then the felling, the clearing and the disposal option you picked. Anything you asked to keep gets pointed out at the walk and left on the property.

  3. 03

    The handover

    We walk the finished job with you, and you get a folder. It is the same folder every time, and it is handed over whether or not you asked for it.

What you keep

Every job comes with the paperwork, whether or not you ask.

  • What we did on your property — a written record of the work and the standard it was done to. This is the page your insurer wants.
  • Your itemised invoice — with the wildfire-mitigation work separated out, because that is what the state credit is claimed against.
  • Before-and-after photos — dated, from fixed positions.
  • Getting money back — the state credit and the insurance re-score, with what each one needs from you. What a discount is worth is the insurer's decision, not ours.
  • Programs in your county — the ones that pay toward future mitigation. All of them need approval before work starts, which is why they are in here for next time rather than this time.
  • A card, a magnet and a sticker — so the number is on the fridge when a neighbour asks who did the work.

One document set, and it serves your tax return and your carrier both. You get it at the end of the job as a matter of course.

Sixfold produces the record. It does not file your return, contact your carrier, or handle a claim — those stay with you and your advisers, and the folder is built so they have what they need without a phone call to us.

And if anything about the work is not right, call. The crew that did it is the crew that comes back.
