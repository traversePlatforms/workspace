The work

Three things, and they usually turn up on the same property.

Fire mitigation

Felling, full tree removal and trimming, brush and dead fall out, slash piled and chipped, rounds cut to length. Defensible space, fuel breaks and thinning by the acre, worked to Colorado State Forest Service standards.

Land clearing

Who pays for it

Two parties will help cover this. Most people are never told about either.

The state tax credit

Colorado's wildfire mitigation tax credit covers 100% of what you pay a contractor for qualifying vegetation work, up to a cap. No application and no waiting list — you claim it on your state return.

Income limits apply, and the cap rises in 2027.

C.R.S. 39-22-543 · Colorado Dept. of Revenue, Income Tax Topics: Wildfire Mitigation Measures

Your insurer

Since 1 July 2026, if your carrier uses a wildfire risk score to price your policy, they have to give you that score in writing every year — and a discount for mitigation you can document.

You can ask for a re-score once the work is done.

Colorado HB25-1182, effective 1 July 2026
