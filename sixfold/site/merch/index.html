Merch & branding — concept board

Things that can carry the mark.

Shirts, caps, patches, stickers, and the pieces that go on a truck and a job site. These are directions for review, not a shop.

  • Not a store. No prices and no order flow — the launch offer and the rate card are still open, so nothing here commits to a number.
  • Mark shown is Ridge, which every Sixfold asset uses. Sweep is still formally an open call.
  • "Land & Fire" is the settled descriptor — it is printed on the business cards and the vehicle magnets.
  • Nothing claims "licensed and insured", "certified", "bonded" or "Firewise" — none of that is true yet. The hi-vis shirt is a graphic direction only; a real crew garment has to meet the visibility spec the work calls for.
  • No web address is printed anywhere — the domain isn't hosted. Contact pieces carry the phone and email instead.
  • Colorways use the agreed five: Pine Black, Ember, Moss, Bone, Timber.
  • Mockups are rendered vector, not photos — for form and placement, not final art.
