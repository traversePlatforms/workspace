## Register rules applied to all five

From [../marketing/marketing-plan.md](../marketing/marketing-plan.md) §1 and §2, and [../marketing/platform-sheet.md](../marketing/platform-sheet.md):

- **Information first.** The change in the law, what it means, here is the thing you can read yourself. No urgency theatre, no fear selling, no premium promises.
- **No "licensed and insured"**, no certification or NFPA/Firewise claim, anywhere — bios and posts included.
- **No county cost-share named** until Sixfold is on an approved list.
- **No aeration.** Fire is the only line described.
- **Tax and insurance language is descriptive, never advisory**, and every post points the reader to their own accountant or carrier.
- **No implied track record.** Sixfold has no completed jobs, no portfolio and no reviews yet, and none of these posts pretend otherwise.
- **Nextdoor punishes selling.** Four of the five close on an offer to explain something rather than an offer to quote.

**Contact: still deliberately left out, but the reason changed (2026-09-18).** Sixfold now has `Contact@SixfoldColorado.com`, so the brand-separation problem is gone — social posts still carry no contact line because a post is not a handout, not because the only address was a Surge one. The original note: printing `m.sharpe@surgeftw.com` on Sixfold content re-creates the brand-separation problem the platform sheet flags. These wait for a `sixfoldland.com` address.

## What the research turned up that changes other files

Three corrections came out of verifying these posts against enacted statute. All three affect files outside this folder:

1. **The 2027 tax credit change is narrower than our files say.** [../research/fire-mitigation-market-research.md](../research/fire-mitigation-market-research.md) and [../marketing/marketing-plan.md](../marketing/marketing-plan.md) both record that HB26-1289 "added thinning woody vegetation" as qualifying work. Thinning was **already** qualifying. What the bill adds is a separate, narrower category — thinning of vegetation **at risk of or killed by mountain pine beetle or spruce beetle**. The marketing plan calls this "precisely Sixfold's core service"; that sentence overstates it.
2. **The credit is currently neither refundable nor carry-forward** — excess over your tax liability is simply lost, until tax year 2027. This is commercially important and appears in none of our files.
3. **The HB26-1289 fiscal note contradicts the enacted act** on refundability. Don't use fiscal notes for this bill.

Full detail and citations in the fact-check log of [02-tax-credit-post.md](02-tax-credit-post.md).
