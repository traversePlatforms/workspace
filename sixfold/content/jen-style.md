**Each month, before drafting:**
1. Run `node traverse/post-calendar/tools/jen-learn.mjs report`. It lists every post handed over, what Jen changed (captions, pictures, dates), her change requests, removed posts, and what she approved untouched.
2. Add what it shows to the log below. Turn anything she does twice into a rule.
3. Draft the next month against the rules. Load it with `pc.mjs import`, which records what was handed over in `traverse/post-calendar/loaded.jsonl`.

A change she makes once is a note. Twice is a rule. Approved untouched means more of the same.

## Rules so far
- **Captions:**
  - One or two short sentences.
  - Then the contact line, "Message us here, or text a picture of your lawn to (720) 830-4553 for a free quote." On Instagram: "Send us a DM, or text...".
  - Instagram ends with #coloradolawn #lawncare #frontrange.
- **No labels like "Saturday photo day"** on photo sets (Matthew, 16 Nov).
- **No urgency or scarcity** ("books up fast", "limited spots") unless she asks for it.
- **Real job photos only, never AI.** No readable house numbers, street signs, plates, faces, or another company's branding. Location data stripped.
- **Photo sets are 3–4 photos,** 4:3 at 1440x1080.
- **Images stay seasonless.** Captions may be seasonal.
- **Schedule:** Tue/Thu 3 pm singles, Sat 2 pm photo sets. She can change this under Posting days; follow whatever it shows.
