## Corrected or changed while building

- **The 10 Nov real-work post first said "All those little dots are soil plugs"** over Brad's 19 Oct drone shot. Dropped: at full size the
  dots could be tufts of grass on a thin lawn, nobody has confirmed they are cores, and the lawn reads brown. Replaced with a green front
  lawn from the 23 Oct job and no result claim.
- **"A thick mat is better cleared"** came off the 5 Nov picture. No CSU page read today says that in so many words; the caption keeps
  only the sourced fact (snow mold appears where leaves and debris piled up).
- **"Water the day before" vs "a couple of days before".** PlantTalk 1505 now says "thoroughly watered the day before aerating". The Oct
  365 post 3 ("Water a couple of days before") and the ad kit say a couple of days. Not used in any November caption; worth matching the
  older posts to the current page before they run again.

## Left out (unsourced, conflicting or risky)

- **"Trees taken out" on the landscaping post.** The 365 site says it, but `sixfold/README.md` records that Denver requires a Tree
  Service Company licence (DRMC Ch. 57) to prune or remove a tree for pay anywhere in the city. Matthew confirmed 31 Oct: it stays out.
- **How common snow mold is on the Front Range.** PlantTalk says it "can occur on many Front Range lawns"; a 2007 CSU news release says
  gray snow mold is generally uncommon here. They conflict and the release is old, so no frequency is given.
- **"Too thick a leaf cover smothers the turf"** (Arapahoe County Master Gardener, seen only as a search snippet). Not opened, not used.
- **How much water to give a lawn in winter.** 7.211 gives gallons for trees and shrubs, not lawns.
- **Denver Water fines.** Reports differ ($500 then $1,000, vs up to $2,000). Not needed, left out.
- **Spring as the best time to seed.** CSU PlantTalk 1516 says cool-season grass establishes more easily in late summer, and 1550 calls
  September a good time to overseed. The 3 Nov post says spring is the next window for *aeration* and does not claim spring is ideal for seed.
- **"The spring schedule is filling up" or any booking deadline.** No such fact; booking early is offered, not pushed.
- **How long plugs take to break down.** No source gives a time; captions say "untidy at first" only where needed.
