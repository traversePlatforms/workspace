**Preheader:** Winter watering, leaves, and getting on the spring list.

Hi [first name],

Thanks for having us out this season. Here are a few things that will help your lawn come through the winter.

### Water a little this winter

Colorado winters are dry, and a lawn can dry out under no snow. CSU Extension's advice:
- Water only when the air and soil are above 40°F and there's no snow on the lawn.
- Water at midday, so it soaks in before it freezes at night.
- During long dry spells with no snow, that's about once or twice a month.

If we seeded your lawn this fall, this matters most. New grass is the most at risk, along with south- and west-facing lawns and windy spots. Check your water provider's rules before you water.

### Mulch the leaves, don't smother the lawn

You can mow leaves straight into the lawn: highest mower setting, bag off, at least two passes. If you can't see grass through the leaves, there are too many to mulch, so rake some off first. Whole leaves left on the lawn can smother it.

### Keep mowing at the same height

Keep mowing at 2½ to 3 inches while the grass is still growing. There's no need to cut it shorter in the fall. Take off no more than a third of the blade at a time.
