  • Since August 2024 it covers only what you pay a third-party contractor — buying or renting kit and doing it yourself does not qualify.
  • For work done before 2027 the credit cannot exceed what you owe. The remainder is not refunded and cannot be carried forward.
  • Any cost paid or reimbursed by a grant does not qualify. Programmes combine; they never double-dip.

Not tax advice, and we do not file anyone’s return. Your accountant is the right person to confirm any of this.

Why we keep mentioning it

The state’s own evaluators went looking for why the number is 450 and found the answer was simply that people have not been told. That is a strange thing to be true of money.
