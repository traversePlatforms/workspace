Fresh green pine slash is habitat. A pile of green rounds sitting ten feet from healthy trees through the wrong months is doing something you did not intend.

Chip it
Destroys the inner bark the larvae live in.
Scatter it
Spread thin so it dries fast, rather than piled.
Haul it off

Round it
Cut and season it well away from standing trees.

Never stack green or infested conifer wood next to living conifers. Piling it and forgetting it is the only genuinely bad option of the five.

Which is why we would rather come in winter

The beetles are not flying, the ground holds equipment better, and anyone doing this work has an open calendar between November and February that they will not have in April.

Sources: CSU Extension fact sheet 5.558, Ips Beetles (Cranshaw & Leatherman) — flight period, generations, and the stacking rule. CSFS / CSU fact sheet 5.528, Mountain Pine Beetle — emergence window and both peaks, read first-hand 14 September 2026.
The “only cut October to April” rule that circulates with this topic is not in either fact sheet and is not claimed here. The months above are flight periods, which are.
