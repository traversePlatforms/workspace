## Left out, and why (Matthew or Jen can put any back)

- **"Don't mow for 4–7 days."** CSU gives no number. The other guides found say wait until the new grass is about 3 inches, roughly 2–4 weeks, which conflicts with 4–7 days. Left out rather than pick one.
- **"The fertilizers are active for 60 days."** Depends on the product. If the bag's label says 60 days, it can go back in as "the fertilizer we used keeps working for about 60 days".
- **"Don't lay any fertilizer … for 2 months."** CSU says nothing against fertilizer on new seed (its seeding guide uses a starter fertilizer); the weed-killer half is kept, sourced.
- **"Bag clippings if possible."** CSU PlantTalk 1504, *Grass Clippings*, says the opposite: leave them, they feed the lawn; only big clumps should come off.

Sources: planttalk.colostate.edu 1505, 1516, 1513, 1504 (fetched 19 Oct 2026); ask.extension.org (CSU, Jefferson County).
