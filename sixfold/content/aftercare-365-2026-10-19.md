## The message (full package: aerate, seed and feed)

> Hi Kirk, this is Jen with 365 Aeration & Seeding. Thanks for having us!
>
> Here's what we did:
> Core aerated the lawn
> Spot sprayed the large weeds
> Laid seed with a root and soil conditioner
>
> A few notes:
>
> Leave the plugs on the lawn. They break down back into the grass on their own.
>
> Give the lawn a good watering soon, then water lightly and often enough to keep the soil surface moist while the new seed comes up. Germination can take up to two weeks.
>
> Once the new grass is up and rooting, water less often but more deeply.
>
> Don't mow for 4 to 7 days.
>
> The fertilizer we put down stays active for about 60 days, so don't lay any fertilizer or weed killer for 2 months while the seed comes in.
>
> Bag your clippings if you can.
>
> How quickly the lawn recovers depends on how much damage it had, and it can take a few months.
>
> This adds to your regular lawn care, so keep up the basics: pull weeds, clean up debris regularly, and water when you can.
>
> You'll see a charge on your card ending in 3312 for the remaining balance of $249. Thanks so much for your business, cheers to a healthy lawn! 🌱
