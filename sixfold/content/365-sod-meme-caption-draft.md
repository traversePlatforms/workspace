## Lines cut, and why

- **"80% cheaper than sod."** Left out of the caption. The video already says it, and nothing here backs the number yet (see the fact-check log).
- **"Free quote."** The Sixfold rule is that "free quote" is fine on printed sheets but not on the website. I kept it off the social post too, to be safe.
- **"Book now for fall."** Late October is the end of the season. Whether 365 is still taking fall jobs is Jen's call, so I left out any timing.
- **"Green in two weeks."** No promise about how fast seed comes up. It depends on watering, and 2026 water restrictions hit overseeding harder than aeration.
