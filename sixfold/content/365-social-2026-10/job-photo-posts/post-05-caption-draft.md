## Facebook

Take off a third, no more.

A simple mowing rule for Colorado lawns: never cut more than a third of the grass blade at once, and keep the mower somewhere between 2 and 3½ inches. Cutting shorter than 2 inches makes grass less able to handle heat and drought. Leave the clippings; they break down quickly and put nutrients back. Keep mowing until the lawn stops growing.
