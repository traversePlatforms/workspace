## Facebook

Thin lawn? Check the soil.

A lot of lawn trouble in Colorado starts underground. When soil gets packed down, it squeezes out the little air pockets roots need, and water runs off instead of soaking in. Core aeration pulls plugs out of that packed soil so air, water and fertilizer can get down to the roots.
