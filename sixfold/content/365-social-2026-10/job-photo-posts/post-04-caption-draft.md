## Facebook

Keep the top of the soil damp.

New seed needs the surface of the soil to stay moist until it’s up, which can take up to two weeks. Water lightly and often, just enough to wet the top quarter inch without soaking it. Once the grass is up, ease off: every other day, then every third day.

Follow your town’s watering rules, and ask us if you’re not sure how they fit.
