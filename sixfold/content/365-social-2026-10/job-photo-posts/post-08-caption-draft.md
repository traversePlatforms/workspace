## Facebook

What to expect when we come.

A few things make the day go smoothly:

• Let us know about sprinklers, shallow pipes or invisible dog fences so we can mark them first.
• Leave the gates unlocked and keep pets inside.
• If the weather or the ground stops us (frozen, waterlogged or very dry), we’ll reach out to move the day.
• Need to change it? Call or text at least 24 hours ahead.
