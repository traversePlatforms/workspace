# What US aeration companies post to get calls (research, 2026-10-22)

Asked by Matthew after Brandon wanted the 365 posts aimed at sales. Done by a research agent; I checked the claims against the URLs it lists below.

## The limit

The best source would have been the **Meta Ad Library**: ads that run for months are ads that pay. It was blocked to us (HTTP 403), and so were Reddit and the Facebook/Instagram pages themselves. The evidence below comes from company **ad landing pages** (URLs containing "ppc-", "2025-offer" and "ffi-2025"), trade press and one vendor audit. **It shows the offers behind the ads, not the posts.**

A logged-in Ad Library search would close the gap: "aeration", US, active ads only, noting each ad's start date.

## Patterns

| 9 | Where the ad sends people | Vendor data | PipelineOn audit of 145 advertisers and 762 ads (June 2026): 50% to a website, 15% to a lead form, 12% "Call Now" |

## Cautions

- **Prices.** Every published price carries conditions. 365 has no flat rate (Matthew, 2026-09-28), so we skip this pattern.
- **Urgency has to be true.** The FTC's 2022 dark-patterns report names fake time limits.
- **No borrowed or AI-made results.** The FTC's fake-reviews rule took effect in 2024.
- **Dates.** Several offer pages still show deadlines that have passed.
