## 2. The one real conflict to be aware of

**Sixfold AI, Inc.** — New York, founded 2023, Series B, $51.5M raised from Salesforce Ventures and Brewer Lane Ventures. Builds generative AI for **insurance underwriting**; says it has processed 1.5M+ submissions across 50+ lines of business in North America, South America, the UK, Europe and Australia. It is registered as a foreign corporation in Colorado and holds sixfold.ai.

**Why this probably does not block you:** trademark rights are tied to goods and services classes and to actual market overlap. AI underwriting software sits in the software and technology classes; land clearing, forestry and fire mitigation sit in construction services and agriculture/forestry services. Two businesses can hold the same name in unrelated classes, and routinely do.

**Why it still deserves the attorney call:**
- They are well funded, which means they are likely to have filed and likely to enforce.
- Your strongest marketing angle is **insurance-driven** — HB25-1182, risk scores, mitigation discounts. That is content, not services, so it does not change your class. But it does mean Sixfold marketing copy and an insurance-AI company's name will both be appearing in the phrase "wildfire" and "insurance." Worth twenty minutes of a trademark attorney's time before anything gets wrapped on a truck.

**Not a conflict:** Sixfold Wonder LLC (Erie, CO) — different name, no trade overlap. The withdrawn 2009 California entity is dead.
