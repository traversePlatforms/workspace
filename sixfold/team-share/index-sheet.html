What is in it
and what is not

Everything here is yours to open without asking.

Seven folders, numbered in the order you are likely to need them. The Sixfold 365 app is where the work is run — your schedule, each job card, Done with your time, job photos and the aftercare message all live there. The paper here is for walks and jobs that need more detail than the app holds, for customers who sign on paper, and for when there is no signal. New crew: start with the Field Guide beside this file. If something is not here, the last page says why.

01Agreements

When a job is agreed · read the crib sheet first

  • Lawn job sheet — one lawn job, three pages. For aeration, mowing and the small work. Page 1 is the agreement; 2 and 3 are the cancellation forms.
  • Lawn care agreement — monthly. Four pages, and it runs month to month until the customer cancels. Section 4 is boxed: read it to them. It says how they stop it.
  • Service agreement — fire mitigation and — landscaping. Five pages each. Print all five: pages 4 and 5 are the customer’s two cancellation forms.
  • Tablet version, pages 1–3, for typing on a screen. Pages 4 and 5 still have to be paper.
  • Two crib sheets — signing at the door, and emailing it. Internal. They are not the same rules.
02Job paperwork

Walks and bigger jobs · print or fill in on a screen

  • Quote walk sheet, two versions — fill in on screen, or print and write on. Counts only: no price is given on the property.
  • Job close-out sheet. The day the job ends. Same rows as the walk sheet, so the two can be compared.
  • What the work takes and what things cost us. Internal. A marked guess beats a blank.
03Safety

Read once properly · then when something changes

  • Crew safety and compliance. What OSHA requires of us, which licences and certifications apply, and what is not held yet. The one worth your time.
  • Gear checklist. What goes on the truck.
04For the customer

Print and hand over · or email

  • Preparing your home for wildfire. The pamphlet. Most of it the homeowner can do themselves, which is the point.
  • Who pays for wildfire mitigation. The tax credit and the insurance law, on one page.
  • Referral packet — blank template. What goes in the folder at the end of a job.
05Money

Before you answer a question about credits

  • Tax credit reference notebook. What to say, and a never-say list. Read that list even if you read nothing else.
  • Tax credit — forms the customer needs.
  • Script kit. What to say and what never to say.
06Look it up

Mid-job · when you need an address

  • Debris disposal directory. Every confirmed site, cheapest first. Phone ahead: rates move, and several sites bar commercial haulers.
  • Mitigation funding directory. The programmes, by county.
07Events

Events · take a clipboard and a pen that works

  • Event sign-up sheet. Name, phone, email, ZIP code and a Free quote tick box. Ticking it is their OK for us to call, text or email about the quote, and the sheet says so. Thirteen people per sheet.

What is not here

And why — so nobody re-adds it by accident

Shared folder
Page 2 of 2

Two of these are not housekeeping. Read them.

The agreements are in folder 01, and the paperwork is what bites — not the wording. If a cancellation form goes out with the date left blank or wrong, the three-day clock never starts and the job stays cancellable for three years, with no payment for work already done. The contract itself is correct; the risk is entirely in filling it in. Read the crib sheet in that folder before you send or sign one — there are two, one for the door and one for email, and they are different.

Don’t promise a price, a discount or a deal. Prices and any discount come from the office, per job, through the app and in writing. A number said at a door or on a walk becomes the number the customer holds us to.

  • Prices of any kind — rate cards, price lists, sample invoices. Prices come from the office, per job.
  • Anything naming a real customer. Past quotes and signed agreements name people, addresses and prices. They are not here and should not be forwarded. Job photos live in the separate Photo content dump drive.
  • The old Sixfold look. The services sheet, the September flyers, door hangers, the old business cards and the magnet and colour proofs were replaced by the Sixfold 365 cards, yard signs and website. Don’t print anything carrying the orange “Land & Fire” look. The old copies are in this drive’s archive folder, for the record only.
  • The crew shirt spec. It still shows the old design, so it stays out until it is redone with the Sixfold 365 look.
  • Business planning and brand print files. Not crew material, and the print-ready cards, signs and magnets stay with Matthew so nothing gets ordered straight from here.

If something here looks wrong

Say so rather than working around it. Several documents in this folder were corrected the day it was built, and the reason they had gone stale is that they sat in a folder nobody re-read. A document that disagrees with what you were told in person is worth a text message.

These are copies. The originals live in the project files, and this folder gets rebuilt from them — so a correction made properly reaches everyone, and a correction scribbled on a copy reaches nobody.
