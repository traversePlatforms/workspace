Business cards

Printed at 3.5 × 2 in, actual size, so what you see is what arrives. The dashed line is the 0.125 in safe margin — nothing important crosses it, because trimming moves. Add a further 0.125 in bleed on every edge when you send artwork.

Roots are on, and they are card-only. The three wavy root strokes need the mark at roughly 0.8 in to survive: at that size the thinnest is 0.67pt, which offset and digital hold on either stock. They are 5–10× under a screen-print floor at any wearable size, which is why the shirt drops them and the card keeps them.

The opacity problem does not apply here. That was a screen-printing limit. Cards print full-colour digital or offset, which holds the mark's layering exactly as drawn — so cards are not waiting on the one-colour garment mark.
