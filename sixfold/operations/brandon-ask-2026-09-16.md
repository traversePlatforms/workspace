# The one ask to Brandon — hosting, email, insurance

**Built:** 2026-09-16 · **Status:** draft, not sent · **From:** Matthew · **To:** Brandon
**Register:** a message between the two of them. Short, one decision, no alarm.

Why one message and not four: each of these has been "waiting on Brandon" separately, and each one on
its own is easy to put off. Together they are a single yes and a single card number, and together they
unblock everything printed, the Springs market, and the crew's email.

**Blanks to fill before sending:** `[registrar]` — where SixfoldColorado.com and the Contact@ mailbox
currently live; `[n]` — how many people get a mailbox.

## The message

Brandon — four things I need a yes on, all at once because they hold each other up. Two are free, one
is about $7 a head a month, and two need an insurance quote. I can do all the setup; I need your OK and
a card for the paid ones.

**1. Website hosting — $0.** The site is built. Cloudflare Pages, free plan, more than we'll use.
I'll set it up on the current domain without touching the email — the Contact@ address stays exactly
as it is. Once it's live the URL is real, and the cards, flyers, referral packet and stickers can go
to print. All of those are designed and waiting on this.

**2. Email for the team — about $7 per person per month.** Google Workspace on SixfoldColorado.com,
so it's brandon@, brad@, cai@ instead of everyone on Contact@. `[n]` people. Confirm the price on
Google's page before I commit — that figure is from memory, not from a quote.

**3. Workers' comp.** Required by the state from the first person on payroll, including part-time
and family. We don't have it. The fine runs per day. Any licensed carrier will write it; Pinnacol is
required to take any Colorado employer, so nobody can turn us down. **I don't have a premium figure —
that's a call to an agent.**

**4. General liability — $1,000,000.** Not a state law, but the Colorado Springs tree licence needs
it, so does a pesticide licence if we ever spray, and so does every customer who asks. Same agent,
same call as 3.

**What 3 and 4 unlock:** the Colorado Springs tree service licence — $110 a year, $1M GL, $100k WC,
a 40-question exam. Without it we can't touch a tree over 15 ft inside city limits. That's our own
market.

**What I need from you:**
- Yes to 1 and 2, and a card for 2.
- Either you call the agent for 3 and 4, or tell me who to call and I'll get the quotes to you.

I'll take the licence application from there.

## What is deliberately not in the message

- **No premium numbers for WC or GL.** Nothing on file is sourced; the compliance guide cites only
  the state's pages. A guessed figure would be the first thing he remembers and the first thing to be
  wrong.
- **No fine amounts.** C.R.S. 8-43-409 is $250/day, $250–500/day after the first — real, but it reads
  as a threat in a message to the owner. "Per day" is enough; the guide has the citation if he asks.
- **No "we are currently breaking the law."** True if anyone is on payroll; the message says what is
  required and that it is not held, and stops there.
- **Nothing about OSHA items 4–6** (first-aider, hearing program, PPE assessment). Those cost nothing
  and need no approval — Matthew does them, not Brandon.
- **Nothing about the pesticide decision.** Separate question; only mentioned as a reason GL matters.
