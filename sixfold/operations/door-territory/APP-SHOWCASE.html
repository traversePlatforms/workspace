How it makes every hour at the doors count

The right doors first

  • Scored before the knock: fire hazard, lawn size and trees on every door, from public records.
  • One filter shows only the doors with all three drivers.
  • Sorts by what sells: lawn, fire, trees, doors left, and our own booking odds as results build.
  • Around our jobs: the doors nearest every job we win, with a "we're doing your neighbour's yard" opener.

No wasted trips

  • Nearest town and nearest door from where you stand.
  • Walking routes through a neighbourhood, opened in Google Maps.
  • Come-back dates: not-now doors return on the day they asked for; no-answers return after two days.
  • No-soliciting flags for a door or a whole neighbourhood, so nobody knocks there twice.

Every yes captured

  • One tap opens everything: walk, phone, email, photos, note, extra work, fire check.
  • The walk is booked at the door, with the day's other walks shown so they don't collide, a calendar reminder, and a confirmation text.
  • Works with no signal: saves wait on the phone and send themselves later.
  • Extra work spotted (gutters, branches over the roof, dead trees) turns one door into several jobs.

A sale the homeowner can afford

  • The money line on every door: the state tax credit, the insurance law, and the county's grant situation.
  • What to say and what not to say, one tap away.
  • Water rules by town: aeration needs the lawn watered before and after; seed needs far more; and on most rural lots a well may not water a lawn at all.
  • The homeowner fire report: a before-and-after check they can send their insurer.

The office picks it up instantly

  • One pipeline for door bookings and website, Meta, Nextdoor and phone enquiries: to call, walk booked, quote sent, won, lost.
  • Quote builder prices the job from the walk, with the lawn measured from the aerial photo.
  • Fire-weather call list when an alert or a wildfire hits a town we work.

We learn what works

  • Results: which kinds of doors turn into jobs, by fire, lawn, trees, value and town.
  • Team stats and a leaderboard, without location tracking.
  • Territories ready for when the team grows.

Customers kept, work repeats

  • Every customer in one list: contact, plan, price, every visit, what they have spent and when they are due next.
  • Due this week on top, overdue flagged, so a monthly lawn or a yearly aeration never slips.
  • One tap from a won job or a won lead: name, address and price carry over, and the door is tagged so nobody knocks it cold.
  • Nothing is overwritten, and nothing is lost. Every change is kept as its own line, a backup saves itself on the server every night (the last 30 are kept), and admins are reminded to download one to a device each week.
The real app runs on the Sixfold website for signed-in crew. Door data comes from county parcel records, the USDA Forest Service wildfire hazard map, USDA aerial photos, USGS lidar and state and county funding sources. This page uses invented properties.
