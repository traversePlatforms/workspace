## Why it's built the way it is

| Decision | Reason |
|---|---|
| **Two groups: "already completed" vs "future work"** | The state tax credit and the insurance discount are claimed after the fact. The Douglas County cost-share, EQIP and Forest-Ag classification all require approval / enrolment **before** work — useless for the completed job, worth knowing for a next phase. Presenting them as one flat list would send Lonnie after money he can't get for this job. |
| **Douglas County only for local programs** | Request: "statewide and for the region he lives in — Sedalia. Don't include surrounding areas he can't claim." Sedalia is unincorporated Douglas County. Everything county-specific from the internal directory (Boulder, Grand, Eagle, Larimer, Routt, Summit, El Paso, Jefferson, SW/WAP, Delta) is excluded. |
| **Douglas County cost-share named, but framed as the county's program** | The Sixfold standing constraint ("no county cost-share by name until on the approved contractor list") exists to stop *Sixfold marketing* implying a cost-share association. This is a factual client resource: it routes Lonnie to the county, states the two-bids-from-the-county-list requirement, and says plainly "the county runs it — Sixfold does not administer it." No claim that Sixfold is on the list. **Flag for Matthew:** if he'd rather this program not appear on a Sixfold-attributed doc at all, it's one card to cut. |
| **Light attribution, no logo, no sell** | "Prepared by Sixfold Colorado for Lonnie." Body reads as a resource guide. Approved framing only on Sixfold's role ("every job comes with the paperwork" → itemised invoice, written scope, before/after photos); **"we handle the paperwork for you" not used**; "Sixfold does not prepare or file returns / will not contact your insurer" stated explicitly. |
| **New phone (303) 915-1045** | Per Matthew's 2026-09-08 instruction. The old `funding-one-pager` still carries (720) 772-6146 — separate sweep. |
| **"What won't apply" section** | Saves Lonnie chasing disaster programs, the expired subtraction, the repealed Resilient Homes Grant, other counties, and entity-only grants. |

## Fact-check log

### Verified (primary source or enacted statute — carried from the internal directory's 2026-09-08 log unless noted)

- **State wildfire-mitigation income tax credit** — C.R.S. 39-22-543; 100% of qualifying contractor cost; cap **$1,000 TY2025 and TY2026**, **$2,000 TY2027–2030** and **refundable** in those years; income limit **AGI ≤ $300,000 from 2027**, **2026 FTI limit not yet published** by Revenue; **third-party contractor costs only** since HB24-1036 (Aug 2024); grant/incentive-funded costs excluded (the stacking rule); **no WUI test** ("real property located in Colorado"); claimed on **DR 0104CR**. Qualifying measures: defensible space, fuel breaks, thinning woody vegetation to reduce risk to structures, and secondary slash treatment (lop-and-scatter, pile, chip, remove, prescribed burn) to CSFS/DFPC standard. Sources: CO Dept of Revenue *Income Tax Topics: Wildfire Mitigation Measures* / *Wildfire Tax Benefits*; HB26-1289 Final Act amending the statute; CO General Assembly 2026 tax-expenditure evaluation 2026-TE4. Full working notes: `sixfold/tax-credit-notebook/README.md`.
- **Insurance — HB25-1182**, effective **1 July 2026**: annual written wildfire-risk-score disclosure; disclosure of available mitigation discounts; parcel-level mitigation incorporated into scoring/pricing; a discount for documented mitigation even absent a formal model; right to appeal the score; **no statutory discount percentage**. Colorado **FAIR Plan** launched **April 2025**, up to **$750,000** residential, actual-cash-value basis. Sources: leg.colorado.gov/bills/hb25-1182; CO Division of Insurance; FAIR Plan (secondary).
- **Douglas County Wildfire Mitigation Cost-Share** — **50%** match, **up to $25,000** individual property, **$50,000** community, **$7,000** community chipping/slash; **2026 application window 13 April – 13 May**; second window anticipated late 2026; selected projects notified in the fall; **pre-approval required** — apply and be selected before work; county inspects on completion and releases its share after proof the homeowner paid their share; application needs a scope of work, a site map, and **two estimates from the county's approved contractor list**. Sources: douglasco.gov Wildfire Mitigation Cost-Share Program page (per the internal directory; the site 403s to automated fetch — re-confirmed via search summary 2026-09-09); *Denver Gazette* 2026-04-01; Denver7 (older $5,000 individual cap and 2023 "84 projects / 750 acres" figures noted as superseded by the 2026 terms).
- **COSWAP landowner cost-share** — Colorado Strategic Wildfire Action Program (CO DNR / CSFS / DFPC). Reimburses a **private landowner up to 50%** of a mitigation project; **free home wildfire risk assessment first**; **"only work begun after an application is approved is eligible for reimbursement"**; **mapped program area only**, areas change each cycle. **Douglas County is a named COSWAP focus area** (alongside Boulder, El Paso, Jefferson, Larimer, La Plata, Teller), and Douglas County itself administers "COSWAP Cash Grant" invoicing (douglascounty.legistar.com) — so some COSWAP money reaches Douglas County landowners, possibly *through* the county cost-share program rather than a separate application. The doc states this uncertainty and routes Lonnie to the county / a CSFS forester. Sources: dnr.colorado.gov CO Strategic Wildfire Action Program; Governor's Office COSWAP updates (5-year / $47M); lpcgov.org COSWAP page; search-summary confirmation 2026-09-09. COSWAP's other tracks (workforce-development grants to fire corps/inmate crews; community project grants) are **not** individual-landowner money — consistent with the internal directory.
- **Douglas County Slash-Mulch site** — located in Sedalia at 5675 Delva Way; generally Saturdays 7 a.m.–5 p.m. (Town of Castle Rock / Douglas County notice via Nextdoor, 2026.) **Cut from the client doc at Matthew's instruction** (rev-2) — kept here and in the disposal directory only.
- **NRCS EQIP** — USDA cost-share for agricultural producers and owners of non-industrial private forestland; wildfire practices include Brush Management (314), fuel breaks, forest stand improvement, thinning, prescribed burn; **apply before the practice**; standard subdivided house lot generally does not qualify. Source: nrcs.usda.gov EQIP Colorado.
- **CSFS Forest Ag property-tax classification** — agricultural valuation for **≥ 40 forested acres** under a CSFS-approved forest management plan managed for wood-product profit; apply by **1 October**; enacted by **HB90-1229**; CSFS recommends eligible parcels to the county assessor annually. Source: csfs.colostate.edu/forest-ag-program.
- **Ended / retired items** — the state wildfire-mitigation income tax **subtraction** (DR 0104AD) expired after **TY2024**; the **Colorado Wildfire Resilient Homes Grant** was **repealed by HB26-1395**, effective **1 July 2026**. Sources: 2026-TE4; leg.colorado.gov/bills/HB26-1395.
- **Sedalia is unincorporated Douglas County** — no separate municipal wildfire program. Consistent with all sources; Sedalia has no incorporated town government.

### Completeness re-check (2026-09-09, Matthew asked "make sure those are the only resources")

Cross-checked the internal `mitigation-funding-directory.md` against fresh searches for a
**Douglas County individual property owner**. Result: the six programs in the doc (state
tax credit, insurance discount, Douglas County cost-share, COSWAP, NRCS EQIP, CSFS Forest-Ag)
are the complete set. Confirmed **not** available / correctly excluded:

- **CSFS competitive grants** (FRWRM, CWDG, Urban & Community Forestry, Wildfire Mitigation
  Outreach) — the CSFS grants page states plainly *"projects that benefit only one landowner
  do not qualify."* Entity-only. Named in the doc's "won't apply" list.
- **Other CSFS landowner programs** — Forest Legacy (conservation easements), Forest
  Stewardship (free management-plan help, no cash), Tree Farm (certification) — none is
  mitigation cost-recovery.
- **Central Colorado Wildfire Mitigation Program** — Chaffee and Lake Counties only.
- **Wildfire Partners / WRAP** — Boulder County / City of Boulder only (a stale secondary
  source calls it "statewide"; it is not).
- **Declared Wildfire Disaster Rebuild sales/use tax refund** (HB23-1240) and **casualty-loss
  deductions / FEMA IA / SBA disaster loans** — all require a *declared disaster* and
  *damage*, not preventive work.
- **The expired state tax *subtraction*** and the **repealed Wildfire Resilient Homes Grant** —
  still listed by stale contractor/aggregator pages; both named in "won't apply".
- **FEMA HMGP / BRIC** — local-government sub-applicant only.

No Douglas-County-specific homeowner program exists beyond the cost-share (no separate
individual chipping voucher; the $7,000 chipping pot is *community* only).
