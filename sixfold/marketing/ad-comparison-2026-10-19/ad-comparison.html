What the Messenger ad is leaving on the table

36 conversations, about 4 in Jen's list. Only four Messenger conversations reached the app as leads (three more, Jim, Rob and Vernon, are tagged "Facebook" and may be Messenger too). That leaves roughly 29 to 32 people who messaged the page and are not being followed up in the pipeline. If even a few of them book, the Messenger ad's cost per job drops fast: at five jobs it would be $86, at eight $54, level with the form.
  • Log every Messenger conversation as a lead (Quick quote, source Messenger) so none drops off, and so this comparison stays honest.
  • Split "Facebook" in Jen's ClickUp into "Facebook Messenger" and "Facebook Form"; the app then sorts every lead into the right column by itself.
  • Per day the two cost about the same (form about $85, Messenger about $86), and the form brought in 21 leads in under two days against Messenger's 36 conversations in five. Until Messenger's conversations are being worked, the form is the cheaper way to buy jobs, on less than two days of running.
  • Look again on Monday 26 October, when Jeremy, Randy and Sreenivas are done and paid, and there is another week of both ads.
