## Cut, and why
- **The full aftercare list.** Jen's text has it. A second copy two days later gets skimmed, so it's cut to the one line that matters at day 2.
- **The plan's price ($199, or $94 if set up the same day).** By day 2 the "today" offer is over. Putting two prices in writing invites a mix-up. Let Jen quote it when they reply.
- **"Reviews help other people find us"** and similar why-it-matters lines. Cut to keep it short.
- **Any reason why spring aeration helps.** That would be a claim, and it would need a source (CSU Extension) before it goes in. It's left out, so the line is only an offer.

## Decisions for Matthew and Jen
1. **A discount for leaving a review: don't.** Google's review rules don't allow offering anything in exchange for a review. The FTC's 2024 rule on consumer reviews also bans buying reviews, and bans offering anything for reviews that depends on what the review says. That's why the 27 Oct decision was 10% off for **every** customer, never tied to a review. A discount for **booking**, either a follow-up visit or the monthly plan, is fine.
   - I'm going from what I know of those rules; I didn't look them up today. Check them before any discount wording changes.
2. **Spring aeration list:** is Jen keeping one? If not, change the line to "reply and we'll call you when spring booking opens".
3. **The plan price** in the email, or left for Jen to quote? I left it out.
4. **Version B's list:** all customers, or only those who haven't booked again yet?

## Before it can send
- **No mailbox is connected for Sixfold 365.** The app's follow-up email, receipts and aftercare can't send until a mailbox app password is set up. The 23 Oct note says contact@365aeration.com would need 2-step verification, and that would disrupt Jen's sign-in. A separate sending address avoids that, e.g. hello@ or noreply@365aeration.com, with replies going to contact@.
- **The plan and discount lines make this a marketing email.** US law (CAN-SPAM) needs a real postal address and a way to opt out in the footer. Fill in the street address. The app should also skip anyone who says stop.
- **The app's follow-up email is built and switched off** (Admin › Follow-up email). Once you two settle the wording, I swap this text into it, show you the preview, and it stays off until you switch it on.
- **Version B is a one-time send** to a list. It needs your go for the send itself.
