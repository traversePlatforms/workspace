Help paying for it

Colorado tax credit $1,000

100% of qualifying vegetation work, up to $1,000, with an income limit. $2,000 and refundable from tax year 2027. About 450 people in the whole state claimed it last time.

Your insurer, since 1 Jul 2026

An insurer using a wildfire risk score to price or non-renew your policy must now send you that score yearly, say what discounts exist, and discount documented mitigation. You can appeal it.

What you get from us The file

An itemised invoice separating the qualifying work, plus before-and-after photographs — what your accountant and your insurer will ask for. Whether you qualify is their call; we don’t give tax advice.
