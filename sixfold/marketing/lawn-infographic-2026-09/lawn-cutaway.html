Sixfold Colorado · Core lawn aeration

Your lawn's problem is
three inches down.

Front Range ground packs hard. Here is what that does, and what pulling a few thousand plugs out of it fixes.

1
It sheds water

Packed ground turns a sprinkler into runoff. It is the patch that goes brown first every year.

2
A punch, not a drill

Hollow tines take a core two to three inches down, two to four inches apart.

3
The plugs stay put

They look untidy for a couple of weeks, then break down back into the lawn.

4
Seed falls in, not on

Overseed straight after and it lands in an open hole. Water, air and roots follow it down.

Spring and fall are the windows. It can be done any time the ground is not frozen, and not when it is baked dry. Water it a couple of days before — dry ground will not give up a deep plug.
