### How the animation works, and how to get a video out of it

Every animation in `motion.html` runs for exactly the same 6 seconds, and freezing is one CSS variable: `.frozen` pauses every keyframe and sets `animation-delay: calc(var(--t) * -1s)`, so the whole drawing stops at the same instant. That is what lets the frames come out of the same file the preview plays — no second copy of the geometry, and no chance of the still and the loop drifting apart.

**To make the MP4:** import `motion/frame-01` … `frame-08` into CapCut in filename order, **0.75s each**, no transitions, loop twice for a 12-second ad. Same workflow as the video kit in `../aeration-video-2026-09/`. There is no ffmpeg on this machine, so the render still has to happen in CapCut.

**More frames if it looks choppy:** raise `FRAMES` in [build-loud.mjs](build-loud.mjs) and re-run. 16 frames at 0.375s each is smoother and takes about twice as long to build.

### Why the drawing is generated

Three passes, and each note moved it somewhere specific:

1. *"More visually striking"* → dark ground, bigger type, fewer words, the split as the idea.
2. *"More like a piece of marketing... some sort of deal or hire us"* → the chip, three proof points, and a call bar with the phone at display size.
3. *"More professional, less hand drawn, a little more fun"* → **this is the note that changed the method.** The first two boards drew everything with strokes, and a round-capped stroke reads as a sketch however carefully it is placed. The third is **generated**: roots are filled tapers from one function, so every root narrows on the same curve; holes, seed drops and tick marks are computed from a single depth scale; gradients and a dot pattern replace flat fills; and a real 0–3 inch ruler runs down the left edge — which is also the claim the whole ad rests on. A measured drawing reads as a company that measures things.

   The fun went into colour and copy rather than wobble: brighter turf, *"Give your lawn room to breathe"*, rounded light cards against the dark panel.

**Edit the script, not the HTML.** Every `.html` in this folder except `lawn-cutaway.html` and `lawn-split.html` is generated and says so at the top.

## What it is

A cutaway of a lawn, cut down the middle: **left side compacted, right side cored.** The drawing carries the argument and the words hang off it, which is the opposite of the 2026-09-14 board set — those are editorial boards built to be read, this one has to stop a thumb first.

Four numbered marks on the drawing, four notes underneath:

1. **It sheds water** — the runoff arrow on the left
2. **A punch, not a drill** — the hollow tine, mid-pull, with the core inside it
3. **The plugs stay put** — the core lying on the turf, grass still on top
4. **Seed falls in, not on** — seed dropping into open holes, roots running deep on that side

**The roots are the payoff.** They are the only thing that differs between the two halves: on the left they hit the compacted pan and turn sideways; on the right they run to the bottom of the frame. Nobody reads that consciously and everybody sees it.

**Contact block is the full set** — phone, website, email, towns, and the services line. This is the first Sixfold graphic to carry **SixfoldColorado.com**: every earlier one left the domain off because it did not resolve, and it has been live since 2026-09-21.

## What is deliberately not on it

- **No price, no minimum, no "free estimate."**
- **No "licensed and insured," certified or bonded.** None of it is true yet.
- **No guarantee** the lawn thickens or the seed takes — it depends on water and weather, and the agreement says so in writing.
- **No crew size, no capacity, no job count.** There is no aeration track record to imply one from.
- **No before-and-after photography.** There is no real Sixfold aeration job photographed.
- **No months.** No CSU source gives a Front Range window in months, so it says spring and fall and stops.

## Design notes

**Palette A**, as adopted for the site on 2026-09-24: green leads, ember appears only on the four markers and the seed. The contact bar is pine so the eye lands on it last and hardest.

**Three illustration-only colours** — `--soil`, `--soil-dark`, `--thatch` — plus one water blue. They are not brand colours, they never touch type, and they exist because you cannot draw a soil profile in five greens.

**The drawing is proportioned to the facts**, not decorated: cores 2–3 inches deep and 2–4 apart, hollow tines, cores left on the surface.
