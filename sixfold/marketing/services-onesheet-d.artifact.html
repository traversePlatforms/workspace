Tree work and fire mitigation — what we do

Hand and saw work on defensible space, from the wall out to the property line — and the written record that lets the state and your insurer pay toward some of it. Every estimate is free and in writing.

Trees

Standing timber, one at a time or by the stand

Felling Dropping a tree where there is room to drop it.
Full tree removal The whole tree gone — felled, limbed, bucked and cleared off the property.
Full tree trimming Crown raising, deadwood out, branches off the roof and back from the chimney. The 6–10 ft of clearance the defensible space guidance asks for.

Ground fuels

The layer that carries fire up into the trees

Full brush removal Undergrowth, ladder fuels, ground juniper and Gambel oak taken out from under the canopy.
Landscaping Putting the ground back once the fuel is off it — including the non-combustible surfacing the first five feet around a house is supposed to have.

By the acre

Defensible space · hand and saw work, Zones 1 and 2

Defensible space around the home The full acre worked to Colorado State Forest Service spacing — crowns apart, ladder fuels gone, the first five feet stripped.
Thinning by the acre Lightly wooded or heavy forest, priced and scheduled by what is actually standing on it.

What happens to the wood

Your call — and the choice that moves a quote most

Slashing Cut down and stacked into piles where you point, or lopped and scattered. Kept 30 ft or more from the house either way.
Chipping Run through the chipper on site. Chips spread, heaped where you want them, or taken away.
Left on site Cut to rounds and stacked, or left where it falls. Green wood needs a season before it burns.

Ask before we start. What happens to the material is usually the biggest single number in any quote, and most companies fold it in without saying so.

The paperwork

Two parties will pay toward this work — both want it documented

Support with the state tax credit An itemised invoice that separates the qualifying vegetation work, a written scope, and before-and-after photographs — what the claim needs attached. We cannot file your return and this is not tax advice; whether you qualify is your accountant’s call.
Help with your insurance premium Since 1 July 2026 a Colorado insurer using a wildfire risk score must tell you the score, what drove it, and what each mitigation action would do to it — and you can ask for a re-score after work is done. We give you the record to send them. The insurer decides what it is worth; we cannot promise a discount.

Where the work happens

Colorado State Forest Service defensible space zones, measured from the wall of each structure

Zone 1 · 0–5 ft Strip it

Brush removal and landscaping. Nothing that burns against the wall — no shrubs, no mulch, no wood stored.

Zone 2 · 5–30 ft Thin it

Felling, removal and trimming. Crowns 10 ft apart, branches up 6–10 ft, ladder fuels out.

Zone 3 · 30–100 ft Open it

Thinning by the acre. Crowns 6–10 ft apart with real gaps, and the woody debris taken off rather than scattered.
