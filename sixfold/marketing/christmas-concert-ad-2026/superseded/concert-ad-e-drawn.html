Pull a plug. Grow a lawn.

Why it helps: On compacted ground, water and fertilizer run off before they reach the roots. The holes let them soak in where the grass needs them, and the plugs break down back into the lawn.

How we do it

1Pull plugsOur machine pulls small plugs of soil, about 2 to 3 inches deep, all over the lawn.
2Seed the holesWe overseed straight after, while the holes are open, with a root and soil conditioner.
3Feed itFertilizer goes down to help the new grass come in.
