## Lines cut, and why

| Line | Why it was cut |
|---|---|
| "Before the ground freezes" | No source for freeze dates across the service area. It also reads as pressure. |
| "We already have your lawn measured" or "we can see your lawn from above" | You don't want customers to know about the lawn data (decided 14 Oct). |
| A price or a price range | There's no flat rate. Each lawn is quoted. |
| "Same-day quote" | It's a promise about turnaround that nobody has set. Add it back if you want to commit to it. |
| A line on water restrictions or overseeding results | Overseeding depends on watering, and 2026 restrictions differ by town. Leave it to the quote. |
