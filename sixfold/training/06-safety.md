# Safety

What everyone on a Sixfold job does to stay safe. One `## ` heading = one lesson in the app (heading: Safety). Short
versions of sixfold/research/crew-safety-and-compliance.md, which has the full rules and their sources. Not legal advice.

## Every job
Source: sixfold/research/crew-safety-and-compliance.md B1, B4 and B7 (29 CFR 1910.132, .151, .95, .1030, .106).

### Protective gear
- Eye protection with saws, chippers, trimmers and edgers.
- Hearing protection whenever a saw, the chipper or a mower is running.
- Gloves, and head, foot and leg protection (chaps) on saw work.
Sixfold provides the protective gear. Ask if you don't have it.

### First aid
- Every crew must have at least one person with current first-aid training. If yours doesn't, say so before the job starts.
- A stocked first-aid kit must be on the truck, with trauma dressings and a tourniquet for saw work.
- Gloves in the kit, and the first-aider uses them.

### Machines and fuel
- Guards stay on. The mower's discharge chute guard is never removed.
- Refuel with the engine off and cooled, no smoking, on bare ground, not in the slash.
- Fuel in approved safety cans, closed, out of the sun, not in the cab.
- A charged fire extinguisher on the truck, next to the fuel.

## Saws, chippers, lines and heights
Source: sixfold/research/crew-safety-and-compliance.md B2-B6.

### Power lines
Never: Touch any tree touching, leaning toward or within 10 feet of a power line until the utility has made it safe. No exceptions, no "just this branch".

### Heights
No climbing, no ladder work in trees, no lifts. Everything is felled from the ground. Anything over 80 feet: call the office first.

### Digging and grinding
Law: Before any stump grinding or digging, Colorado 811 must be notified at least two business days ahead, not counting the day you call.

### If you're unsure
Tip: Stop and call. A job that waits a day costs nothing next to an injury.

## If someone gets hurt
Source: sixfold/research/crew-safety-and-compliance.md Part D.

### The first minutes
- Stop the saw.
- The first-aider takes over.
- A deep cut: direct pressure, a tourniquet if it won't stop, call 911.
- Don't move someone who has fallen.
- Don't drive them yourself if 911 can get there faster.

### Then
- Call the office straight away. Some injuries have to be reported to OSHA within hours, and the office makes that call.
- Write down what happened, when, who was there and what we'll change. It's the record if anything is questioned later.
