# Using the app

How the crew uses the door app day to day. One `## ` heading = one lesson in the app (heading: Using the app).
Short versions of traverse/data-pipeline/door-territory/APP-GUIDE, which describes every screen.

## A day at the doors
Source: APP-GUIDE (A day at the doors; Door rounds, screen by screen).

### Find where to knock
Door rounds opens on Colorado: tap a county, then a town, or tap Nearest town. Pick a neighbourhood. You can sort them by lawn, fire, trees, doors left or nearest.

### Knock in street order
The next door is open at the top; the rest are one line each. Each door shows what we know before you knock: lawn size, trees near the house, fire risk.

### Mark every door
- Quote booked: they want the walk.
- Not now: pick when to come back (a month, three months, spring, fall, a date) or flag a no-soliciting sign.
- No answer: it comes back on the list in two days.
A message offers Undo if you tap the wrong one.

## After a yes
Source: APP-GUIDE (Recording what happens).

### Book the walk first
After Quote booked, the panel opens with Book the walk: a day, a time or "time to agree", and the walks already booked that day so they don't clash. Then Add to calendar for reminders, and Text a confirmation from your phone.

### Add info
One panel holds the rest. Saved items get a tick.
- Phone and email.
- Photos: only with the owner's OK, and of the property, not people.
- A note.
- Extra work you spotted: gutters, branches over the roof, dead trees, bare lawn, beds.
- The fire check.

### What happens next
The booking appears in the office's Pipeline. When the job is won, the office makes them a customer.

## Phone numbers and Do Not Call
Source: APP-GUIDE (Recording what happens, Look up number).

### Two kinds of number
- Given at the door: the person told you. Save it as given.
- Looked up: found by searching. Save it as looked up.
The app records which, because a looked-up number has to clear the Do Not Call lists before anyone calls it.

### Look up number
On a door with no number, Look up number shows the owner of record and the address with Copy buttons, and opens a free people-search page in a new tab. Read it, come back and type the number in yourself. The app never reads those sites for you.

### Texting
Only text a number the person gave you.

## No signal
Source: APP-GUIDE (How it saves, online and off).

No signal is fine. Anything you save without signal waits on the phone, marked "not sent yet", and sends by itself when signal comes back, dated when you tapped it. The app opens with no signal too, along with any neighbourhood you've already opened on that phone.

Before a day somewhere with poor signal, open the neighbourhoods you'll work while you still have it.
