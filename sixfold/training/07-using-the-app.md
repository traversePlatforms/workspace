## A day at the doors
Source: APP-GUIDE (A day at the doors; Door rounds, screen by screen); the app as of 2026-10-13 (Routes tile, Most 8+ sort, score and reasons, grey dots, the four buttons under the score).

### Find where to knock
Tap Routes. It opens on Colorado: tap a county, then a town, or tap Nearest town. Pick a neighbourhood. Sort them to suit the day: Most 8+ puts the neighbourhoods with the most strong doors first, and there's Most left and Nearest too.
Tip: Neighbourhoods assigned to you show on the home screen, best first, with the day if one is set.

### Read the door
- The score, 1 to 10, is how good a door is for lawn work, with the reasons under it: the lawn, trees close to the house.
- The four buttons sit right under the score.
- Below them, the information blocks fold to one line each: tap More to open one. Funding, the lawn plan and water share one line at the bottom.
- A grey dot on the map is not a door to knock: a rental, business or other home that is not on the list.

### Mark every door
- Walk booked: they want the walk.
- Quote sent: they already have a quote from us.
- Not now: pick when to come back (a month, three months, spring, fall, a date), or "No-soliciting sign — skip this door for good".
- No answer: it comes back on the list in two days.
A message offers Undo if you tap the wrong one.

## After a yes
Source: APP-GUIDE (Recording what happens); extra phone numbers (2026-10-12).

### Book the walk first
After Walk booked, the panel opens with Book the walk: a day, a time or "time to agree", and the walks already booked that day so they don't clash. Then Add to calendar for reminders, and Text a confirmation from your phone.

### Add info
One panel holds the rest. Saved items get a tick.
- Phone and email. A door can hold up to four more numbers after the first.
- Photos: only with the owner's OK, and of the property, not people.
- A note.
- Extra work you spotted: gutters, branches over the roof, dead trees, bare lawn, beds.
- The fire check.

### What happens next
The booking appears in the office's Pipeline. When the job is won, the office makes them a customer.

## Phone numbers and Do Not Call
Source: APP-GUIDE (Recording what happens, Look up number).

### Two kinds of number
- Given at the door: the person told you. Save it as given.
- Looked up: found by searching. Save it as looked up.
The app records which, because a looked-up number has to clear the Do Not Call lists before anyone calls it.

### Look up number
On a door with no number, Look up number shows the owner of record and the address with Copy buttons, and opens a free people-search page in a new tab. Read it, come back and type the number in yourself. The app never reads those sites for you.

### Texting
Never: Text a number the person didn't give you.

## Your sign-in
Source: one device at a time (rounds.php startSession, 2026-10-12); the Profile note on the tap log (2026-10-12); location handling (index.html: S.here is used on the phone only); Terms of Use draft sections 1-2. Added 2026-10-13.

### Your code is yours
Never: Share your code, or sign in for someone else.
Your code works on one phone and one computer at a time. Signing in on another phone signs this one out, and this phone tells you why. If that wasn't you, tell your manager straight away.

### What the app records
The app records which screens and buttons you use, so the company can see what helps, never what you type. It records the doors you mark and when.
Tip: Your phone's location is only used on your phone, to show where you are on the map and to order your stops. The app doesn't send it anywhere.

## No signal
Source: APP-GUIDE (How it saves, online and off).

No signal is fine. Anything you save without signal waits on the phone, marked "not sent yet", and sends by itself when signal comes back, dated when you tapped it. The app opens with no signal too, along with any neighbourhood you've already opened on that phone.

Do: Before a day somewhere with poor signal, open the neighbourhoods you'll work while you still have it.
