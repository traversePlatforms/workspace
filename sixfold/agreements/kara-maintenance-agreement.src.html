2 · The work, month by month

A twelve-month agreement. What happens in any given month depends on the season and the weather; the price at section 5 is the same every month regardless.

Mowing the open turf, trimming and edging in against beds, walls, drive and walks, and blowing the hard surfaces clear before leaving. Clippings are mulched back into the turf unless the owner asks at the walk for them to be removed. Mowing visits per month:   Months this runs: to .

Deep root feeding of the trees and shrubs identified and agreed at the walk, twice in the twelve months, once in spring and once in fall. Fertilizer only. No pesticide, herbicide, weed killer or weed-and-feed product of any kind is applied under this agreement — see section 3.

Clearing snow from the driveway, the front walk and the path to the front door, and from any other surface written here: . A visit is triggered by a snowfall leaving inches or more, and the surfaces are cleared within hours of the snow stopping. Months this runs: to . 3 · What is not included

Unless written below, this does not cover weed control, herbicide, pesticide or weed-and-feed of any kind; ice melt, salt, sand or any other ice treatment; aeration, overseeding, dethatching or topdressing; tree felling, limbing or stump work; bed planting, mulching or replanting; irrigation repair, blow-out or start-up; gutter, roof or structural work of any kind; hauling non-green debris; leaf collection and removal beyond the blowing described in section 2; or permit fees.

Sixfold applies no weed control and no ice treatment under this agreement. Fertilizer is not weed killer. If either is wanted, it is a separate written agreement and a separate price — it is not something this one covers, prices or implies.

Nothing added. Scope is as described in section 2.

4 · How long this runs

Twelve months from the start date at section 6. It does not renew itself. When the twelve months are up this agreement simply ends, and if both sides want it to carry on a new one is signed. Nothing rolls over, no notice has to be given to stop it, and no payment is taken after the twelfth month.

Either side may end it earlier than that under clause 16.

Terms

  1. What Sixfold gives you with the job, whether or not you ask A written scope of exactly what was done and where, and an itemised invoice.
  2. What this work does and does not do This is routine grounds maintenance — mowing, feeding and snow clearing. It is not a defensible-space or wildfire mitigation treatment. It does not guarantee any particular condition, thickness or colour of turf, and it is not a promise that any surface is safe underfoot — see clause 14.
  3. You confirm you can authorise the work By signing, you confirm you are the owner of record of the property, or that you have the owner's authority to have this work done, and that no part of the area to be worked is on land belonging to someone else.
  4. Boundaries, buried lines and things we cannot see You will identify the property boundaries. Sixfold will request public utility locates through Colorado 811 before any stump grinding or ground disturbance. Private and unmarked lines are yours to point out — irrigation, septic tanks and leach fields, wells, invisible pet fencing, low-voltage lighting, buried propane, and anything else not on a public locate. Sixfold is not responsible for damage to a line it was not told about and that a public locate did not mark.
  5. Clearing the way Before the crew arrives, please move vehicles, secure pets and livestock, and keep people out of the work area while saws are running.
  6. HOA rules and permits Where a covenant, HOA architectural rule, or local permit applies to removing trees, obtaining that approval is the owner's responsibility unless section 3 says otherwise in writing.
  7. Access and ground conditions Equipment has to reach the work. Driveways, lawns and soft ground can rut or mark under machinery even when the crew is careful, and that kind of ordinary access marking is not damage under clause 11.
  8. If we find something once we start Hidden rot, a hazard lean, a hornet nest, a stump on a boulder — if a condition appears that changes the work, the crew stops and tells you before continuing.
  9. Changes to the work Any change to the scope or the price has to be written down and signed by both of us before that work is done. A verbal agreement to do extra work does not create a charge you owe.
  10. Paying $250.00 is due on the same day of each month, on the date set at section 6, and may be paid by electronic transfer, cash, or check made payable to Sixfold, LLC. Every payment is refundable in full, within ten business days, if you cancel under section 7 — including any payment made before the cancellation period has run. If this agreement ends early under clause 16, any month paid for in advance and not begun is refunded within ten business days.

Terms 11 to 17 · your right to cancel · signatures

  1. Damage Sixfold is responsible for damage its crew causes by negligence in doing this work. Tell us within 14 days of completion so we can look at it while the evidence is fresh. Sixfold is not responsible for pre-existing conditions, for the access marking described in clause 7, or for damage arising from a line or structure that was not disclosed under clause 4.
  2. Colorado law This agreement is governed by the law of the State of Colorado, and any dispute is brought in the county where the property is located.
  3. This document is the agreement What is written on the five pages of this agreement is the whole agreement. Nothing said at the door changes it. A change is only effective if it is in writing and signed by both of us.
  4. What clearing snow does and does not mean Sixfold clears snow from the surfaces listed in section 2. Clearing snow does not make a surface safe to walk or drive on, and no ice treatment of any kind is applied under this agreement — snow can compact, melt and refreeze, and a cleared surface can still be icy. Sixfold does not undertake to keep any surface free of ice or to make it safe.
  5. Getting to the work Snow clearing needs the driveway clear of vehicles, and mowing needs the turf clear of hoses, toys, furniture and pet waste. Where it is not clear the crew works around it and says so on the work record. Anything hidden under snow or in long grass is the owner's to point out under clause 4.
  6. Ending this agreement early Either of us may end this agreement early by giving the other 30 days' written notice, for any reason or none. The owner pays for the months already begun. Any month paid for in advance and not begun is refunded within ten business days. No fee or penalty is charged by either side for ending it this way.
  7. When the weather stops a visit Frozen, saturated or snow-covered ground stops mowing, and a storm can delay a snow visit past the hours set in section 2. Where that happens Sixfold says so and attends as soon as conditions allow. A missed visit caused by weather does not change the monthly price, which is set for the year rather than per visit.

7 · Your right to cancel

You may cancel this agreement, for any reason or none, until midnight of the third business day after the date you signed it. A business day is any day except Sunday and federal holidays. You do not have to give a reason and there is no penalty or fee. The last two pages of this maintenance agreement are your two copies of the Notice of Cancellation — send one to the address printed on it, or write to us any other way that says you do not want to go ahead. Everything you have paid comes back to you.

You, the buyer, may cancel this transaction at any time prior to midnight of the third business day after the date of this transaction. See the attached notice of cancellation form for an explanation of this right.
