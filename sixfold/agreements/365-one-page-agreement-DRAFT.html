What changed, for Brandon

Items 1 to 7 came in with version 2 (Matthew's answers). Item 8 came in with version 3, item 9 with 3.1.

  1. Deposit: non-refundable for now. Brandon to confirm
  2. Reschedule or cancel: by calling or texting, with 24 hours' notice.
  3. Sprinklers, pipes, dog fences: the customer only lets us know, so they can be marked. They don't have to mark or list them.
  4. Gates and pets: gates unlocked and pets inside on the day.
  5. Results: left out. Jen can talk about results on the call if it comes up.
  6. The balance: unless they save their card (item 8), the agreement doesn't say how the balance is paid. Jen settles that on the call (cash, a payment link or a card on the day).
  7. Signing: a typed name and two boxes, one for "I have read this and agree" and one for signing electronically. The app keeps the time, the version and a fingerprint of what they saw.
  8. Card on file for the balance: new in v3 Brandon to confirm
    • An optional box above the Pay button: "Save this card with Square and charge the $224 balance to it on the day of the service." It starts unticked.
    • Their choice is recorded before they go to Square to pay, with the time, their internet address and their device. If they tick it, the same sentence goes into "What we agreed" on the form they sign.
    • Square saves the card on their customer profile. The card number is held by Square, never by 365 or the app; the app only knows the brand and the last four digits.
    • Nothing is charged automatically. Brad or the office taps "Charge the balance" once the work is done, and only the balance shown can be charged, once.
    • Questions for Brandon: should the line also say what happens if the work can't be done that day, or that the card isn't charged for anything else? Does a card on file change the 24-hour cancellation wording?
  9. Not confirmed until the deposit is paid: new in v3.1 Brandon to confirm Matthew's addition (15 Oct). In practice the day is held on the schedule when the form goes out, and the job is booked when they pay and sign; this line says so to the customer.

Still open: whether any of this needs an attorney's look before real customers sign it.
