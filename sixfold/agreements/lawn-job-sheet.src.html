3 · What is not included

Unless ticked or written above, this job does not cover weed control, herbicide, pesticide or weed-and-feed; ice melt, salt or sand; dethatching or topdressing; planting or sod; irrigation work; gutter, roof or structural work; or permit fees. Fertilizer is not weed killer. Any of it is a separate price.

6 · Terms

  1. Changes cost extra, and are agreed before they happen. If the owner wants something not ticked at section 2, Sixfold prices it and the owner signs the change in writing before that work starts.
  2. Mark what is buried, and tell us what we cannot see. Before any ground work the owner marks sprinkler heads, shallow irrigation lines, invisible pet fence, low-voltage lighting cable and septic components. Sixfold is not responsible for damage to anything buried that was not marked; anything marked that is damaged anyway is repaired at Sixfold's cost.
  3. Getting to the work. The owner leaves gates unlocked, the working area clear of vehicles, toys, furniture and pet waste, and pets inside while the crew is on site. Work the crew cannot get to is rescheduled.
  4. Seed is not guaranteed to come up. Germination depends on watering, weather and soil. Sixfold will say what watering the seed needs. No guarantee of germination, coverage or colour is given or implied; reseeding later is a new job.
  5. Weather moves dates. Frozen, saturated or extremely dry ground, snow, and fire restrictions all stop this work. A visit stopped by weather is rescheduled to the next workable day and the price does not change.
  6. The lawn as it is found. Aeration leaves plugs on the surface to break down. Ruts, thin patches, surface roots and existing damage are not created by this work and are not repaired under it.
  7. Cancelling. You, the buyer, may cancel this transaction at any time prior to midnight of the third business day after the date of this transaction. See the attached notice of cancellation form for an explanation of this right.
