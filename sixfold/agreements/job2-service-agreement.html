2 · The work

✗Felling — trees taken down
Stump grinding — ground out below grade
Slash and chipping
Rounds — cut to firewood length

Fuel break / thinning

Felling is ticked. Most of this job is yard and landscape work, which none of the categories above describes. The full scope is written below and it governs.

Two crew on site. The work comprises the following, carried out in whatever order conditions on the day allow: (1) mowing, trimming and edging approximately three-quarters of an acre of open ground, including the edges in against beds, walls and drive; (2) felling three trees, each under approximately ten feet, none over a roof, fence, utility line or drive, taken down whole and cut up on the ground; (3) limbing thirteen trees — lower limbs raised to a height of approximately four feet, shaping only, no removal; (4) pulling out nine existing plants, roots included, leaving the beds ready for new planting; (5) blowing down the drive and walks; (6) loading and hauling off the property — Sixfold truck and 8 × 8 × 4 trailer, approximately one and a half loads to a disposal site, tipping fees paid by Sixfold and included in the price at section 5; (7) walking the finished job with the owner before leaving, and putting right anything in this section not completed as described, while the crew and machines are still on site. Second visit, included in the price at section 5 and at no extra charge: once the owner’s six three-gallon Russian sage are delivered, Sixfold returns on a date agreed at that time, digs the holes and sets the plants, using the plants and the soil supplied with the owner’s delivery. Sixfold supplies no plants and no soil under this agreement. An itemised invoice and a written work record are supplied on completion. Location: the areas of the property walked and agreed at the site walk.

The three trees to be felled, the thirteen to be limbed and the nine bed plants to be pulled are all walked and agreed with the owner before any work starts, and anything the owner wants kept is pointed out at that walk. That walk, not a count written in advance, is what fixes the scope of this agreement. No tree over approximately ten feet is felled under it.

3 · What is not included

Unless written below, this does not cover structural work of any kind (roofing, vents, siding, decking); reseeding or turf replacement; repair of irrigation, fencing or lawn; hauling non-green debris; work on land not identified as the owner's; or permit fees. The template's usual exclusion of landscaping and replanting does not apply to this job — the mowing, edging, bed clearing and the planting are inside the scope at section 2 and are paid for at section 5.

Also excluded for this job: grinding any stump — the stump of every tree felled remains, and grinding cannot be done the same day because Colorado 811 lists it as excavation and the utility locate takes three business days; felling any tree over approximately ten feet, or any tree over a structure or a utility line, which Sixfold will walk and quote separately; the plants and the soil they are planted in, both of which arrive with the owner’s delivery and neither of which Sixfold supplies or warrants as to condition, quantity or survival; watering or aftercare of the plants at any stage; and any replacement planting if the delivery is short, wrong or damaged; removing anything that is not green waste — fencing, wire, metal, building material or household waste — which is set aside and stays with the owner unless a price for it is separately agreed.

Nothing added. Scope is as described in section 2.

Terms

  1. What Sixfold gives you with the job, whether or not you ask A written scope of exactly what was done and where, and an itemised invoice.
  2. What this work does and does not do This is yard and landscape work, including the removal and trimming of small trees. It is not a defensible-space or wildfire mitigation treatment, it does not make a property fireproof, and it does not guarantee any outcome in a wildfire. No one can promise that, and nothing in this agreement claims it.
  3. You confirm you can authorise the work By signing, you confirm you are the owner of record of the property, or that you have the owner's authority to have this work done, and that no part of the area to be worked is on land belonging to someone else.
  4. Boundaries, buried lines and things we cannot see You will identify the property boundaries. Sixfold will request public utility locates through Colorado 811 before any stump grinding or ground disturbance. Private and unmarked lines are yours to point out — irrigation, septic tanks and leach fields, wells, invisible pet fencing, low-voltage lighting, buried propane, and anything else not on a public locate. Sixfold is not responsible for damage to a line it was not told about and that a public locate did not mark.
  5. Clearing the way Before the crew arrives, please move vehicles, secure pets and livestock, and keep people out of the work area while saws are running.
  6. HOA rules and permits Where a covenant, HOA architectural rule, or local permit applies to removing trees, obtaining that approval is the owner's responsibility unless section 3 says otherwise in writing.
  7. Access and ground conditions Equipment has to reach the work. Driveways, lawns and soft ground can rut or mark under machinery even when the crew is careful, and that kind of ordinary access marking is not damage under clause 11.

7 · Your right to cancel

You may cancel this agreement, for any reason or none, until midnight of the third business day after the date you signed it. A business day is any day except Sunday and federal holidays. You do not have to give a reason and there is no penalty or fee. The last two pages of this service agreement are your two copies of the Notice of Cancellation — send one to the address printed on it, or write to us any other way that says you do not want to go ahead. Everything you have paid comes back to you.

You, the buyer, may cancel this transaction at any time prior to midnight of the third business day after the date of this transaction. See the attached notice of cancellation form for an explanation of this right.
