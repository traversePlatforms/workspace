2 · The work

Two crew on site. The work comprises the following, carried out in whatever order conditions on the day allow: (1) core aeration of the open turf across the property — a machine carrying hollow tines that pushes down and pulls plugs of soil and thatch out of the ground, worked in a pattern that covers the whole area; (2) overseeding the aerated ground with 200 lb of grass seed, supplied by Sixfold; (3) applying 150 lb of fertilizer, supplied by Sixfold, across the same ground; (4) blowing the drive, walks and any hard surface clear of seed and fertilizer that lands on them; (5) walking the finished job with the owner before leaving, and putting right anything in this section not completed as described, while the crew and machines are still on site. An itemised invoice and a written work record are supplied on completion. The plugs the aerator pulls stay on the surface — see section 4. Location: the open turf areas of the property walked and agreed at the site walk.

The seed and the fertilizer are both supplied by Sixfold and included in the price at section 5; the mix and the analysis used are written on the work record at completion. No pesticide, herbicide, weed killer or weed-and-feed product of any kind is applied under this agreement — see section 3. The turf to be treated is walked and agreed with the owner before any work starts, and anything to be left alone — beds, new planting, ground being kept bare — is pointed out then, along with irrigation heads and invisible pet fencing under clause 4. That walk, not an area written in advance, fixes the scope.

3 · What is not included

Unless written below, this does not cover watering of any kind, before or after seeding; any guarantee that seed germinates or that any particular thickness, colour or coverage results; weed control, herbicide, pesticide or weed-and-feed of any kind; dethatching, topdressing, soil testing, levelling or grading; repair or replacement of irrigation heads, lines or valves, or of invisible pet fencing; mowing; tree, shrub or bed work; hauling anything off the property; or permit fees.

Sixfold does not water. Overseeded ground needs water to germinate and keeping it watered is the owner's to do. Sixfold applies no weed control — fertilizer is not weed killer. If the ground is frozen, saturated or baked hard on the day, the crew says so and agrees a return date rather than run a machine that pulls no plug.

Nothing added. Scope is as described in section 2.

4 · What happens to the plugs

Core aeration drops the plugs it pulls onto the surface and they stay there. They break down back into the lawn over the following weeks and that is what they are for — they are not debris the crew has failed to clear. Nothing is hauled off the property under this agreement. If the owner would rather the plugs were collected and taken away, that is a separate price agreed in writing beforehand, under clause 9.

Terms

  1. What Sixfold gives you with the job, whether or not you ask A written scope of exactly what was done and where, and an itemised invoice.
  2. What this work does and does not do This is turf work — aeration, overseeding and feeding. It is not a defensible-space or wildfire mitigation treatment and it makes no difference to how a property behaves in a wildfire. It also does not guarantee that seed germinates, that the lawn thickens, or that any particular colour or coverage results. Those depend on water, weather and soil, and Sixfold controls none of them.
  3. You confirm you can authorise the work By signing, you confirm you are the owner of record of the property, or that you have the owner's authority to have this work done, and that no part of the area to be worked is on land belonging to someone else.
  4. Boundaries, buried lines and things we cannot see You will identify the property boundaries. Sixfold will request public utility locates through Colorado 811 before any stump grinding or ground disturbance. Private and unmarked lines are yours to point out — irrigation, septic tanks and leach fields, wells, invisible pet fencing, low-voltage lighting, buried propane, and anything else not on a public locate. Sixfold is not responsible for damage to a line it was not told about and that a public locate did not mark.
  5. Clearing the way Before the crew arrives, please move vehicles, secure pets and livestock, and keep people out of the work area while saws are running.
  6. HOA rules and permits Where a covenant, HOA architectural rule, or local permit applies to removing trees, obtaining that approval is the owner's responsibility unless section 3 says otherwise in writing.
  7. Access and ground conditions Equipment has to reach the work. Driveways, lawns and soft ground can rut or mark under machinery even when the crew is careful, and that kind of ordinary access marking is not damage under clause 11.

7 · Your right to cancel

You may cancel this agreement, for any reason or none, until midnight of the third business day after the date you signed it. A business day is any day except Sunday and federal holidays. You do not have to give a reason and there is no penalty or fee. The last two pages of this service agreement are your two copies of the Notice of Cancellation — send one to the address printed on it, or write to us any other way that says you do not want to go ahead. Everything you have paid comes back to you.

You, the buyer, may cancel this transaction at any time prior to midnight of the third business day after the date of this transaction. See the attached notice of cancellation form for an explanation of this right.
