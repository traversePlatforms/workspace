## ⚑ Sixfold fills these before it reaches him

These must already be on the page **when Brian opens it**, not added after it comes back. A cancellation
notice that is blank when he receives it is the defect that leaves the sale cancellable for three years.
Put them in as sender-filled text before you send (DocuSign's prefill fields), or tell me the signing
day and I will print them into the PDF.

| Value | Where | Places |
|---|---|---|
| **Date of the transaction** (the day he signs) | Page 2 standfirst; pages 5 and 6 above "(Date of transaction)" | **3** |
| **Cancellation deadline** | Page 3 §6 "Cancellation period ends"; pages 5 and 6 after "NOT LATER THAN MIDNIGHT OF" | **3** |
| Agreement no. | Page 2 masthead | 1 |
| Quote date (optional) | Page 1 masthead "Date" | 1 |

**The three transaction dates must match, and so must the three deadlines.**

### Which deadline to write

| He signs | Deadline to write | Work may begin |
|---|---|---|
| Mon 5 Oct | midnight **Thu 8 Oct** | Fri 9 Oct |
| Tue 6 Oct | midnight **Fri 9 Oct** | Sat 10 Oct |
| Wed 7 Oct | midnight **Sat 10 Oct** | Sun 11 Oct |
| Thu 8 Oct | midnight **Tue 13 Oct** | Wed 14 Oct |
| Fri 9 Oct | midnight **Wed 14 Oct** | Thu 15 Oct ✓ last safe day |
| Sat 10 Oct or later | **too late for the 15th** | — |

Sundays and federal holidays don't count; Saturday does. **Monday 12 October is Columbus Day**, which is
why signing on Thursday 8th gives a Tuesday deadline. **If he signs later than the day you wrote, the
window is shorter than the law requires: correct or reissue before the job.** A deadline that falls
later than needed is fine; one that falls too early is not.
