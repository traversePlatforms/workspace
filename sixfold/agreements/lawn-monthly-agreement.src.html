3 · What is not included

Unless it is ticked above or written on the line above, this does not cover weed control, herbicide, pesticide or weed-and-feed of any kind; ice melt, salt, sand or any other ice treatment; dethatching or topdressing; planting or sod; tree felling, limbing or stump work; irrigation repair, blow-out or start-up; gutter, roof or structural work; hauling non-green debris; or permit fees. Fertilizer is not weed killer. If any of it is wanted it is a separate price, agreed in writing.

4 · How long this runs, and how it ends

THIS AGREEMENT CONTINUES FROM MONTH TO MONTH UNTIL YOU CANCEL IT. It has no end date. You will be charged the monthly price at section 5 every month until you tell us to stop.

TO CANCEL, at any time: call (720) 830-4553, email contact@365aeration.com, or write to us at the address above. No fee, and no reason needed. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the month you have already paid for, and nothing is charged after that.

ONCE A YEAR we will send you a written reminder, between 25 and 40 days before each twelve-month anniversary of the start date, telling you this agreement is still running and how to cancel it.

Sixfold may also end it with 30 days' written notice. Either side may end it sooner if the other breaks it and does not put that right within 14 days of being asked in writing.

6 · Terms

  1. Changes cost extra, and are agreed before they happen. If the owner wants something not ticked at section 2, Sixfold prices it and the owner signs the change in writing before that work starts.
  2. Mark what is buried, and tell us what we cannot see. Before aeration, mowing or any ground work the owner marks sprinkler heads, shallow irrigation lines, invisible pet fence, low-voltage lighting cable and septic components. Sixfold is not responsible for damage to anything buried that was not marked, and anything marked that is damaged anyway is repaired by Sixfold at its own cost.
  3. Getting to the work. The owner leaves gates unlocked and the working area clear on visit days, and keeps pets inside while the crew is on site. A visit the crew cannot get to is not made up and is not refunded.
  4. Weather moves visits. Frozen, saturated or extremely dry ground, snow, and fire restrictions all stop this work. A visit stopped by weather is moved to the next workable day where the season allows it. The monthly price does not change because weather cost a visit — it is a price for the year's work spread evenly, not a price per visit.
  5. Seed is not guaranteed to come up. Germination depends on watering, weather and soil, and most of that is outside Sixfold's control. No guarantee of germination, coverage or colour is given or implied.
  6. Changing the monthly price. Sixfold may change it with at least 30 days' written notice before the month it applies to. If the owner does not want the new price they cancel under section 4 and nothing at the new price is charged.
  7. Payment. Due on the day of the month set at section 5.
  8. Cancelling in the first three days. You, the buyer, may cancel this transaction at any time prior to midnight of the third business day after the date of this transaction. See the attached notice of cancellation form for an explanation of this right.
