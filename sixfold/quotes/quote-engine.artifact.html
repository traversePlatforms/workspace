    Where the numbers come from

    RECORDED · PRICED · ALLOCATED
    Priced arrived 2026-09-23: Brandon and Brad gave a price for one named line — $150 to mow ¾ acre, $50 to edge it, $16.66 a plant, $600 for 13 trees limbed, and a complete fell under 30 ft — and the table carries that price divided by their own $100/man-hour. The figure is theirs and it is per-line, which is what separates it from Allocated below; the division is arithmetic, not judgement. Nobody has yet timed any of these with a stopwatch.

    Recorded is Brandon's own number for that line: every recorded cost on a job — dump fees, hire, purchases, fuel — plus the handling percentage, the rounding, and the 16-hour total. Allocated is that total shared out across the time lines by Matthew so the table reproduces Sixfold's own recorded 16 man-hours on job 2. The total is a measurement; the share-out is one job, one crew, one property, split by judgement — a starting point, not a measurement. A tick means Brandon confirmed the rate outright, and it becomes Recorded.

    COMPETITOR
    Taken from outside the company, never from Sixfold. Every figure below was re-read at source on 2026-09-14.

    Times per tree — Wexford Insurance / TreeGuard, How to Price Tree Service Jobs, Nate Jones, 6 May 2026: 2.0–3.5 man-hours under 30 ft, 4.0–7.0 at 30–50 ft, 7.0–12.0 at 50–70 ft, 12.0–24.0 above that. Man-hours, not crew-hours — the rate card records these as crew-hours, which would double them.

    Prices in Sixfold's own market — Hawk Tree Service, Colorado Springs, removal page 12 Oct 2025 and trimming page 9 Mar 2026: removal $150–$500 under 30 ft, $500–$1,000 at 30–60, $1,000–$2,000+ above 60; trimming $75–$415 on a tree under 30 ft; labour $65–$140/hr; a two-person crew $115–$143/hr; stump grinding $2–$5 per diameter inch; per acre $733–$2,333 lightly wooded, $3,395–$6,155 heavily forested.

    Cost models — SynkedUP, Weston Zimmerman, updated 11 Aug 2026: 2026 band $65–$145/man-hour, lean startup $65–$75, worked example $71.10 (labour $36 + overhead $24.44, +15% margin). ArboStar, 26 May 2026: groundworker $28/hr loaded, ~$20/man-hour overhead. These are cost models, not a competitor's price list — they say what it costs to run a crew, not what another firm charges, so they are not evidence for a “market value” column.

    Where the billable rate now sits. Brandon and Brad set it at $100 a man-hour on 2026-09-23, replacing the $70 taken from SynkedUP. That is $200 for a two-person crew, against Hawk's published $115–$143 — Sixfold is now some 40% above the top of the only published rate in its own market, deliberately. The stump-grinding comparison that used to sit here is retired with the service.

    UNBACKED
    Still nothing behind them after the 2026-09-23 round: chipping (no Colorado operator publishes a rate — checked twice, they all say “call”; the $320/24h chipper hire prices the machine, not the work), rigging, bucking, slash piling, dead fall and brush. Stump grinding left this list by being retired, not by being answered. Confirm each with Brandon before it carries a quote.

    UNRESOLVED AS OF 2026-09-23
    Limbing contradicts itself. Brandon and Brad now say $600 for 13 trees; Brandon previously said $975 for the same 13, and Hawk's published $75 floor × 13 corroborated that exactly. The table carries the newer figure and this note. Nothing above 30 ft is a Sixfold figure — under 30 ft is now 65 man-minutes (Matthew, 2026-09-30, a whole tree with two people), 30–60 ft is still Wexford's 330. An 8×14 trailer of rounds is deliberately unpriced: “a lot more than that” is not a figure. Nobody has timed an aeration.
