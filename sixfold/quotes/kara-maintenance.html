Terms

  • The estimate is free and the price is fixed. $250 a month for twelve months, $3,000 over the term. Travel is not billed, and nothing further is owed unless additional work is agreed in writing.
  • Payment. Due on the 1st of each month, first on 1 November 2026 and last on 1 October 2027, by electronic transfer, cash, or check made payable to Sixfold, LLC.
  • It does not renew itself. Twelve months and then it stops — nothing rolls over and no payment is taken after the twelfth month. Sixfold will contact you about 45 days before the end of the term to go over how the year has gone and agree what the next one should cover.
  • Either of us can end it early on 30 days’ written notice, for any reason and with no fee. Any month paid for in advance and not begun is refunded within ten business days.
  • Clearing snow does not make a surface safe. Snow can compact, melt and refreeze, and a cleared surface can still be icy. Sixfold does not undertake to keep any surface free of ice.
  • Right to cancel. An agreement signed at the property carries a three-business-day right of cancellation, in writing, for any reason and without fee. This is Colorado law and cannot be waived; all payments are refunded in full. Work does not begin until that period has run, unless an earlier start is requested in writing.
  • This price does not expire. No time-limited discount is attached to it.
  • Please water the lawn a day or two before an aeration visit. Aeration pulls a proper plug from moist ground and very little from ground that is dry and hard.
