Terms

  • The estimate is free and the price is fixed. $400 is the full price for the work listed above, including the seed, the fertilizer and the machine. It does not change unless additional work is requested in writing.
  • Payment. The first payment of $200 is due on or before the first day of work, and the remaining $200 on completion. The first payment books the date — once it is paid, the agreed date is held for this job and no other work is booked into it.
  • Watering after the day is yours. Sixfold can water the ground in on the day of the work, using the property’s own watering system, and does not return to water. Overseeded ground needs regular water over the following weeks, and how the seed comes in depends on water, weather and soil. Check what your water provider currently allows.
  • Right to cancel. An agreement signed at the property carries a three-business-day right of cancellation, in writing, for any reason and without fee. This is Colorado law and cannot be waived; all payments are refunded in full, including any made before the cancellation period has run. Work does not begin until that period has run, unless an earlier start is requested in writing.
  • This price does not expire. No time-limited discount is attached to it.
  • Overruns are Sixfold’s cost. The price covers the work listed, not the time taken to complete it.
  • Please water the lawn a day or two before we come out. Aeration pulls a proper plug from moist ground and very little from ground that is dry and hard.
