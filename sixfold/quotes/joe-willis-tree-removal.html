Terms

  • The estimate is free and the price is fixed. $2,400 is the full price for the work listed above, including the chipper, travel and fuel. It does not change unless additional work is requested in writing.
  • Payment. The first payment of $1,200 is due on or before the day of the work, and the remaining $1,200 on completion. The first payment books the date — once it is paid, it is held for this job and no other work is booked into it.
  • Why chip, and why in winter. The Colorado State Forest Service lists chipping as a way to treat beetle-infested wood (beetle larvae do not survive it), and advises dealing with infested trees before the adult beetles fly the following summer. Fresh pine chips can draw other bark beetles to standing trees, so the work is done in November or December, after the main beetle flights and when they are least active, and the chips are spread thin and kept off the base of your standing pines.
  • This price holds for work done by 31 December 2026. If the work moves later, the price is agreed again in writing.
  • Not included. Grinding or removing the stumps, taking wood or chips off the property, any tree not listed above, and spraying or treating standing trees.
  • Right to cancel. An agreement signed at the property carries a three-business-day right of cancellation, in writing, for any reason and without fee. This is Colorado law and cannot be waived; all payments are refunded in full, including any made before the cancellation period has run. Work does not begin until that period has run, unless an earlier start is requested in writing.
  • Overruns in time are Sixfold’s cost. The price covers the work listed, not the time taken to complete it.
