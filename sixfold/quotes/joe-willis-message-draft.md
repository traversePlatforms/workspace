## Fact-check log

- 1¾ hours each way: OSRM, Monument (17890 Sierra Way) to 156 Old Corral Rd, 103 min (13 Oct 2026).
- "A good part of that is the drive": drive time about 7.9 person-hours ≈ $790 + gas $69 ≈ $860 of $2,400 (notes, rev 3). About
  a third; the message doesn't state a share.
- "After the main beetle season", not "not flying": MPB adults emerge mid-June through September (CSFS 5.528); RTB peak flight is
  spring, "throughout the summer and into early fall" (USDA FS FIDL 55, 2010); **Ips adults "have been observed entering trees during
  warm days as early as late-February on through November"** (CSU/CSFS fact sheet 5.558, 2013). So November is not beetle-free on a
  warm day; December is not mentioned. Corrected 13 Oct after Matthew asked about October.
- Fresh pine chips drawing bark beetles: Fettig et al. 2007, USDA FS PSW-GTR-203 (ponderosa pine, chips left on site); the authors
  suggest keeping chips from contacting residual trees.
- "The colder it is, the safer" is a reading of that guidance; no source names a best month. Kept as a plain statement, not
  attributed to the Forest Service.
- Treat before mid-June: CSFS 5.528.
