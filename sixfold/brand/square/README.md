## Where each dimension came from — read this before trusting a number

**Square publishes real specs for the logo, invoices, eGift cards, and the Online favicon/banner. It does not publish one for the customer display or the booking-site cover** — those two are built centre-weighted and symmetric so an unknown crop takes the same amount off both sides.

**Verified — Square Support, *Customize receipts* / *Customize Square Invoices* / *Upload a custom favicon*, read 2026-08-31:**

- **Logo minimum: 200 × 200 px.** Must be **square** to sit inside the invoice/receipt frame without cropping.
- **Full logo, high resolution: at least 1,280 × 648 px** — Square's own number, and the "2:1 ratio" it names for the invoice **full** layout.
- **Small logo: 400 × 400 px** — the invoice **framed** layout.
- **Printed logo: at least 400 × 200 px.** Prints monochrome; a known support issue is a colour or low-contrast logo coming out as a black block on the printer.
- **Square Register customer display: 2,560 × 1,600 px** recommended.
- **File type: JPG or PNG** (one article also lists GIF).
- **eGift card design: 640 × 400 px** recommended; the upload converts whatever you give it to PNG.
- **Square Online favicon: square, no larger than 64 × 64 px.**
- **Square Online banner / hero / background: at least 2,000 × 1,000 px** so it holds up full-width on any screen. `2400 × 1200` clears that with margin and is a clean 2:1.
- **Square Online logo: up to 2,560 × 2,560 px.**

**Not verified — customer-display and booking-site cover crops.** Square documents the customer display's *resolution* but not how it frames a non-matching image, and the Appointments booking-site article covers the logo only, not a cover image. Both assets here keep everything that matters inside the centre ~70% of the width.

**Not verified first-party — physical gift-card trim.** Square's card print is fulfilled by a partner; the working numbers are in §Physical gift cards below. Do not send to print off this file — drop the artwork into the template Square gives you in the gift-card order flow, which is the authority on bleed and safe area.

### How the assets defend against an unknown crop

- **Every banner-style asset is centre-weighted and symmetric** (customer display, online banner, eGift). The lockup, tagline and phone all sit in the middle; a crop of unknown width degrades the composition evenly instead of losing the mark or the number.
- **Square logos are square and the mark is set at 60% width** — inside the inscribed circle with margin, so the Loyalty/Appointments disc crop doesn't clip it.
- **The favicon and app icon drop the roots.** The ten root strands run 0.75–1.6 units on a 100-unit canvas; below ~96 px they render as grey mush that dirties the silhouette. Same call as the social set — this is a crop at the ground line, not a redraw.

## The brand colour

Square's "brand colour" is a single hex value and it shows behind headers and accents on **digital** receipts and invoices. Set it to **Ember `#E4703A`**.

One caution: Square sometimes places white or light text on the brand colour. `#E4703A` carries white text at about 2.4:1, which is under the WCAG AA threshold. If Square renders the business name white-on-Ember and it looks weak, fall back to **Pine Black `#0E1512`** as the brand colour — the ember then lives only in the logo, which is how the rest of the Sixfold print family already works (ember is the accent, Pine is the ground).

## Physical gift cards

`sixfold-square-giftcard-proof-1037x664.png` is a **proof**, not a print file.

- Standard card is **CR80 — 85.6 × 54 mm** trimmed (the credit-card size).
- Square's partner has asked for artwork at **87.8 × 56.2 mm including 1.1 mm bleed on all four edges** — that is the 1037 × 664 px canvas here at 300 dpi.
- Keep text and the mark **at least ~4 mm inside the trim.** In this proof everything is well inside that; the "GIFT CARD" label sits bottom-left with room.

**Order flow:** start a gift-card order in Square, download the template it gives you, place this artwork into it, and check bleed and safe area against *that* file. The back of the card is Square's standard layout (magstripe, terms) and is not customisable.

## What is deliberately not on any of these

Same standing rules as every other Sixfold surface — they apply to a Square profile, a receipt footer and an invoice header just as they do to print:

- **No "licensed and insured", "certified", "bonded", "Firewise".** None of it is true yet. This is the single most likely thing to reach for on an invoice footer — don't.
- **No county cost-share by name** (Douglas County included) until Sixfold is on the approved contractor list.
- **No website URL.** `SixfoldColorado.com` is owned but resolves to nothing. A dead URL on a receipt is worse than no URL. `Contact@SixfoldColorado.com` is live and can go in Square's business-info email field.
- **No street address on the artwork.** Sixfold's Monument address is not on file yet, and Square carries the address in its own business-info fields regardless. Set a **service area**, not a location.
- **No price, no offer, no discount, no track record.** The launch offer is still unpicked and the company is new.

The only fixed copy on the banner-style assets is the wordmark, the descriptor **"Land & Fire"** (still a placeholder), the bio line **"We do the work and show you who pays for it."** and **"Fire mitigation · Front Range · (303) 915-1045"** — all three already written and fact-checked in [../../marketing/facebook-page-copy.md](../../marketing/facebook-page-copy.md), reused rather than reinvented.
