Land & Fire

Two changes to the Ridge mark: the smallest peak is now a tree, and the ground line puts roots down at its far right corner. The four ember peaks are untouched — same coordinates as the mark that was approved.

What changed

The first peak is a tree

Three stacked tiers and a trunk, in moss, drawn in front of the ridge rather than behind it. It keeps the shortest silhouette in the row, so the growth still reads left to right.

Roots off the right corner

Ten thin strands, kinked and out of step with each other — roots change direction as they hit resistance, so a strand that bends on a regular beat stops reading as a root. Four come off the line, the finer six branch away from strands already in the ground.

Every earlier attempt failed this row, and each failed differently: shallow roots turned into a swoosh, hard kinks turned into lightning, and four thin strands on their own went weak and sparse. Thin only works at this density. Below about 32px the root mass softens into texture — the ridge is carrying the mark at that size anyway.

Build notes

  • The canvas is now viewBox="0 0 100 116". Everything above the ground line is byte-identical to the approved mark — the extra 16 units are root depth only, so the ridge silhouette did not change.
  • Masters updated: sixfold-mark-ridge.svg and sixfold-mark-ridge-mono.svg.
  • The animation can no longer key off :nth-of-type — the tree is a group of four shapes. Delays now sit on explicit classes g1–g5 and r1–r10. The root strokes carry pathLength="100" so one dash-offset keyframe draws all ten regardless of their real lengths.
  • Eleven copies of the old mark are still inlined across the marketing sheets, the content set and the flyers. They have not been touched — say the word and they get swept together, with the affected artifacts republished.
