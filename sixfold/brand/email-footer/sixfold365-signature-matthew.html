Putting it on (do this one, not a Gmail signature)

Your emails already get a signature added automatically: the orange one from 13 Oct, set as the "Matthew footer" in the admin console. Adding this as a Gmail signature too would put two signatures on every email. Replace the footer instead:

  1. Signed in as Contact@SixfoldColorado.com, open admin.google.com.
  2. Go to Apps → Google Workspace → Gmail → Compliance.
  3. On the left, pick your own organizational unit (the one with only you in it).
  4. Under Append footer, open Matthew footer, click into the footer box, select everything in it and delete it.
  5. Click Copy signature above, paste it into the box (Ctrl+V), then Save.
  6. Send a test to a personal address. Changes can take up to an hour to apply.

Sending as M.Sharpe@

M.Sharpe@SixfoldColorado.com is a second name for the Contact@ inbox, not its own mailbox. To make your emails go out from M.Sharpe@ (so replies come back to that name), in Gmail open Settings → See all settings → Accounts → Send mail as → Add another email address, add M.Sharpe@SixfoldColorado.com, and pick it in the From line when you write. Replies land in the Contact@ inbox either way.
