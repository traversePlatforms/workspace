Putting it on Cai's Gmail

  1. Signed in as Cai, open Gmail, then Settings (gear) → See all settings → General.
  2. Under Signature, click Create new and name it Sixfold.
  3. Click into the box and paste (Ctrl+V).
  4. Under Signature defaults, pick it for new emails and for replies, for the C.Silva address.
  5. Scroll down and click Save changes.

Before you turn it on

The team footer already goes on every email that leaves Sixfold. With this signature on too, Cai's emails would show two Sixfold blocks: this one, then the footer under it. Two ways round it:

Keep the footer for everyone and give Cai a short signature: just their name, and their number if it differs from the office line. The footer adds the logo and the rest.

Or take Cai off the team footer: in the admin console, put Cai in their own organizational unit with no footer rule (or a footer that is this signature), and use the full signature above.
