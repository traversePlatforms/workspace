Putting it on your Gmail

  1. Signed in as M.Sharpe@SixfoldColorado.com, open Gmail, then Settings (gear) → See all settings → General.
  2. Under Signature, click Create new and name it Sixfold.
  3. Click into the box and paste (Ctrl+V).
  4. Under Signature defaults, pick it for new emails and for replies.
  5. Scroll down and click Save changes. Send a test to a personal address to check the logo shows.

Before you turn it on

The logo has to be on the website. Gmail only shows a picture from a public address. Open https://sixfoldcolorado.com/assets/email/sixfold-mark-email-64.png in your browser: if the orange mark shows, you're set. If not, upload sixfold-mark-email-64.png and sixfold-mark-email.png from sixfold\site\assets\email\ to public_html/assets/email/.

If the team footer is switched on, your emails would show two Sixfold blocks: this one, then the green footer under it. Either keep the footer and use a shorter signature (just your name, title and number), or move yourself to your own organizational unit in the admin console with no footer rule.
