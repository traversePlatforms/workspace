Sixfold email footer

One footer, added by Google Workspace to every email anyone at Sixfold sends. Below is how it looks under a message.

Hi Alex, thanks for walking the yard with us today. Your quote is attached.

Sixfold Colorado
Lawn & turf care · Landscaping · Land clearing · Fire mitigation
(303) 915-1045 · Contact@SixfoldColorado.com · SixfoldColorado.com
Monument, CO · serving El Paso, Teller and Douglas counties

The footer goes at the very end of each email, so in a long reply thread it sits under the quoted text.

1. Put the logo online (once)

Gmail's footer can only show a picture from a public website, not from Google Drive. Upload the two green files, sixfold-mark-email-green-64.png and sixfold-mark-email-green.png, from sixfold\site\assets\email\ to public_html/assets/email/ on sixfoldcolorado.com, so this address opens the picture:

https://sixfoldcolorado.com/assets/email/sixfold-mark-email-green-64.png

2. Add the footer for everyone

  1. Sign in to admin.google.com with the Sixfold super admin account.
  2. Go to Apps → Google Workspace → Gmail → Compliance.
  3. Pick the top organizational unit on the left, so it covers the whole team.
  4. Find Append footer and click Configure. Name it Sixfold footer.
  5. Click into the footer box and paste (Ctrl+V) what Copy footer copied. If the picture doesn't come across, put the cursor at the start, click the picture button and enter the address from step 1.
  6. Leave Also apply footer for internal messages unticked, so emails between the team stay clean.
  7. Click Save. Changes can take up to an hour to reach everyone. Send yourself a test to an outside address, like a personal Gmail.

3. Each person's own name (optional)

The footer is the same for everyone, so it carries the company, not a name. Anyone who wants their name and role on their emails can add a short personal signature in Gmail (Settings → See all settings → Signature), for example just their name and role on one line. Keep the phone, email and logo out of it, because the footer already adds them.

If anything comes across wrong in the paste (the admin editor keeps formatting, not page code), use Copy as plain text instead, paste it, set each line as in the table Claude gave you, and add the logo with the picture button.
