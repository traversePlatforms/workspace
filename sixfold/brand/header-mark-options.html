What to look at

B versus C. B centres the whole box, so the ground line sits low and the mark reads as hanging. C centres the solid mass instead and lets the roots overflow, so the ground line lands near the wordmark's baseline — the peaks and the word sit on the same line, the way they do on the business card.

Crowding. The bar is 64px. A 44px mark plus its roots is ~46px of artwork, so C and D leave roughly 9px of air above and below the solid part. Look at whether the mark starts to feel packed against the top edge of the bar.

Root legibility. At 44px the finest root stroke is 0.75 CSS px and the thickest 1.35. Still hairline, but on the dark ground they carry better than on Bone.
