## Square

Everything for logos and colour is in one place. Square's help article *Customize receipts* is the source for the sizes.

**Dashboard › Settings › Account & Settings › My business › About › Branding › + Create new**

| Square asks for | File | Size Square gives |
|---|---|---|
| **Small logo** | `square/square-logo-small-400x400.png` | 400 × 400 |
| **Full logo** | `square/square-logo-full-1280x648.png` (or the `-transparent` one on a coloured page) | at least 1280 × 648 |
| **Brand colour** | type **#2F5D2C** (forest green, from the logo) | digital receipts and invoices only |
| Printed receipt logo | `square/square-logo-print-400x200.png` (or the sharper `-800x400`) | at least 400 × 200. Printed receipts are black only, so this is black type. |
| Square Register customer screen | `square/square-customer-display-2560x1600.png` | 2560 × 1600 (Settings › Hardware › Customer Display) |

Invoices and receipts share the same logo; Square has no separate invoice logo. Square shows the small logo in a square, which is why it is the round badge on white.

**About (longer):**
> 365 Aeration & Seeding does core aeration and overseeding for lawns across the Denver metro. Aeration pulls small plugs of soil so water and fertilizer reach the roots of a compacted lawn, and seeding straight after it helps thicken thin spots. Ask for a quote at aeration365.com, or call or text (720) 830-4553.

Fact check on the text: plug depth, water and fertilizer reaching the roots, and seeding after aeration all come from CSU Extension PlantTalk 1505 and fact sheet 7.241 (see `../../../aeration365-site/FACT-CHECK.md`). The old Facebook bio's "fast scheduling, fair pricing" and "the two most effective ways" are **left out**: neither can be backed up.
