Logos

Badge: profile pictures, stickers, small spaces

Badge and name: headers, invoices, signs, shirts
  • Keep clear space around a logo at least the height of the "365" numbers.
  • Don't stretch it, recolour it, add shadows, or put it on a busy photo. On colour, use the cream or white backgrounds shown here.
  • Where only one colour prints (receipts, faxes, engraving), use the black type version: out/square/square-logo-print-400x200.png.

How we say it

  • Name: 365 Aeration & Seeding. Short: 365 Aeration.
  • Line: Pull a plug. Grow a lawn.
  • Plain and friendly. Say what we do and how to book.
  • Only say what we can back up. No "best", "most effective" or "guaranteed". Facts about aeration come from Colorado State University Extension.
