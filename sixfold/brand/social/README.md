## Composition

Three banner layouts off one lockup so every ratio stays consistent:

| Variant | Content | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| **full** | mark · Sixfold / LAND & FIRE / ember rule / "We do the work and show you who pays for it." / contact | Facebook, Google cover, 16:9 |
| **compact** | mark · Sixfold / LAND & FIRE / ember rule / contact | X, Nextdoor, LinkedIn personal, YouTube |
| **slim** | mark · Sixfold │ (303) 915-1045 | LinkedIn company (1128×191) |

Profile marks: **crest** (no roots) for small circle crops — LinkedIn, Facebook,
Instagram, X; **full** (with roots) for larger placements — Nextdoor, YouTube,
Google logo. Roots turn to mush below ~96px. Mark viewBox is tight to the drawn
artwork, not the `0 0 100 116` source canvas, so it centres on the mark, not on
empty space.

## Where each dimension came from

**LinkedIn, X and YouTube publish image specs** — used as-is. **Google Business
Profile** specs are verified first-party (Google Business Profile Help, *Manage
your Business Profile photos & videos*, read 2026-08-21): JPG/PNG, 10 KB–5 MB,
recommended 720×720, min 250×250; the logo shows only once the profile carries
name, address, category, phone and hours; photos appear 24–48 h after
verification.

**Facebook and Nextdoor do not publish usable specs** (Meta's help centre returns
HTTP 400 to automated reads; Nextdoor's business help carries no image
dimensions). `1640×624` (Facebook, 2.63:1) and `1440×400` / `1920×1080`
(Nextdoor) are long-standing working ratios. Every banner is centre-weighted and
symmetric, so an unknown crop takes the same amount off both sides. **The upload
dialog's crop preview is the authority — trust it over this file** and re-run the
generator if any platform's box differs.

## Standing rules honoured (profiles and bios too, not just print)

- No "licensed and insured", "certified", "bonded", "Firewise" — none true yet.
- No county cost-share by name until on the approved contractor list.
- No website URL (`SixfoldColorado.com` owned, not hosted). `Contact@SixfoldColorado.com`
  is live but is **not** on the banners.
- No street address — service area "Front Range".
- No price, offer, "free estimates", or track record.
- "We handle the paperwork for you" — banned phrasing, not used.

Lines on the banners are the short bio already fact-checked in
[../../marketing/facebook-page-copy.md](../../marketing/facebook-page-copy.md),
reused rather than reinvented.
