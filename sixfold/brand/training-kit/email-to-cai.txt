Hi Cai,

Here's the logo kit for the training packet (zip attached). It has the logo for dark and light pages, the mark on its own, and a one-page sheet with every colour.

Which logo to use:
- White or light pages: the solid green logo (the files ending "for-light-backgrounds").
- Dark pages or photos: the multi-green logo (the files ending "for-dark-backgrounds"), same as the business cards.

Colours for lettering:
- Text: Pine #0E1512 (RGB 14, 21, 18)
- Headings and accents: Turf #2F7A4F (RGB 47, 122, 79)
- Small labels and job titles: Moss #8AA07A (RGB 138, 160, 122)
- On a dark page, use Bone #EDE7DC (RGB 237, 231, 220) for text instead.

Font: Palatino Linotype for headings (Book Antiqua if you don't have it).

Please keep the logo at its original shape, no stretching or recolouring.
