Using the logo

  • White or light page: the solid green logo. The multi-green one disappears on white.
  • Dark page or photo: the multi-green logo, exactly as on the business cards.
  • Don't stretch, squash, recolour or add effects. Resize from the corner.
  • Leave clear space around it about the height of the small tree.
  • In print, keep it at least the size it is on the business card (just under 1 in wide), so the fine roots under the ground line still show.
  • The name is Sixfold; the public name is Sixfold Colorado.
