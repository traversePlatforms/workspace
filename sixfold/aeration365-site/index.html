What we do

Two jobs, done well, usually on the same visit.

Core aeration

A machine pulls small plugs of soil out of the lawn, about two to three inches deep. On compacted ground, that lets water and fertilizer reach the roots instead of running off.

Overseeding

We spread grass seed straight after aerating, the order Colorado State University Extension describes for thickening a thin lawn.

Good to know

1
Water if you canA little water a day or two before helps the machine go deeper. If you can't, no problem: we can run your sprinklers for a few minutes when we arrive.
2
The plugs stay on the lawnThey look a little messy for a week or two, then break down back into the grass. That's part of the benefit.
3
Spring and fall work bestAny time the ground isn't frozen, but not when it's hot and dry.
