## Errors found and corrected

**1. The credit cap was wrongly described as inflation-indexed.** Notebook sheet 4 showed tax years 2028–2030 as *"$2,000, indexed"*. Reading the enacted HB26-1289 text settles it — the inflation clause attaches to the **income threshold**, not the cap:

> *"...A LANDOWNER WITH AN ADJUSTED GROSS INCOME AT OR BELOW THREE HUNDRED THOUSAND DOLLARS... **AS ADJUSTED FOR INFLATION** AND ROUNDED TO THE NEAREST HUNDRED DOLLARS FOR EACH INCOME TAX YEAR THEREAFTER, IS ALLOWED A CREDIT... IN AN AMOUNT UP TO TWO THOUSAND DOLLARS. THE MAXIMUM TOTAL CREDIT IN A TAXABLE YEAR FOR A LANDOWNER IS TWO THOUSAND DOLLARS."* — §39-22-543(4)(c)

The parallel provision at (4)(b) confirms the reading: same sentence structure, and the Department of Revenue table shows the income limit moving ($120,000 → $126,300 → $129,200) while the cap stayed flat. **Corrected to "$2,000".** The AGI limit *is* indexed and still says so.

**2. The insurance discount was overstated in two places.** The notebook said an insurer "has to... discount documented mitigation." The enacted HB25-1182 is conditional: §(3) requires property- and community-level mitigation to be **incorporated into underwriting and pricing**, and §(4) requires a **discount only where the insurer does not incorporate it** into its models. Both lines rewritten to "count documented mitigation in how it underwrites and prices the property," which is true unconditionally. The client-facing Form C was already worded correctly and was not changed.

**3. Entity name.** Every document said "Sixfold LLC". The Colorado Secretary of State record (entity 20258292716) returns **"Sixfold, LLC"** with a comma. Corrected across the agreement, both cancellation notices, and the notebook — the contracting party and the seller named on a statutory notice should match the register exactly. The brand name stays "Sixfold" on banners, bio and QR.

**4. ZIP code filled.** 80110, confirmed directly from the same Secretary of State record, which returns the principal address as *333 W Hampden Ave Ste 300, Englewood, CO 80110, US*. This is the address a cancellation gets mailed to, so an incomplete one was a live defect.

- Applies to personally solicited sales agreed at the residence, $25+. §429.3 exemptions are **motor vehicles at auctions or tent sales, and arts and crafts at fairs** — checked specifically; neither is a route out.
- Ten-point bold statement in immediate proximity to the signature — present, page 3, `.legal10`.
- Notice of Cancellation in duplicate, statutory wording — pages 4 and 5, verbatim including the obsolete telegram line.
- No waiver or confession of judgment — none present.
- Refund all payments within 10 business days — clause 11 and section 5.

**C.R.S. 5-3-401 to 5-3-405** (home solicitation sales), official Title 5 PDF:
- 5-3-402: three business days. 5-3-403(1): the agreement must designate the date the buyer actually signs — page 1 does. 5-3-403(3): non-compliance extends cancellation to **three years**. 5-3-404: keep-the-payment clauses unenforceable; refund within ten days. 5-3-405(3): **no compensation for services performed** if cancelled.

**C.R.S. 6-1-105(1)(p)** — door-to-door sellers must identify themselves, whom they represent, and the purpose of the call within thirty seconds. On field sheet 1.

**C.R.S. 9-1.5-103(3)(b)** — excavation notice at least two business days before, not counting the day of notice. On field sheet 2.
**C.R.S. 9-1.5-105(1)** — confirms the notification association operates the 811 number, so "Colorado 811" in agreement clause 5 is accurate.

**HB26-1289 enacted text** (session laws Ch. 364): 2027–2030 window, $2,000 cap, $300,000 AGI indexed, refundable from 2027 and not before, beetle-infestation category additive, repeal moved to **1 January 2040**, same cap single or joint. All as stated in the notebook.

**HB25-1182 enacted text** (session laws Ch. 278): effective **1 July 2026**; annual written notice of score and applicable discounts; mitigation incorporated into underwriting and pricing; conditional discount duty; right to request a revised score.

**Colorado Department of Revenue**, *Income Tax Topics: Wildfire Mitigation Measures* and *Wildfire Tax Benefits*: credit table, four qualifying measures, CSFS/DFPC standards test, non-qualifying list, one-claimant rules, DR 0104CR + DR 0104 + receipts, and the retired DR 0104AD subtraction.
