From the price list to a working app

Every screen a company sees when it buys Traverse on the website, in order, and what the server does behind each one. Nothing on Traverse's side is manual. Taken on the local test copy with Stripe's test account; the company and people are made up.

1. Choosing and paying

Screen 1

The homepage's prices

Each plan's button is Start now and goes to the checkout with that plan picked. "Or book a demo first" stays under it. After a demo, you send the same page as a link, e.g. /start/?edition=roofing&plan=crew&iv=year&people=7.

The setup-time line is gone: "No setup fee and no waiting: pay, and your app opens with your homes and a short checklist." The old "$50 for Roofing and Solar" line is gone too.

Screen 2

The checkout page (/start/)

Trade, plan, yearly or monthly, how many people, and the terms box. The summary shows the founding price exactly as the app's Billing screen works it out (the list price struck through), what is due today, and the step up after 12 months.

Prices come from the server (billing.php), so the page cannot drift from what Stripe charges. The button stays off until a trade is picked and the Terms of Use and Subscription Agreement are ticked; the acceptance (versions, time, address) is kept.

Screen 2, phone

The same on a phone

One column; the summary and pay button follow the choices.

Screen 3

Stripe's payment page

Stripe's own page takes the card (it never touches our server). It shows the founding price directly: the subscription is made of the real founding prices you created, no coupon.

Behind it: a Subscription Schedule is made as soon as Stripe reports the payment: 12 months on the founding prices, then the list prices, locked in. If the buyer closes the tab after paying, they get an email with a link back to the next step, and you get an email about every new paid company.

2. The welcome page (three steps)

Screen 4

Where you work

First, the service area: around the business address (filled in from what they gave Stripe) with a radius slider, default 25 miles, or…

The address goes to the U.S. Census Bureau's geocoder (free, first-party). As soon as this step is sent, the server starts linking every town of the edition whose centre, or any home, is in the area: links to the edition's own files, never copies.

Screen 4b

…pick counties

Checkboxes of every Colorado county the edition covers, with how many homes each has.

Screen 5

Your brand

Colour (swatches or any colour) and logo (PNG, JPG or SVG, under 400 KB). The preview on the right shows it at once. Meanwhile: "Loading your homes in 25 mi around Englewood… this carries on while you finish."

Logos are cleaned on the server: pictures are redrawn (anything hidden in the file is dropped), SVGs keep their shapes only.

Screen 6

Your company and sign-in

Company name, their name, email, mobile and a password (10 characters or more). By now the homes are in: "672,592 homes in 72 towns are ready (25 mi around Englewood)."

Screen 7

Your Traverse is ready

The sign-in email, an admin code for a quick phone sign-in, and Open my app (it opens by itself after a few seconds). The same arrives by email.

In those seconds the server made the install from its edition: its own page folder and API files, its own data and private folders, the admin's sign-in, Traverse's super sign-in for you, company and colours, billing.json from Stripe's own events, and released at once: the 7-day money-back guarantee starts now. A double click or a retry makes one install, never two.

3. Inside their app

Screen 8

Straight into the welcome checklist

Signed in already (a one-use pass). The checklist: their homes, invite your team, connect your email, connect payments, import your customers, check your logo and colours. Each ticks itself when done; "Hide this list" puts it away.

Screen 8, computer

The same on a computer

Screen 9

If the homes are still loading

The app opens anyway with "Loading your homes in …" and fills in by itself when they are linked (here, Douglas County picked)…

Screen 9b

…then the homes are in

Screen 10

Outside Colorado (or no towns in the area)

The app still opens; pipeline, customers, jobs and the team all work. A plain note: "Door data for your area is being added." No setup-mode words. You get an email "NEEDS DOOR DATA" with what to do.

4. The team: invites with passwords

Screen 11

Invite a person

First and last name, email, mobile (optional), role, and that person's own switches (the ones the role takes: takes payments, does jobs, knocks doors, results, won and lost).

Screen 12

Invited, until they set a password

Their line says Invited, with Resend invite (a new link; the old one stops working) and Cancel invite.

Screen 13

The invite email

From "Peakline Roofing LLC via Traverse" (Traverse's own mailbox, so it works before they connect theirs), Reply-To the admin who sent it, with their logo and colour and a Set your password button. The link works once, for 7 days.

Screen 14

Set a password

The link opens the app on "Welcome, Marco"; a password twice; then they are signed in (and asked to accept the Terms of Use, as every new person is).

Screen 15

Sign in: code or email

The 6-digit code keypad stays for everyone who has one; under it, "Sign in with email and password".

Screen 16

Email and password

Screen 17

Forgot password

Always the same answer (so nobody can test which emails exist); the link works once, for 24 hours. Wrong passwords are rate limited.

5. Your side

Screen 18

Profile: Clients

Beside Edition and Role: a dropdown of every client with where its plan stands; picking one opens it as Traverse.

Screen 19

All clients

Search, a status filter (live, setting up, past due, locked, cancelled), plan, trade, since when, and their homes.

Screen 20

Inside a client's app, as Traverse

Billing there has Refund and cancel and Give more time, as in the editions. Past due locks it after 3 days, paying opens it at once; all of that arrives through the one central Stripe webhook.
