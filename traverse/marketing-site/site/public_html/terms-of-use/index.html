2. What the app records

So that your company can run its teams and so that sign-ins are not shared, the app records:

  • When and on what kind of device you sign in (for example "iPhone" or "Windows computer"), and when you sign out.

  • Downloads made from the app, and unusually heavy viewing of door data.
  • Flags for a sign-in that looks shared. We compare where the doors you mark are (from each door's address, not from your phone) and the time between marks. Two marks further apart than anyone could drive in that time are flagged for your company's admin, and so is a code that moves between phones several times in a day. A flag is a prompt to ask, not proof.

Your location. The app asks for your phone's location only when you choose to use it (for example "nearest door" or the map). It is used on your phone to show where you are and to order your stops. The app does not send it to Traverse or store it, and it does not track you in the background. You can say no and still use the app.

Your company's admins can see what you record and the records above. Our privacy policy explains the rest.
