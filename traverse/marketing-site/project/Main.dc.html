How it works

From the first door to the signed job

STATION 01

Load your territory

The homes in the towns you work, grouped into neighbourhoods your crews can claim. Working somewhere new? We add towns every week.

STATION 02

Knock and mark

Reps work street by street and mark every door. The office sees each one as it happens.

STATION 03

Book the appointment

A yes at the door becomes a booked walk on the calendar and a lead in the pipeline, with its owner set.

THE CLOSE

Win and keep the customer

The signed job becomes a customer record with their visits, what is due next and a review request.

Questions

What people ask

Something else? Ask us in your demo, or email hello@traverseplatforms.com.

Where does Traverse work?

We started on Colorado's Front Range and add towns every week. Tell us where you work when you book a demo, and we'll build your towns for you.

How accurate is the information on each door?

Much of it is an estimate worked out from published sources, and the app says so where it is. Check anything that matters before you rely on it, and never present an estimate to a homeowner as a measurement.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Doors, notes and photos are saved on the phone and sent the moment the signal returns.

What do my crews need?

A phone with a web browser. Traverse installs to the home screen from the browser, so there's no app store and nothing to update. It runs on computers and tablets too.

Who owns our customers and notes?

You do. Your company's records stay in your own copy of Traverse, are never shown to another company, and can be downloaded any time.

How does setup work?

We set up your company's app for you, with your name, colour and towns, then show your admin how to add the team.

Can we cancel?

Yes. Monthly plans cancel any time and run to the end of the month you've paid for. Yearly plans run to the end of the year. You can download your records before you go.

Can a team share one sign-in?

No. Each person has their own, so you can see who did what. A sign-in works on one phone and one computer at a time.
