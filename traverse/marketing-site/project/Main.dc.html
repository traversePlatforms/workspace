How it works

From the first lead to the paid job

STATION 01

Map your territory

Every property in the towns you work, with the facts for your trade. Storms and severe weather show up as they happen.

STATION 02

Work every lead

Doors, calls, your website and your ads feed one pipeline. Each lead has an owner, notes and a next step on the calendar.

STATION 03

Quote and sign

A quote that fills in from the property, signed on the homeowner's phone, with the deposit taken by card or your own pay link.

STATION 04

Run the jobs

Put each job on the schedule, give every crew its day, and see the work marked done.

THE OFFICE

Payments due, results by rep and neighborhood, and every customer with their history, next visit and a review request.

Questions

What people ask

Something else? Ask in your demo, or email hello@traverseplatforms.com.

Where does Traverse work?+

Across Colorado, with more than a million properties mapped and more added all the time. Tell us where you work when you book a demo.

How does setup work?+

Pick your trade and plan, pay, and answer a few questions: where you work, your logo and colors, and your admin sign-in. Your app opens with your homes, and a short checklist walks you through inviting your team, connecting email and payments, and bringing in your current customers, leads and past work.

Is every feature in every plan?+

Yes. Solo, Team and Company all get every feature from day one. They differ in how many people they cover.

Does the app carry our brand?+

Yes. Your logo, name and colors go on the app, your quotes, signing forms and emails.

Does it work offline?+

Yes. Crews keep working with no signal: doors, notes and photos are saved on the device and upload as soon as a connection is back.

What do my crews need?+

A phone, tablet or computer. Large, high-contrast text stays readable in bright sun, and big, solid buttons work with gloves on.

Who owns our customers and notes?+

You do. Your company's records stay in your own copy of Traverse, are never shown to another company, and can be downloaded any time.

Can we cancel?+

Yes. Monthly plans cancel any time and run to the end of the month you've paid for. Yearly plans run to the end of the year. You can download your records before you go. Every plan also has a 7-day money-back guarantee from the day your app is live: tell us and we refund the payment in full.
