Not counted in the totals

  • Card fees on what you sell. Stripe takes 2.9% + 30¢ per card payment, or 0.8% (capped at $5) per bank debit. On a $200 monthly subscription that is about $6.10 by card or $1.60 by bank debit.
  • Colorado sales tax on subscriptions from 1 January 2027 (HB 26-1223). Your customer pays it and you file it, so it changes your quotes, not your costs. Home-rule cities such as Colorado Springs (3.07%) and Denver (5.15%) need their own registration.
  • The Do Not Call list. Each client that makes the calls needs its own access: the first five area codes are free, then $85 each a year from 1 October 2026. Their cost, not Traverse's.
  • Trademark extras. The USPTO adds $100 to $200 per class if the description is not picked from its standard list, and asks for maintenance filings in years 5 to 6 and every 10 years.
  • The attorney until you type a quote into that line.
  • Prices change. Porkbun will likely raise its .com price after Verisign's 1 November 2026 increase, and the SiteGround and .app prices shown are first-year offers where marked.
