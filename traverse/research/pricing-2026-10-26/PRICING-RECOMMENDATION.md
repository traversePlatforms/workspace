## Why this shape

**1. The data is the reason to buy, so every plan has it.** The 10 Oct plans had a "Crew" tier without edition data at
$35–$45 a user. That puts Traverse head to head with RepCard ($24–$49), Knockio ($20–$60) and LeadScout ($8–$28), where it
has no edge and the lowest price wins. The per-house data costs nothing extra to serve, so holding it back only makes the
cheap plan look like every other app.

**2. Per company with users included is how small trade companies already buy.** Jobber ($299 for 5 users, $29 each after),
Housecall Pro ($189 for 5, $329 for 8), QuoteIQ ($199.99 for 4) and SiteRecon ($400 for 5) all price this way. The owner
signs one bill; adding a rep feels small. For Traverse it means a floor of revenue per customer: a 3-person crew is $199–$249,
not 3 × $35 = $105.

**3. About half the stack, not a race to the bottom.** A roofer with SalesRabbit loaded pays $87–$137 per rep per month
before the hail map and owner list, and signs for 1–3 years with a setup package. Traverse Crew + Roofing for 5 reps is about
$50–$60 per rep with the data in and no contract. That gap is wide enough to switch for, from a new company, and leaves room
to raise prices after the first references.

**4. Revenue first.** Yearly prepay (about 20% off) brings cash in now. The founding offer is shorter and smaller than the
10 Oct one (30% off Pro for 12 months) but adds a price lock, which costs nothing today and is the thing early customers value.

**5. Honest about what isn't there yet.** Coverage is the Colorado Front Range (Roofing 365,271 homes in 27 towns; Security
326,213; Yards 313,415; Solar 263,605; Pest 144,343; editions built 26 Oct). The office side Sixfold runs on (jobs, schedule,
quote pages, signed forms, Square payments, stats) is built but switched on only for Sixfold, so the editions don't compete
with Jobber on invoicing yet. Price as a door-sales app with data; add the office side as a paid tier once each trade's
version is checked (see "Later").

## Revenue it points to (illustration, my arithmetic)

| Customers | Mix | Monthly revenue (month-to-month list) |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | 3 Crew, 1 Crew + Roofing, 1 Team | $747 + $299 + $549 = **$1,595** |
| 10 | 6 Crew, 2 Crew + Roofing, 2 Team | $1,494 + $598 + $1,098 = **$3,190** |
| 20 | 10 Crew, 5 Crew + Roofing, 4 Team, 1 Team + Roofing | $2,490 + $1,495 + $2,196 + $599 = **$6,780** |

Founding discount takes 25% off the first ten for a year. Stripe takes about 2.9% + $0.30 a charge (Stripe's standard US
rate; check the account's own rate). Hosting is a flat monthly bill; the data is almost all free public record, so the cost
that grows with customers is setup and support time.
