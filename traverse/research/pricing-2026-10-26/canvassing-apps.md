## SalesRabbit

Source: https://salesrabbit.com/pricing/ (first-party, read 2026-10-26)

**Plans (per seat per month)**

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|

| Pro | $75 | $49 | Everything in Team, plus custom fields, map overlays, customer locator, lead file attachments, Amplify Gamification, API and integrations, Scheduler for setters and closers (Appointment Manager is included in Pro and Enterprise, not sold separately) |
| Enterprise | Contact sales | Contact sales | Everything in Pro, plus Amplify Coaching, SSO, multiple Amplify accounts, custom SLA. All add-ons and custom integrations at unpublished prices |

**Add-ons (per seat per month)**

| Add-on | Annual | Quarterly | Monthly | Availability |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DataGrid AI | $19 | $26 | $31 | Team, Pro, Enterprise (the only add-on offered on Team) |
| Weather (hail/storm overlays) | $19 | $26 | $31 | Pro, Enterprise |
| Mover Leads | $13 | $17 | $20 | Pro, Enterprise |
| Digital Contracts | $13 | $17 | $20 | Pro, Enterprise. No per-contract processing fee |

DataGrid AI (https://salesrabbit.com/datagrid/, first-party, read 2026-10-26) claims "homeowner information for every home", data on "multiple members of the household", and a "Buyer Score" for homes, neighbourhoods, counties, cities and states. That page does not say whether names, phone numbers or emails are included, and it gives no usage caps.

**Terms (from the pricing FAQ)**
- Billing is by **seat**: the maximum number of users active at once. Annual and quarterly invoices charge for "the minimum number of seats required or the number of active users, whichever is higher." No numeric seat minimum is published.
- Annual is paid upfront for 12 months. Quarterly is paid upfront for 3 months. Seats added mid-term are prorated.
- **Contracts: "we do contracts from one to three years"**, with pricing locked for the term.
- **Implementation: required.** Tiers are Basic, Guided, Premium and Custom, with no prices published. Re-signing after 30 days triggers a new implementation fee.
- No cancellation fee. No refund for a mid-cycle cancellation.
- No free trial of paid plans.

**SalesRabbit + RoofLink bundle** (https://salesrabbit.com/srrl-bundle/, first-party, read 2026-10-26): SalesRabbit Pro, DataGrid AI, Weather, Digital Contracts and RoofLink Pro. Marketed as "over 40% off the list price" and "limited-time". No price published.

## RoofLink

- https://salesrabbit.com/pricing/ (first-party, read 2026-10-26) lists **"RoofLink Pro": $120 per user per month, billed monthly**. Includes roof measuring and estimates, inspections, product library, basic canvassing, weather data, and QuickBooks/financing integration.
- https://rooflink.com/pricing (first-party, read 2026-10-26) lists **"$400 user / month" plus "a one-time $1,500 setup fee"**. "One flat rate, no per-measurement costs, no surprise add-ons." Covers targeting, measurements, estimates, approvals, production, collections, canvassing and storm/weather mapping. Integrations: SRS, QuickBooks, Hover, Stripe, CompanyCam, Zapier, EagleView. The page doesn't name its plan. Its login menu shows separate "Pro" and "Enterprise" logins.
- **Conflict:** both pages are first-party and the figures differ. They may be two tiers ($120 = "RoofLink Pro"; $400 = the full product), but neither page says so. Contract length and trial are not stated on either page.
