Research for Traverse pricing. All vendor pages were read on **2026-10-26** unless a line says otherwise. Prices are USD as the vendor page showed them that day. "Hidden" means the vendor page shows no figure and points to sales or a demo.

Third-party figures appear only where they carry a date and nothing conflicts with them, and each is marked **third-party** with its date. Where third-party figures conflict and the vendor publishes nothing, the figures are left out and the conflict is noted.
