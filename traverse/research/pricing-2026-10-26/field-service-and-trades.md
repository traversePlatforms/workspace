# Field-service and trade software pricing — 26 Oct 2026

Prices for software that small lawn/landscaping, solar, pest-control and home-security companies use for sales and service. Gathered to inform Traverse's own prices. Facts only, no recommendations.

**How this was gathered.** Every vendor pricing page listed below was fetched on **2026-10-26** unless the fact-check log says otherwise. Figures are USD and exclude tax. Where a vendor hides pricing, this file says so and does not fill the gap from listicles or review aggregators. Third-party figures are marked as such, with a date, and kept only where they are dated and nothing first-party contradicts them. In practice none met that bar, so this file uses **no third-party prices**.

**Extraction caveat.** Pages were read as text. On several pages (Jobber, Service Autopilot, LMN, Deep Lawn, Aurora) the feature-comparison grid's tick marks did not survive extraction. Where that happened, tier inclusions come from each plan card's own description, and anything the card doesn't say is marked "not confirmed".

Door-to-door canvassing apps (SalesRabbit, SPOTIO, RepCard) appear here only where they serve solar or security. A separate file in this folder, `canvassing-apps.md`, covers that category.
