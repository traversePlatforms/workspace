Where to sell first

  • Lawn and landscaping companies are the largest group: Yards' buyers.
  • They cluster in Denver, Colorado Springs, Aurora, Fort Collins, Longmont, Littleton and Lakewood.
  • Aurora is being built now: 60,757 doors in 1,533 neighbourhoods.
  • Larimer County (Fort Collins, Loveland) has the most lawn companies per home, and no towns built yet.

Against the competition

  • SalesRabbit charges $49 to $75 per rep a month, and $19 to $31 more each for its data and weather add-ons.
  • RepCard's canvassing plan is $49 per rep a month; SPOTIO quotes only, for teams of five or more.
  • Traverse Crew is $35 to $45 per person with the door data built in: hail at the house, roof age, permits, lawn size, trees.

Business model

One install per company, in its trade's edition, sold by the seat. The door data is the product; the app is how a crew uses it. Prices are the ones set in Traverse's billing settings.

Founding companies

  • 14-day free pilot before the first charge.
  • 30% off Pro for the first year (a Stripe coupon).
  • The pilot is how the first roofer and the first lawn company are signed.

One worked example (arithmetic on these prices)

  • A six-person crew on Crew, yearly: 6 × $35 = $210 a month.
  • Stripe takes 2.9% + 30¢ + 0.7%: about $8.
  • Fixed running costs today are about $37 a month (hosting renewal and email), plus your Claude plan.
  • So one such company covers the fixed costs; every company after that is mostly margin until hosting needs an upgrade.

What protects it

  • Only Traverse's super admin downloads the door list, 500 at a time, and every download is logged.
  • The client agreement (outline for counsel ready) licenses the data in the app only.
  • Each company's own customers and notes stay theirs.
