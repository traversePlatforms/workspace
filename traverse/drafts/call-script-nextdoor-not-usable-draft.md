# Traverse: short call script for the call list (draft, 31 Oct 2026)

For Matthew, calling the 8 companies on Desktop "Traverse call list - Nextdoor not usable.txt" (6 roofing, 2 lawn and tree; Reliable Roofing has no number on file, so 7 calls). Draft only. Calls go to business numbers listed on their own websites. After each call, mark it on the Prospect Desk: **Called / left voicemail**, plus a note of what was said.

Keep it under a minute unless they ask questions. The aim is a 15-minute demo on the calendar, not a sale on the phone.

---

## If the owner (or whoever runs sales) picks up

> Hi, is this {company}? This is Matthew with Traverse, here in Colorado. I'll be quick.
>
> I built an app for roofing companies that puts the door maps, CRM, quotes and the office in one place. Every house card shows the hail at that house, the roof size and the roof's age, so reps knock the best doors first.
>
> I'm opening it to a few small Colorado companies at a founding price, and I'd like to show you your own area. Do you have 15 minutes this week?

For lawn and tree companies, swap the middle line: "Every house card shows the lawn size, so reps know which doors are worth knocking, and quotes go out from the phone."

## If they say "we already use something"

> Makes sense. What are you using now? ... A lot of small teams use two or three tools for the same job. If you ever want to see it all in one app, I'm happy to show you. Can I send you the link?

(Note what they use on the desk card. Don't argue with it.)

## If they say "not interested" / "too busy"

> No problem at all. Thanks for picking up. Have a good one.

Mark **Not now** on the desk if they're busy, **No** if they're clear they don't want it.

## Voicemail (20 seconds)

> Hi, this is Matthew with Traverse in Colorado. I built an app for {roofing / lawn} companies that puts door maps, quotes and the office in one place. I'd like to show you your area in a quick 15-minute demo. You can book a time at traverseplatforms.com, or call me back at 303-596-9515. Thanks.

## Notes

- Don't say how we found them or mention Nextdoor.
- Don't name where the data comes from.
- Proof, if asked: "A client has had success with it." No trade named, no client named.
- These are business lines the companies list for customers. Still: no calls before 8 am or after 9 pm, and take any "don't call again" at its word (mark No).
