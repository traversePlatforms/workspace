## Where each county's data comes from

Checked on the cloud machine on 29 Oct with group3/coverage-g3.mjs (results in group3/coverage.json; decisions in group3/county-sources.json): for each county, the statewide parcel layer's row count, a 1,000-row sample, a sample in a 0.75-mile box round its biggest place, and the state's address points in the same box joined by parcel number.

- **20 counties are not on the statewide parcel layer at all** (no owners, so no owner-lives-here test). Eight of them have the state's address points but nothing to say who owns the house.
- **co (26 counties):** the statewide layer carries owner, mailing address and property address. build-walklist-co.mjs, exactly as Groups 1 and 2.
- **ap (2 counties, Eagle and Custer):** the parcel rows have no property address (0-2%), so it comes from the address points, joined by parcel number: group3/build-walklist-ap.mjs, Pueblo's adapter made general (any county; parcel numbers compared without punctuation or leading zeros; a parcel row's own property address used when it has one and no point; land use dropped when it reads as non-residential; organisation owners dropped).
- **Owner names withheld:** Eagle puts one placeholder in place of every owner name and Summit leaves the name blank (mailing addresses are real in both). Doors in these two counties will carry no owner name. The owner-lives-here test does not need the name. build-walklist-ap.mjs spots a placeholder (one value on over half the parcels) and leaves the owner blank.
- **Partial counties:** property address on only part of the rows, so expect a part of the houses: Gilpin (49% in the county sample, 5% round Central City), Yuma (11% county-wide, 87% in Yuma town), Morgan (57%), Huerfano (61-68%), Teller (48% round Cripple Creek), Clear Creek (40% county-wide, 83% in Idaho Springs); Custer's address points join about a third of parcels.

## What "doors" means, and what to expect

Stage A writes every house in the lot band (0-10 acres) that has a property address. Each row says whether the owner lives there ("YES") or should get a postcard ("no — post"). Town files keep only the "YES" rows, so the number to compare is the **owner-lives-here count**.

**Smoke test on the cloud machine, 29 Oct** (group3/test, not in out/):

| Test | Method | Census houses | Owner-occupied | Rows | Owner lives here | Local PO box |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ignacio (La Plata) | co | 329 | 222 | 304 | 22 | 186 |
| Dotsero (Eagle) | ap | 336 | 295 | 274 | 134 | 0 |

**The PO-box finding.** In Ignacio 199 of 304 owners mail to a PO box, 186 of them in Ignacio's own ZIP or town: a small town without home delivery. The owner-lives-here rule since Group 1 says a PO box is "no — post", so those houses drop out. Mountain and farm towns across Group 3 will do the same (Summit's parcel rows are 57% PO boxes). summary.tsv therefore has a **local_po_box** column: rows whose owner's PO box is in the house's own ZIP or town. Whether those count as "lives here" is Matthew's call at stage B. The CSVs hold everything needed, so no second pull.

**Expected owner-lives-here doors:** about **50,000-95,000** under today's rule (40-75% of the 131,900 owner-occupied houses: the high end is Group 2's rate, the low end allows for PO-box towns and the partial counties). Counting local PO boxes as well could add a large share of what is left. These are estimates; the real counts are in ~/run/group3/out/summary.tsv.

## The build list (172 targets, in run order)

Counties in order of their Census gap, biggest first. In each county the places come first, biggest first: every town or CDP of 150+ Census houses under a quarter covered. Then each county subdivision is swept as a "rural" target: the land outside the places plus anything a place's pull missed. Houses already pulled by an earlier target are dropped (same parcel number), so a sweep never repeats a place. "Census houses" for a sweep is the subdivision minus the places inside it. A place that spans two counties is pulled in the county holding most of it. Its part in the other county comes in through that county's sweep. Places under 150 houses come in through their subdivision's sweep. No military housing in these counties.

**Overlaps with towns of ours (stage B should merge, not make a second town):** g3-cripple-creek (18 of our doors against 459 houses, from our cripple-creek town) and g3-idaho-springs (84 of 633, our idaho-springs). ## How it runs

- **Stage A, door lists (running on the cloud machine since 29 Oct 16:19Z):** group3/run-group3.sh in ~/run/group3, one target at a time in the order above. co counties use build-walklist-co.mjs --pieces (the Group 2 copy: 6 tries per page at 3 s steps); Eagle and Custer use build-walklist-ap.mjs. Each pull is clipped by clip-group3.mjs (Group 2's clip, unchanged: same house = same parcel number; our doors within 5 m). Resumable (<key>.done). It refuses to start while another parcel job runs and stops under 5 GB free. One parcel-heavy job at a time on gis.colorado.gov: nothing else pulls parcels while it runs.
- **Check progress:** on the cloud machine, `cat ~/run-status.txt` (lines start "group3 A:"; "pull <key> (n/172 ...)"), the log `~/run-group3.log` (one "new doors, owner lives here" line per target), and `ls ~/run/group3/*.done | wc -l`. **Done** when the status reads "group3 A: door lists ready" and ~/run/group3/g3a.vmdone exists; counts in ~/run/group3/out/summary.tsv (key, county, method, census, owner_occupied, rows, owner_lives_here, local_po_box). If it ends "... targets MISSING", look at raw/<key>.log for those keys and re-run the script: finished targets are skipped.
- **Owner names stay on the cloud machine** in ~/run/group3/raw and out. They are not copied to the laptop in stage A and are never committed (group3/raw, out, test and *.csv are gitignored).
- **Stage B (after Matthew looks at the counts, and needs his go):** decide the local-PO-box rule and whether Eagle and Summit doors without owner names are wanted. Then, as Group 2: register each target as a town (build-rounds-data SOURCES, centres, groups, county in assessor/town-county.json, survey in sample-trees), merge g3-cripple-creek and g3-idaho-springs into our existing towns, walk-list areas, yard v2 and trees on the cloud machine, values and year built where the county allows (to be checked), the editions' layers, rebuild, upload in batches with backups, md5 and index.json merged. The app's COUNTY_NAME has none of these counties yet, so the towns would fold under "Other towns" until it does.
