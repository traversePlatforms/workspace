# Gap plan: toward every house in Colorado (8 Nov 2026)

Matthew, 8 Nov: "we are sitting at 1.24 million and I know Colorado has over 2 mill residential properties so I want to keep pulling more. I'd like to have every property in Colorado on the list."

**Nothing in this plan has gone live.** The cloud machine has counted the gap and started building the safe batches (below). No finisher pushes anything until Matthew has read this and the first batch's numbers.

## The short version

- **The target.** Colorado has 2,589,053 housing units. **1,788,606** of them are houses and townhomes (Census ACS 2020-24, B25024: 1-unit detached + attached). The rest are apartments and mobile homes, which aren't door-knock targets. Of the 1,788,606 houses: **1,416,681 are owner-occupied, 252,068 rented, 119,857 vacant** (second homes, for sale). The tenure split is from B25032.
- **What we have.** The 31 Oct count of 1,237,418 includes 5,088 commercial test doors. That leaves **1,232,330 house doors**, plus **269,259 "office lookup" homes**: houses we already hold, measured and on the server, shown only in the office House lookup and never on a door list.
- **Why we are short.** The door lists hold only houses whose owner lives there. That rule was on purpose: the knock is for the owner. Our 1,232,330 doors are **87% of Colorado's owner-occupied houses**. So most of the gap is not missing data but the rule:
  - **~372,000** rented or vacant houses. 269,259 of these are already our lookup homes.
  - **~184,000** owner-occupied houses we lack. Measured below, they break down into: never pulled (El Paso, Front Range edges), a broken PO-box test in mountain towns, and the 13 counties with no usable source.
- **What can be added:**
  - **~105,000 under today's rules.** About 70,000 of these are built or building on the cloud machine now.
  - **~400,000 more if renters and second homes become doors.** This is Matthew's call. 269,259 of them are already measured.
  - **~51,500 cannot be filled**: 13 counties (Fremont, Chaffee and Otero by their terms; the rest have no bulk source).

## How it was measured

The cloud machine read the whole state parcel layer (2,599,937 parcels, 44 counties, 52 min) and sorted every parcel against our 1,232,330 doors and 269,259 lookup homes. A parcel matches when a door or lookup home sits within 6 m of its point (15 m in El Paso), or the same address sits within 150 m. Every parcel that doesn't match gets the first reason that applies: not a home, apartments, condo, mobile, farm house, no address, over 10 acres, organisation owner, owner lives here (= should be a door today), local PO box, or owner elsewhere.

El Paso's state rows carry shapes only. There, the county assessor's own file (EPC Parcel Data, 277,915 parcels) supplied the address, use, owner test and acreage, joined by parcel number. Census by county: ACS 2020-24 through the API.

Files: `gap1108/gap-counts-2026-11-08.json` (counts only, with the top cities for each county's "owner lives here" misses), `gap1108/acs-county-2026-11-08.json`, `gap1108/census-vs-doors.cjs`.

Scripts: `gap1108/gap-pull-vm.mjs` (the pull; it stores no owner names), `gap1108/gap-classify.mjs`, `gap1108/export-door-points.mjs`.

## What cannot be filled, and why

- **Fremont (15,459 houses), Chaffee (9,817), Otero (6,790): the terms forbid it.** Fremont's county GIS layer is "NOT licensed for public or third-party use" and is sold by fee schedule. Chaffee: "the only entity where this data can be purchased". Otero: no redistribution without an agreement. Only by buying or signing (TR-36).
- **Cheyenne, Phillips, Moffat (6,554 houses): a public layer but no licence text.** They need the county's permission first. Matthew to email them (TR-36).
- **Prowers, Kit Carson, Lincoln, Baca, Crowley, Jackson, Kiowa (12,882 houses): no public bulk source.** Records requests only; Baca sells spreadsheets.
- **Mobile homes** (Larimer 5,450, statewide 7,857 owner-lived): not in the Census house figure, and the walk lists drop them on purpose. Can be added if wanted.
- **Second homes in resort counties** never "live here". Under the renters rule (5-6) they come in as absentee doors. Under today's rule they stay out by design.
