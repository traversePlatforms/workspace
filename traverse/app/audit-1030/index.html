B. Live radar

Works on every size. It looks blocky because the IEM composite is a ~0.5 km grid, the app asks for it at zoom 8 (RADAR_NATIVE = 8) and stretches it 16-128x, and an iPad doubles every stretched pixel again. Recommended: NWS MRMS conus_bref_qcd from opengeo.ncep.noaa.gov, drawn with interpolations=bicubic at 512 px retina (panel C). NOAA/NWS public domain: "may be used without charge for any lawful purpose"; GetCapabilities Fees none, AccessConstraints none. Credit "Radar: NOAA NWS MRMS". Details and the other five options in REPORT.md part B; the comparison page is radar-compare.html.

C. Office dashboard map

Cold: Roofing desktop 0.71 s to the map (18 requests, 329 KB), tablet 1.5-2.0 s; Sixfold desktop 10.1 s because its home does not pre-load the 1.7 MB towns list and the harness cache was cold (live warms it hourly). Warm: 0.2-0.3 s. 8,619 SVG circles in the harness; the live editions have 49,662 neighbourhoods, which an iPad draws in 1.5 s (13 s with the CPU throttled 4x) and pans in 0.5-2.9 s. Recommendation: the office does not need it. The same answer is on Home ("Best neighborhoods to knock next"), Plan the properties and Territories; take the map off the dashboard and link to Plan.
