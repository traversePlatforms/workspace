What changed

  • Source: NWS MRMS conus/conus_bref_qcd (1 km, quality controlled) from opengeo.ncep.noaa.gov over WMS 1.3.0, EPSG:3857, transparent PNG, interpolations=bicubic, 512 px images at the map's own zoom (256 CSS px of them on a retina screen). Nothing is stretched in the browser any more; before, tiles were asked for at zoom 8 and scaled 16-128x.
  • Time: the layer's time dimension from GetCapabilities (60 frames, 2 h), shared by every open map and re-read every 2.5 minutes; the map asks for the latest frame by its time and redraws when a newer one appears (checked each minute). If the list cannot be read the server's latest is shown with no time, refreshed every 2 minutes.
  • Play last 2 hours: 24 past frames 5 minutes apart (the listed frame nearest each step) plus the live frame, drawn at zoom 9 at most to keep a loop light; each frame's time and age shown; Stop as before.
  • Colour key: the NWS reflectivity scale in six bands (very light, light rain, moderate, heavy, very heavy, hail likely), on the Live radar screen, the office map and now in the Doors map's radar panel.
  • Credit "Radar: NOAA NWS MRMS". Licence note in the code: NOAA/NWS data, public domain (weather.gov/disclaimer); GetCapabilities Fees none, AccessConstraints none. No key, nothing paid.
  • Fallback: a tile error brings one redraw; errors again with nothing loaded and the map switches to the IEM NEXRAD tiles it used before, with a one-line notice and IEM's credit, for as long as that map is open.

Tests (harness on port 8810, live NWS requests)

  • radar-test.mjs, edition mode: 94 passed, 0 failed. Sixfold mode: 94 passed, 0 failed. Phone 390x844 (dpr 3), tablet 820x1180 and 1180x820 (dpr 2), desktop 1440x900 (dpr 1): MRMS tiles load as HTTP 200 PNGs; tile bbox matches the map zoom at zoom 9 and zoom 15; "as of" from the time list; the loop asks 25 distinct frame times and its tiles load; key, credit, switch, Spanish phrases; the Doors map's switch; with the NWS host dead the IEM tiles take over in about 4 s with the notice; no script errors.
  • wx-test.mjs (the 29 Oct weather suite, its radar lines updated for the new source), edition mode: 40 passed, 0 failed.

Limits

  • MRMS outage: the first map to hit it waits one retry (about 3 s) before switching to IEM; the switch is per open map, so the next map opened tries NWS again. IEM's loop is the last hour (its past layers), not two hours.
  • Load: a dry 512 px tile is about 6 KB; in rain 260-390 KB at about 1-1.3 s each (measured over southeast Kansas tonight). A live view is 6-20 tiles; a loop at zoom 9 is 24 frames of 2-6 tiles each (about 150 requests on a tablet). NWS asks not to retry more than once a minute, which the engine keeps to.
  • Smoother, not more detailed: 1 km data against IEM's 0.5 km grid; street-level detail would need the single-radar super-resolution layers (option D in the audit).
