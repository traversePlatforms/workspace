App notes, 30 Oct

Each change on a computer and a phone, from the test copy (a made-up company, test codes). Nothing here is live until it is deployed.

1. Company: each Setup entry opens its own part

Setup's "Your company" opens name, phone, email, website and review link. "Your logo and color" opens the color and logo. The Office menu's Company shows both, each under its heading.

2. The Office menu fits the window

The cause: the Office menu beside Accounts and the other office screens was sticky with no height limit, so on a short window its bottom ran past the screen. It now fits the window and scrolls by itself.

3 and 6. Setup: Back comes here; Training is in it

Back from every Setup choice returns to Setup; Back from Setup goes to the Office on a computer and Home on a phone. Training is listed beside Import and Your costs.

5 and 11. Billing for a company that already pays

Plan and people, the price now, the founding price and the day it steps up, the next charge, Manage billing for invoices, card and canceling, and a request to add people or change plan (an email to Traverse). No pay-to-start screen for a client install. Plans read Solo, Team, Company.

6 and 10. The welcome checklist

"Train your team" opens Training. The line "each one opens where it is done" is gone.

7. Your service area

In Setup for a self-serve client: a radius of 5 to 100 miles around the business, or counties. Colorado only. On save the towns are linked again.

8. Zelle QR code

An optional QR code picture on Payments, shown with the Zelle email or phone on the quote and signing pages and the job's balance.

9. Forgot your code

On the code screen, "Forgot your code?" emails a link that sets a password, to the email on the account (or, for the first admin, the company's email on file). Traverse can also email an admin that link from Accounts. No code is shown.
