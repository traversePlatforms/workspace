Written 8 Nov 2026 for Matthew, who is creating the Page himself. Nothing here has been posted, published or sent.
Images are in `out/` beside this file (and in `Desktop\Traverse\Facebook page\`), built by `build-facebook-images.mjs`.
Facebook moves its screens around: where a step below doesn't match what Facebook shows, the screen wins. Character counts are
exact (counted with Node). Limits marked "Facebook's limit" are ones Facebook enforces; the rest have no published limit that I
could confirm.

The Page text below (name, categories, bio, About, contact details, button, usernames, instant reply, pinned post, settings) is

## 1. Create the Page (facebook.com/pages/create)

Facebook asks for these three first, then "Create Page".

| Field | Type this | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Page name | `Traverse` | 8 characters |
| Category | `Software Company` first (it becomes the label under the name), then add `Business Service` | up to 3 categories (Facebook's limit) |
| Bio | `The all-in-one sales platform for the field and office. Built in Colorado for roofing, lawn & more.` | 99 of 101 (Facebook's limit) |

Categories: both are on Facebook's own list (type the start of the name and pick it from the drop-down; don't make up a new one).
Leave the third slot empty, or add `App Page` if you want one; it isn't needed.

## 3. After the Page exists: Edit Page and settings

### Action button (the blue button under the cover)

"+ Add action button" (or Edit button) > **Book now** > "Link to website" (not "Book on Facebook") >
`https://traverseplatforms.com/demo/` (35 characters).

Alternative: **Sign up** > `https://traverseplatforms.com/start/` (36), which goes straight to picking a trade and plan.
Book now matches the site's main button.

### Username (the @name and facebook.com/name address)

Settings > Page setup (or Edit details) > Username. In order of preference:

1. `@traverseplatforms` (18 characters)
2. `@traverseapp` (11)
3. `@gotraverse` (10)

Facebook's rules: at least 5 characters, letters, numbers and full stops only. Whether each one is free is for you to check:
Facebook shows a green tick or "not available" as you type.

### About (longer description)

The current Page layout often shows only the Bio. If Edit details shows a longer "About" or "Description" box, paste:

> Traverse is the all-in-one sales platform for field-sales companies in roofing, lawn and landscape, solar, pest control and home security. Every property in your area comes with the facts that matter to your trade, and the best properties rise to the top. Assign streets to your reps, quote from the property's own size at your own prices, take signatures and deposits at the door, and run the pipeline, jobs and crews from the office, all in one app on phone and computer. Every plan comes with every feature. Built in Colorado. Book a demo at traverseplatforms.com.

567 characters, 96 words. If the box stops you partway (some older Description boxes stopped at 255), say so and I'll write a
short version for you to approve. Don't let Facebook cut it off mid-sentence.

### Messaging: instant reply

Meta Business Suite > Inbox > Automations > **Instant reply** > On. Message:

> Thanks for reaching out to Traverse! I'm Matthew, the owner. I'll get back to you shortly. Want to see the app with your own area? Book a 15-minute demo: traverseplatforms.com/demo

180 characters. (Two points about this text under "Check before you paste".)

Leave the away message and the FAQ off for now. Turn them on only once you know which questions come in.

### Settings to turn on and off

| Setting | Where | Set to |
|---|---|---|
| Messaging | Settings > Messaging (or the Message button) | **On**, with the instant reply above |
| Reviews / recommendations | Settings > Privacy > Reviews (or Page setup > Reviews) | **On** |
| Who can post on the Page | Settings > Privacy > Page and tagging (or "Others can post") | **Only the Page posts.** Visitors can still comment and send messages. |
| Profanity filter | Settings > Privacy > Page and tagging (or Moderation) | **Medium** |
| Country and age limits | Settings > Privacy > Country / Age | **None** (no limits) |
| Two-factor authentication | Your own account: Settings > Accounts Center > Password and security | On, if it isn't already. Facebook asks Page admins for it. |

## Check before you paste (nothing below was changed)

1. **Exclamation mark in the instant reply** ("Thanks for reaching out to Traverse!"). The Traverse brand rules say no
   exclamation marks (`traverse/brand/project/README.md`, Voice). Swap it for a full stop if you want it to match.
2. **"15-minute demo"** in the instant reply and the pinned post. The website's demo page says **30 minutes** ("Thirty minutes
   with us", "30 minutes, by phone or video call") and the homepage says "30 minutes, on your own trade". Either change the
   two Facebook texts to 30 or change the site.
3. **"Every property in your area"** (About and pinned post). The site says "Across Colorado ... Tell us where you work when you
   book a demo" and the demo page says "We'll show you yours, and tell you when we can add any that aren't in yet." Someone
   outside Colorado could read "your area" as a promise. Fine for Colorado; your call.
4. **Email.** The Page shows `M.Sharpe@TraversePlatforms.com`; the website lists `hello@traverseplatforms.com`. Both work as
   wording; pick one if you want them the same.
5. **Hours.** "Every day 8 AM to 8 PM" is the demo booking window; the booking calendar itself skips school runs (Tue-Fri
   before 9:30) and Tuesday 4:30-6 pm. Fine as Page hours; mentioned only so it isn't a surprise.
