# Google "send email" verification: what to record and submit

For Matthew (30 Oct 2026). Branding is already verified. This is the second step: Google reviews the Gmail permission
(`gmail.send`) because the app sends email on a company's behalf. Until it passes, people see Google's "unverified app" screen and
the app is capped at 100 users.

## Where
Google Cloud Console › Google Auth Platform › **Verification center** › start verification for the data access (the `gmail.send` scope).

## What Google asks for
1. **Why the app needs it** (paste):
   > Traverse is a sales and CRM app for field-service companies. A company's admin connects their own Gmail so that quotes, signed
   > agreements, receipts and follow-up emails go to that company's customers from the company's own address. Traverse only sends
   > email the company asks it to send; it never reads the mailbox. The permission is stored encrypted and removed when the admin
   > disconnects.
2. **A short screen recording** (unlisted YouTube link), 1 to 3 minutes, showing:
   1. Signing in to a Traverse app as the company's admin (use TraverseRoofing; don't show any sign-in code on screen).
   2. Office › Setup › **Email**.
   3. Pressing **Sign in with Google**. On Google's screen, show the app name "Traverse" and the permission being asked for,
      then choose an account and allow.
   4. Back in the app: "Email connected as …".
   5. Sending something that uses it: Setup › Email › **Send a test**, then show the email arriving in the inbox it was sent to.
   6. Disconnecting: Setup › Email › Disconnect.
   Say or caption each step in plain words. Make sure the browser's address bar shows traverseplatforms.com.
3. **Privacy policy link:** https://traverseplatforms.com/privacy/ (it already has the "Connecting a mailbox" section and Google's
   Limited Use statement).
4. **Homepage link:** https://traverseplatforms.com/

## After submitting
Google usually replies by email within a few days to a few weeks and may ask follow-up questions. Keep the site open and the app
reachable during review. Nothing else in the app changes while it's pending.
