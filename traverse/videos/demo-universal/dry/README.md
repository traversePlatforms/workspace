## The one local demo copy

- Made by `traverse/videos/tools/demo-universal-copy.sh` from today's built code (`traverse/editions/dist`): the Roofing edition's page and API with one town, Highlands Ranch, and the Lawn & Landscape and Solar editions' per-door layers for the same town added to its data folder (lawn outlines, sun and solar, the funding and water sheets). company.json is the live Roofing one (read once, read-only) with the Solar layer and the lawn, trees and water card parts switched on and a mixed service list; on top, the made-up company: Example Co, (303) 555-0100, office@example.com. One company and one look on every screen.
- `bash demo-universal-copy.sh reset` remakes it, starts PHP on 127.0.0.1:8811, adds the made-up people (admin Jordan Blake, reps Alex Rivera and Sam Ortiz, technician Riley Chen; local test codes only), the made-up leads, a few made-up knocks by the two reps, and picks the demo house (the one whose card shows the most of the script's facts).
- Dry run: `node record-demo-universal.mjs --dry` after a reset (it signs, books and assigns on the copy). Recording: `node voice-demo-universal.mjs`, a reset, `node record-demo-universal.mjs --record`, then `node build-demo-universal.mjs`.

## What the copy does differently from a customer's install, and why

- **Walking order.** One install has one "best first" order. The video uses the roofing order, except the YouTube-only line "Properties that already have solar move down the list", recorded with the order set to solar for that one scene (company.json "best").
- **Deposit.** Taken at the door by card and recorded ("I took the deposit"); paying on the homeowner's own phone needs the company's Square or Stripe, which a demo copy does not have.
- **Send it back to the office.** The editions have no separate "send to office" button (Sixfold 365's Quick quote has one). Shown: a rep's quote, saved on his phone on his own lead, opened by the office in the Pipeline, where the office takes the deposit and sends the form ("They said yes: deposit and form"). The Agreements and deposits switch in Accounts decides whether reps or only the office do this.
- **Knocks.** "Who knocked what" shows made-up knocks marked on the copy by the made-up reps before recording, the same way the leads are made up.
- **Facebook leads.** A lead arriving from Facebook cannot be shown (the copy is not connected to a Page); the real Facebook leads screen in Setup is shown.
- **Aftercare.** The copy has no mailbox, so nothing is sent; Setup, Customer emails shows the aftercare email switched on.
- **On-screen words still in the app's own text.** The best-first list says "Best for roofing first" (the walking order's own label). Prices on the quote, deposit and end-of-day screens are the made-up company's starter prices, not Traverse's.
