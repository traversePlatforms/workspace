## Title

Traverse: the sales platform for the field and the office (Roofing, Lawn & Landscape, Solar demo)

## Description

Traverse puts the facts that matter to your trade on every house in your area, ranks the doors worth knocking first, and runs everything after the knock: the quote, the signature, the deposit, the job and the follow-up. One app, on the phone in the field and the computer in the office.

This demo walks through three editions:

- Roofing: every storm day with its hail size and path, the houses it reached, and each roof's size, pitch, material and age. Plan a street and it is on your rep's phone before they leave the lot.
- Lawn & Landscape: the lawn's size and outline before you pull up, the trees by the house, the town's watering days, a quote from the lawn's own size, and the homeowner signing on their phone.
- Solar: the system that fits each roof and a year's output for that spot, with houses that already have solar moved down the list.

Back at the office: one pipeline for every lead, Facebook lead ads straight into it, territories on the map, and a picture of the day by email every night.

Shown on demo copies with made-up companies. Homeowner names and addresses are hidden.
