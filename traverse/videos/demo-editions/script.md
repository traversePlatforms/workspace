# Traverse demo video: script (draft 2, recorded 2 Nov 2026)

For YouTube and for Brandon to send to companies. 16:9, about 3:40. Narrated by a Gemini voice once this script is approved.
Editions shown: Roofing, Lawn & Landscape, Solar.

**On screen:** real recordings of the app on the demo copies (made-up companies, homeowner names hidden, 555 phone numbers), on a
computer screen with the phone version inset where the field side matters. Designed title cards between sections. No generated footage.

**Rules kept:** no data sources named (it's public, like the website); no numbers we can't back up; no pricing (it changes); no client named.

---

## 0:00 to 0:15. Opening

**Voice:** Every day your reps knock on doors. Some of those houses need you right now. Most don't. Traverse tells you which is which before anyone leaves the truck.

**Screen:** the map of a town, doors coloured best to worst; zoom to one house card.
**Card:** TRAVERSE · The sales platform for the field and the office.

## 0:15 to 0:35. What it is

**Voice:** Traverse puts the facts that matter to your trade on every house in your area, ranks the doors worth knocking first, and runs everything after the knock: the quote, the signature, the deposit, the job and the follow-up. One app, on the phone in the field and the computer in the office. Here it is for three trades.

**Screen:** fast cuts: house card, best-doors list, a signed quote on a phone, the office pipeline.
**Card:** Roofing · Lawn & Landscape · Solar

## 0:35 to 1:35. Roofing

**Voice:** For roofers, it starts with the storm. Open Storms and every storm day is there, with the hail size and the path drawn over your map. Tap a day and you get the houses it reached, biggest hail first.

**Screen:** Storms → Every storm day → one day: the path over the map, the list of houses.

**Voice:** Every house shows the hail it took, storm by storm, the damaging wind nearby, the roof's size and pitch, what it's made of and how old it is, and whether it's been re-roofed since. New owners are flagged too, with the month they moved in.

**Screen:** a house card scrolling: hail by date, wind, roof size and pitch, material and age, last re-roof, moved in.

**Voice:** The best doors rise to the top. A manager ticks a street, assigns it to a rep, and it's on their phone before they leave the lot. Live radar and storm alerts let you know the moment severe weather hits your towns.

**Screen:** Plan → tick properties → assign to a rep; cut to the rep's phone showing the street; the live radar map.

## 1:35 to 2:30. Lawn & Landscape

**Voice:** For lawn and landscape companies, the card shows the lawn before you pull up. Its size in square feet, the property line on the photo with the lawn shaded in, how much of it sits near the house, and the trees close to the house.

**Screen:** a house card: lawn size, the aerial with the yellow lot line and green lawn, trees.

**Voice:** It even knows the town's watering days. Quote from the lawn's own size at your own prices. The homeowner signs on their phone, and you take the deposit right there.

**Screen:** Quote builder filling from the lawn size → the signing page on a phone → the deposit recorded and the job booked.

**Voice:** Mark the job done. After the job, the aftercare tips and your review link go out on their own.

**Screen:** the job marked Done; Setup, Customer emails (aftercare).

## 2:30 to 3:10. Solar

**Voice:** For solar teams, every roof shows its sun-facing area, the shade from tall trees, the system that fits it, and a year's output for that exact spot. Houses that already have solar are moved down, so nobody wastes a knock.

**Screen:** a house card: system that fits, panels, output a year, shade; the list with "already has solar" lower.

**Voice:** The proposal starts from the roof, and the homeowner signs it on the spot.

**Screen:** the proposal on a phone, signed.

## 3:10 to 3:30. The office

**Voice:** Back at the office, every lead is in one pipeline. Connect your Facebook lead ads and new leads land in the pipeline. Draw territories, see who knocked what, and get a picture of the day by email every night.

**Screen:** pipeline on a computer; the Facebook leads setup screen; territories drawn on the map; the end-of-day email.

## 3:30 to 3:40. Close

**Voice:** Traverse. The all-in-one sales platform for the field and office. See it with your own area at traverseplatforms.com.

## Changes for the recording (2 Nov, after the dry run; Matthew said "start the recording")

Each keeps the line to what the demo copies really show.

1. **Deposit.** "and pays the deposit right there" became "you take the deposit right there": paying on the homeowner's phone needs the company's own Square or Stripe, which a demo copy doesn't have. Shown: the deposit recorded at the door and the job booked.
2. **After the job.** The pop-up "Aftercare email has gone out" is Sixfold 365's only, and the editions' follow-up is for quotes that go quiet, not a note after the job. A plan is offered on the quote page, not emailed after the job. New line: "After the job, the aftercare tips and your review link go out on their own", over Setup, Customer emails.
3. **Facebook.** A lead arriving can't be shown without a connected Page. New line: "Connect your Facebook lead ads and new leads land in the pipeline", over the Facebook leads screen.
4. **Solar proposal.** "fills in from the roof" became "starts from the roof": the roof facts fill in, but the rep sets the system size.
5. **Lawn shading.** Kept: the Lawn house now has its lawn outline, and the phone opens the photo with the lawn shaded in.

## Lines cut, and why

- "Reps waste half their day on the wrong doors." We have no source for the figure.
- Pricing and the founding offer. They change, and the video will be around for a while.
- Where the data comes from (storm radar, county records, laser scans). Same rule as the website.
