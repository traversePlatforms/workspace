# Brand

Everything about Surge that isn't a draft. Drop things in the right folder and tell me it's there — I'll read what's relevant to the job rather than everything, every time.

| Folder | What goes in | Can I use it? |
|---|---|---|
| [logos/](logos/) | Logo files, wordmarks, marks, favicons | Yes — embedded into infographics |
| [photos/](photos/) | Team, field, event, office photos | Yes — I can see images and describe or place them |
| [videos/](videos/) | Raw clips, screen recordings | **No — I cannot watch video.** Storage only |
| [notes/](notes/) | Your typed-up notes, one file per topic | Yes — this is the main one |
| [guidelines/](guidelines/) | Colours, fonts, rules, do-and-don't | Yes — overrides my design choices |

**Related folders elsewhere:** [training-source/](../training-source/) holds training-step material (the Law of Averages doc lives there). Keep training steps there rather than in `notes/` — they have their own template and CLAUDE.md points at them.

## Formats

**Word documents are fine anywhere.** Save as `.docx` and I'll extract with [tools/Extract-Docx.ps1](../../shared/tools/Extract-Docx.ps1). Text, paragraphs and table cells come through; bold, heading styles and bullet dots don't, so put headings on their own line as plain words. Markdown, plain text and PDFs also work.

**Don't tidy anything up before sending it.** Fragments, shorthand and half-finished thoughts are more useful to me than a cleaned-up summary, and how you actually phrase things is data I need for the voice work.

## What I most need, roughly in order

1. **The three referenced systems** — 5 Steps, Successful Business Practices, Gifts. Named in the Law of Averages doc as the system reps work, never defined. When a template had to fill that gap it invented a four-step process that contradicted your own five. → `training-source/`
2. **Real numbers**, with scope and date. Money raised, products sold, customers acquired, over what period, for which kind of client. Results are your stated differentiator and I currently have zero figures, so every asset says "benchmarks, not results." → `notes/numbers.md`
3. **Samples of your actual writing** — Slack messages, texts to clients, emails. Unedited, not composed for the exercise. This settles which voice register is really yours. → `notes/voice-samples.md`
4. **Brand colours**, if official ones exist. The blue on the boards is one I picked. → `guidelines/`
5. **The competitor list**, or the category boundary. "Never name competitors" is only enforceable if I know who they are. → `notes/`
