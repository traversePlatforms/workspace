## The finding: the Instagram account and RESTRICTED.md disagree

Not a small overlap. Live, public Surge posts contain several things `brand/notes/RESTRICTED.md` says are restricted:

**Pay.** Three separate recruitment graphics state the rate outright — `$800/week | Base or performance pay structure` and `$800 weekly base + comission bonus` (their typo). RESTRICTED.md Tier 2 opens the compensation entry with *"$800/week base"* as the first item, and the whole subject is restricted.

**Footprint.** One post pairs the Denver and Houston skylines under *"Could your model work in a new city tomorrow?"* Tier 2 restricts structure and scale explicitly, ending *"no footprint, scale, growth, or nationwide framing."*

**Sales technique.** A run of quote cards teaches operator-side method to a public audience — *"Can You Sit in Silence after the close? Hesitation kills more sales than price ever will,"* *"Objections Aren't a Personal Attack,"* *"99% of what we do is attitude and people are buying into you,"* *"If your confidence isn't there, then their confidence isn't there."* Tier 1 and Tier 2 restrict the influence-technique material; these are softer than the scripted rebuttals, but they are the same category of content.

**A whiteboard already ruled unpublishable.** One post shows a trainer beside a board reading `Sense of urgency: … motion→emo… verbal: "2 se…" physical: d…`. That is the exact board that forced the `IMG_0481` crop, described in `photos-notes.md` as *"operator-side training language, and publishing a photo of it is the same act as publishing the text."* It is already published.

**A child.** One group photo has a young child front and centre. Children are a hard NO in this register with no qualifier.

**A client shirt.** One group photo has a rep in a client-branded tee, the thing 55 files in `client-branded-DO-NOT-PUBLISH/` are quarantined for.

### What I have and haven't done about it

I filed those seven in `FLAGGED-restricted/` and left the rest alone. **I have not changed any restriction, and I have not treated "already published" as clearance.**

This needs a decision from Matthew, and it is genuinely his:

- **If RESTRICTED.md is right**, then the Instagram account is publishing restricted material now, and that is a live problem rather than a filing one — the pay figure and the Houston post especially.
- **If the Instagram account is right**, then RESTRICTED.md is over-restricting in a way that has already shaped the website, the drafts and every clearance call in this folder. The pay entry in particular reads differently once the company has been advertising the number itself.

**Both cannot govern.** Whichever way it goes, the losing document should be updated rather than quietly ignored, because the whole value of RESTRICTED.md is that it is the one authoritative list.

My read, offered as a read and not a decision: **the pay figure is the one to resolve first.** It is a hard fact, it is in a live recruitment ad, and the restriction on it is specific and unambiguous. The technique cards are a much softer call and could reasonably stay as they are.
