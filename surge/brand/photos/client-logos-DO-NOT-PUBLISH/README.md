## The rule that did not change

**Clients are never named in text.** No sentence, heading, caption or body copy calls an
organisation a client — sector only. The logo may appear; the prose may not name them.
`alt` text carrying the organisation's bare name on its own logo is currently treated as
part of the mark rather than as copy, and is flagged for Matthew's ruling in the
2026-09-11 revision.
