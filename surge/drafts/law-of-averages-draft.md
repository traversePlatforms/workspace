## The source, verbatim

> Law of averages- Proven principles for maximizing success
> Grace: (Goals, Ratios, Attitude, Consistency, Exposure)
>
> **Goals** — 100 People / 90 people say "No" / 10 people say "yes"
> **Ratios** — 1:15 = Training, 1:10 = Standard, 1:05 = Great
> **Attitude** — 99% attitude, 1% ability, 0% luck, 100% you. No bad weather, location, or event. Have fun!
> **Consistency** — Pitch stays the same, delivery changes. Work the system: 5 Steps, Successful Busines Practices, Gifts
> **Exposure** — You miss 100% of the shots you don't take. It's a numbers game, not a sale game. No cherry-picking or pre-judging. Talk to everyone!

## Fact-check log (2026-08-05)

### Verified internally consistent
- **100 people at 90/10 is exactly the 1:10 called Standard.** The Goals figure and the Ratios scale are one claim from two angles. Worth using when teaching it; used as the basis for the square, which drops to the 1-in-10 unit so the asset only makes one claim at one scale.

### Changed for public use, with reasons
| Item | Internal | Public | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Proven principles" | kept | dropped | "Proven" is a claim with nothing behind it |
| Attitude percentages | kept as written | replaced | 99%/0% is unfalsifiable; publicly it reads as putting every outcome on the rep, which is the exact criticism the company already attracts |
| "No bad weather, location, or event" | kept | replaced | Same reason. Converted to what a rep actually controls: the number of conversations, not the result of any one |
| Ratios | stated plainly | labelled "training benchmarks, not audited results" | Avoids implying a Surge performance claim, per company spec §2 |
| "most people who leave field sales" | n/a | "the people I've watched leave" | My line, not yours. The first is a statistic with no source; the second is an observation, which is what it is |

### Invented and then removed
- **"About two pages"** describing the manual's rejection section, from the earlier training-content draft. Never in any source. Cut.

### Unresolved — needs you
- **5 Steps, Successful Business Practices, Gifts.** Named in the source as the system reps work, contents unknown. Rendered as named-but-unexpanded on the internal board; absent from public assets. These are likely the strongest material available.
- **"Successful Busines Practices"** is spelled with one "s" in the source. Rendered with two. Flagging in case the source spelling is deliberate.
- **"1:05"** reproduced as written on the internal board. Public assets don't use the notation.

### Constraints checked
- No client named, no sector identified beyond what's already public in the company spec
- No metrics presented as Surge results
- No proprietary-method claim — the public board opens by saying the idea belongs to everyone in field sales
- No income, hiring, pay or advancement claims, so personal spec §9 doesn't bite

## Lines cut

- **"Have fun!"** — carried on the internal board, dropped publicly. Fine in a room, reads as instruction-to-be-happy in a feed.
- **The full 100-dot grid.** Built, then replaced with ten marks. It was a cleverer picture of the same fact and harder to read at a glance; at feed thumbnail size it was unreadable.
- **The 1:15 / 1:10 / 1:05 ratio ladder as a dot diagram.** Same problem — three rows of dots to say one thing. Kept as plain text on the public board.
- **The taped "board notes" panel.** Removed from the public board on 2026-08-05 and folded into this file. It was me showing my working, which belongs in the draft, not on a published graphic.
