Donation Guidelines
To ensure all collected toys can be safely distributed, please keep the following guidelines in mind:


* Condition: All donated toys must be new and unwrapped.
* Accepted Items: Board games, plush toys, action figures, art supplies, puzzles, sports equipment, etc
* Items to Avoid: Used toys, realistic toy weapons, or items containing food/perishables.
