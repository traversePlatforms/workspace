## Flags (checked against RESTRICTED.md and brand-voice-company.md)

1. **VAA and the beneficiaries are named on Matthew's word (6 Nov).** RESTRICTED.md carries a campaign-only revision. If VAA's OK is in an email, keep a copy with the campaign.
2. **The poster and the event doc disagree, twice.** Poster: "new or slightly used", 12-6 pm. Event doc: "new and unwrapped", "items to avoid: used toys", 11:30 am-6 pm. The captions follow the doc on items (and Matthew's call); the hours wait for his answer. Post 1's image is the poster itself, so "slightly used" shows in it. Worth telling the team so the boxes don't turn people away for following the poster.
3. **The standing test.** In the photos the toy boxes sit on the same table as VAA's donor sign-up material. A reader who already suspects the worst could read the drive as a draw for sign-ups. The posts say nothing about signing up and the crops leave the sign-up material out; keeping the box visibly separate at events is the rest of it. Matthew's call.
4. **"Boys & Girls Club".** The poster says "Boys & Girls Club of America"; the national body is Boys & Girls Clubs of America, and the toys likely go to a local club. The posts say "the Boys & Girls Club".
5. **No tax-deduction language.** Surge is a for-profit; whether VAA issues receipts for in-kind gifts is unknown.
6. **Counts need a source.** Every number is a Surge metric under the voice spec §2: a scope (this drive), a date and a record someone can pull.
7. **Platform deviations, flagged:** captions run 40-110 words (the Facebook precedent, not LinkedIn's 150-200); 4 hashtags; the event doc link goes in the first comment, not the caption.
