Until Maya makes it view-only, Matthew may want to hold post 1 or ask for the QR to be covered. Not changed in the image: his call.
3. **Photo d's location is unconfirmed** (post 4). The caption no longer depends on it.
4. **The event doc still says "new and unwrapped" and lists used toys under "avoid".** The posts now follow the poster ("new or slightly used", Matthew 10 Nov). Worth telling the team so the boxes take what the posts invite, and so the doc gets updated with the view-only change.
5. **Post 13 says the boxes are out on 23 Dec.** The only day-specific line in the set; confirm or cut (see post 13).
6. **The standing test.** In the photos the toy boxes sit on the same table as VAA's donor sign-up material. A reader who already suspects the worst could read the drive as a draw for sign-ups. The posts say nothing about signing up and the crops leave the sign-up material out; keeping the box visibly separate at events is the rest of it. Matthew's call.
7. **"Boys & Girls Club".** The poster says "Boys & Girls Club of America"; the national body is Boys & Girls Clubs of America, and the toys likely go to a local club. The posts say "the Boys & Girls Club".
8. **No tax-deduction language.** Surge is a for-profit; whether VAA issues receipts for in-kind gifts is unknown.
9. **No counts** (decision 6). If counts come back, each is a Surge metric under the voice spec §2: a scope (this drive), a date and a record someone can pull.
10. **Platform deviations, flagged:** captions run 40-100 words (the Facebook precedent, not LinkedIn's 150-200); 4 hashtags; no first comments.
