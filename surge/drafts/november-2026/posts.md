3. The source line carries the 31 Oct re-check date.

**Facebook caption:**
> Forgetting is the default, and it's been measured since 1880. In 2015, Murre and Dros replicated Ebbinghaus's forgetting curve in PLOS ONE. It held.
>
> The more useful study is Roediger and Karpicke, 2006. Students either re-read a passage or practised recalling it, the same number of times. Five minutes later, the re-readers were ahead. Two days and a week later, recall was well ahead, even though re-reading had made students more confident.
>
> Our read: "we covered that in training" describes the calendar, not what someone can do. Our mornings sit on the recall side: the pitch written out on a whiteboard, then practised out loud in pairs.
>
> What does your team do the week after training?
>
> Sources in the comments.
>
> #SalesTraining #Onboarding #FieldSales
