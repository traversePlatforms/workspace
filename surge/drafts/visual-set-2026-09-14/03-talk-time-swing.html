High performers are reported to hold roughly the same ratio whether they win or lose. No figure is published for them, so no third row is drawn here.

Our read

The gap between a won call and a lost one is five points. The old rule asked reps to cut seventeen. Meanwhile the same reps move ten points between their own good days and bad ones — and a rep whose talk time climbs on a call going badly is not talking more on purpose. They have lost the thread and are filling the silence. A target number hides that. The swing is what shows it.
