The 5 Steps to
Build Value

What a rep is taught to do at a door, in order, before anything else.

1

Introduction

SEE — Smile · Eye contact · Enthusiasm

First impression, lasting impression. A smile is contagious, eye contact builds trust and shows confidence, enthusiasm builds curiosity. Three things a new rep controls on day one.

2

Short Story

WWW & KISS — Who we are · What we're doing · Why

Keep it short and simple. Three answers, delivered plainly, before anything is asked of the person. That is the entire step.

3

Presentation

Put it in their hands · Package and present

People engage differently with something they are holding, and holding it shows ownership. Present it so they can see what they'd support, not just hear about it.

4

The Close

Qualify · Then ask, clearly

Questions first, to understand what actually fits the person in front of you. Then ask with confidence — it's a specific thing a rep does, not something that happens on its own.

5

Rehash

Thank them · Then ask once more

Nothing stops at the first yes. Thank the person, say what their support pays for, then make one further offer. It is a second ask, and it is taught as a named step rather than left to instinct.

None of this is a system nobody else has — plenty of field teams run the same five steps. What a company can be judged on is how well it teaches them, and how fast a new rep can do it unaided.
