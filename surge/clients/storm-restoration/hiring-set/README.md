# Storm restoration hiring — five pieces

Built 2026-08-18. **Register: company** — recruiting copy is read by a prospective
client and a prospective rep at the same time (company spec §4). **Status: all five
are drafts. Nothing published, nothing scheduled, nothing staged to a platform.**
The Blotato visual API renders images; it does not post. No posting endpoint was
called and no account state was changed.

The client is never named anywhere in the set. "Storm restoration" / "weather
restoration" is the sector word and it is as specific as the copy gets.

## Two things to decide before any of this goes out

1. **Piece 3 — do reps train on the Haag protocol?** The post describes it as the
   industry standard and stops there, because nobody has confirmed Surge teaches it.
   A yes makes it the strongest recruiting line in the set. A no changes nothing in
   the copy, and the post still stands.
2. **Piece 2 — impact or accuracy on the asset.** The structured carousel is exactly
   true and a bit plain. The billboard render is dense and bold and has a garbled
   hail-size strip. Both are in `assets/`.

## What the render pass found

Four AI-drawing templates invented material that is not in the sources — a bylined
reporter twice over, a publication date, an edition number, a pull quote nobody said,
and a hail-belt map. The full table is in piece 2's draft. **Structured templates —
one text field per card — invented nothing across every render.** Anything carrying a
citation belongs on one of those.

## What is deliberately absent from all five

- No pay, no earnings figures, no ranges, no "uncapped", no atmospheric income
  language. One verified statistic — the average wind and hail claim value — was cut
  on this ground alone.
- No hours, no schedule, no day-in-the-life.
- No promotion or advancement framing.
- No client name and no client statistics — not their approval rate, not their
  out-of-pocket line, not the rep-sourced "30% more back."
- Nothing about deductibles, coverage, or what an insurer will pay. Colorado bars
  advertising deductible handling and bars acting as a public adjuster without a
  licence (C.R.S. 6-22-105).
- No scale, footprint or growth framing. Englewood appears as where the job is, never
  as where Surge sells.
- No uniqueness or proprietary-method claim. Pieces 3 and 5 credit an outside standard
  and an outside study and claim nothing but the execution.
- The word "employee" appears nowhere. It is "the role", "reps", "field reps".
