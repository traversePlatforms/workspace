# Team bios — as submitted by the person

Some team members were asked (by Matthew) to write their own bio. Those come back in the
individual's own words — often first-person, with warmth / positivity that is not the site's
third-person brand voice. This file preserves the as-submitted text so nothing is lost when a
bio is adapted for the site.

Matthew's note, 2026-09-07: *"they are written as the individuals words and not in the brand
voice"* — expect more of these as other people send theirs in.
